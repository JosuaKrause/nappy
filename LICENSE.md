# Licensing: what MIT covers here, and what CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 covers

The [MIT license](LICENSE) covers this repository's **code**: everything under `src/`, `tests/`,
`tools/`, `.claude/`, and the project configuration files (`project.godot`, export presets, and the
like). Use it, fork it, learn from it, ship things built with it.

**Everything that makes this repository a game rather than a codebase is licensed under Creative
Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-NoDerivatives 4.0 International**, the full text of which is in
[LICENSE-ASSETS](LICENSE-ASSETS). That is:

- everything under `assets/` — the art, sprites, tiles and icons;
- everything under `docs/` — the design documents, the narrative and its characters, the playtest
  records;
- the game's title and its story.

**In plain language, alongside the legal text, which is the part that governs:** you may read, copy
and re-share this material as it is, in whole or in part, as long as you credit the project and link
back to the license. You may not sell it, and you may not publish a modified or derivative version
of it. This **permits verbatim sharing with credit**, which a bare "all rights reserved" statement
does not, and it still **forbids selling it and forbids an altered version**.

If you want to do something this license does not cover — sell the assets, publish a derivative, or
anything else outside verbatim credited sharing — ask; open an issue.
