# minty-quail — The last third of the parked probes are typed · 2026-11-12

**The ask.** Test code is typed as the code it tests is ("we type when we can", inbox #1280 in
olive-ferret), and a test reads its subject only through methods (inbox #1182 in gray-dolphin):
`typed-everywhere.md`, one pass per module. This pass is the last third of the parked probes under
`tests/probes/`, the measurements run by path and never by the runner or CI, from `m226_arrival_timing` to
`walk_rows`. Its search is none: it types and deletes lines and converts no read.

## What was built

- **Twenty-two probes parse without their file-level opt-out line**, which each loses (the pull request's
  table gives each line): `m226_arrival_timing`, `m226_corner_runtime`, `m64_density`, `m64_measure`,
  `m96_crying_at_the_top`, `m96_dog_sprinkle`, `m98_return_phase`, `m99_caps`, `merry_elk_poster_density`,

  `velvet_stork_guard_path_cost`, `velvet_stork_other_pursuers` and `walk_rows`. No helper a probe loads changed,
  nothing under `src/`, and no check was deleted or moved.
- **Typing**: loop variables take the type their writer puts in; a mean fed ints by one caller and floats by
  another takes `float` elements, the engine converting on the way in as the old `float(v)` did; arrays inside a
  Dictionary are appended through `as Array`; scenes are loaded into a `PackedScene` before `instantiate()`; a probe
  that holds another is typed through a preload of its file. Three loops guarded by `is_instance_valid()` (in
  `minty_quail_crowd_yields` and `minty_quail_pursuit_timing`) keep their element as `Variant` and cast after the
  guard, since a typed loop variable throws on a freed element; a comment above each says so.

  (`dog_picture()`, the `*_BY_VIEW` and `WHEELS_BY_LOOK` constants) and for `_native_size()`, which #861 made the
  public `EventPictures.native_size()` that the game's looks call; it now asks `EventPictures`, typed.
  `m226_corner_runtime` draws through it and is fixed by the same change.
- **Read by name, which the tool cannot see**: `m226_corner_runtime` reads `Main`'s private `_player`, `_city` and
  `_edge` through `get()`, and `scenery_shader_warmup_runtime` reads `_player` and `_hud` and writes
  `_escape_scene_requested` the same way, no method of `Main` answering them. Methods called by name:

  and `Crowd._step_the_agents` and `CrowdAgent._process`. No probe writes a script out from a string.

## Parity

Each probe ran by path at `origin/main` (da510b555) and at the head, one at a time, with a twenty-minute limit,
and the outputs were compared with timing lines set aside. Eighteen print the same lines, four of them but for
wall-clock figures (`minty_quail_crowd_yields`, `velvet_stork_guard_path_cost`, `walk_rows`,
`scenery_shader_warmup_runtime`); `scenery_animation_runtime`'s drawing half needs a window and ran at neither.
The two m226 probes run where main errors: `m226_arrival_timing` prints no script error and fills its "drawn
entry" column, its other columns and checks the same as main's; `m226_corner_runtime` sees the charging dog under
the screen's cover and coming out of it, where main saw neither. `minty_quail_day13_real_marks` stopped at the
limit at both revisions, the census standing for its parse, and the lines both printed are identical. After the
merge of main, which typed `tests/test_day13_guard.gd`, the three probes that load it match again.

**Found, not changed** (the brief types a probe that fails on main and fixes nothing else): `m96_dog_sprinkle`
stops on main reading `EventDirector._owed` (a key read on a null at line 55), and `minty_quail_crowd_cadence` and
`minty_quail_crowd_yields` exit 1 on main for leaving three `EventBus` connections behind (`car_near_miss`,
`crowd_bumped`, `hard_fail_triggered`).
