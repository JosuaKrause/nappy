## M205 — Handing a note over now costs his inner radius, not a brush at REACH · 2026-09-27

*(PLAYTEST-144, statement 22: "Handing over a task's note costs excitement. A task is an extra
challenge about managing excitement and is deliberately dangerous; the ordinary route is the
almost free path." A fork proposed making her stand inside his field for a while first, and the
player rejected it outright: "the player should stand for 2.5s? no way. the moment the player
touches the inner circle it counts as delivered.")*

**The bug.** Day 6's note handover (`ResistanceSteps.by_index(2)`, riding a live `homeless_yeller`)
completed the instant `ContactPoint.REACH` (36px) was reached — a fixed plumbing constant every
task's contact shares, which happens to sit inside his 45px `inner_radius` (his own full-strength
field). So the handover only ever charged the last few pixels of the approach: ~4.7 points awake
(`tests/probes/m205_note_handover.gd`, averaged over eight points in his own pulse), against ~11.1
for an ordinary full pass doing nothing in particular (`M174Pass.pass_net_averaged()` at 0px, the
same simulation `docs/COSTS.md`'s "the pass — awake" row is generated from).

**What is built.** `ResistanceSteps.Step.completes_at_inner_radius` (true only for this one step)
makes `ContactPoint._physics_process()` complete the instant she is within the rider's own
`inner_radius` instead of `REACH` — still instant, not a dwell. A first version of this fix tied
the handover to a continuous 2.5s stand inside `inner_radius`
(`Tuning.NOTE_HANDOVER_DWELL_SECONDS`); the player rejected it, quoted above, before it reached
`main`, so the dwell and its constant never merged — the field and the constant it would have used
are gone from the tree entirely rather than left dead.

**Measured, and the residual gap is real, not hidden.** Because `REACH` (36px) already sits
*inside* `inner_radius` (45px), stopping at `inner_radius` actually ends the approach a few pixels
*earlier*, not later: `tests/probes/m205_note_handover.gd` measures ~4.1 points awake at
`inner_radius` against ~4.7 at `REACH` — both still well under the ~11.1 an ordinary pass costs.
What this decision buys is tying the handover to a fact about the row itself (his own
full-strength field) rather than a generic constant that happened to sit inside it; it does not
close the gap to an ordinary pass, and nobody has asked for a bigger number since — the player's
own answer settled the mechanism, not the size. `tests/test_resistance.gd`,
`_test_the_notes_handover_completes_at_his_inner_radius_not_reach`, pins the instant completion at
`inner_radius` and the fact that an ordinary step's plain `REACH` check does not fire at the same
distance.

**Applies to every task whose handover rides a man shouting.** Only day 6's note does today
(`ResistanceSteps._build()`); the field lives on `Step` rather than on index 2 specifically, so a
later task built the same way (`_perform()`, riding `homeless_yeller`) inherits the same fix by
setting the same field rather than needing a second one.
