# minty-quail — One give-way for a crowd car and an event car; the siting's region built once per plan (shared-components G) · 2026-10-31


minty-quail's `shared-components.md`, its fifth PR (PR #888) under the approved plan, after B2
(minty-quail-30). Two items: the carry-over from events-composed (minty-quail-26), an event car's
driving copying crowd traffic's give-way (`EventCarDriving.room_to_its_line()`), which the plan settled
as one shared give-way (F8) rather than the event car becoming a `LaneMover`; and the cost the B2 review
found in `SpawnSiting.ask_of_ground()`, which rebuilt the day's region on every ask.

**Built.**
- `GiveWay` (`src/world/give_way.gd`), stateless: the junction box ahead, whether its light is red, the
  setback line a car stops at, whether a car owes her the way (the main-road exemption and the
  half-street lateral test), the zebra edge ahead and whether a zebra lies past the box.
- `CarStrategy` and `EventCarDriving.room_to_its_line()` both ask it, and each keeps what is its own: the
  crowd car its braking curve, commit rule and mid-turn exemption; the event car its sight circle, nose
  test and step refusal. `room_to_its_line()` stays, as the event car's method, without the copy.
- `LaneMover._junction_index()` is gone rather than left as a forwarder (minty-dolphin #852):
  `CarStrategy` asks `GiveWay.box_index()`; `junction_ahead()` and `junction_occupied()` key their box
  with `GiveWay.junction_at()`, and `distance_to_junction()` and the event car's distance to the box use
  `GiveWay.distance_to_box()`; `Crowd.give_way_at_junctions()` asks it for the red light and the stop
  line. The crowd's queueing and its who-goes-first at a box are unchanged (open to overturn). The event
  car's distance to a zebra's paint (`to_the_paint`) stays its own, since `GiveWay` answers the paint's
  position, not a distance to it.
- `GiveWay.zebra_edge_ahead()` answers the paint's world coordinate rather than a distance, so the event
  car's past-the-box comparison stays float-identical.
- `City.siting_region()` builds the region once per plan and hands it again until the plan is another
  plan. A plan is never edited once the city holds it: the scene recipe's `_select_structures()` and
  `test_scene_recipe_task`, which edited the city's plan in place, build a fresh `RegionPlan` and hand it
  over through `City.set_region_plan()`, since a key on the plan's lists (the review's finding) would
  hand back old wall positions after an edit that kept their lengths. `ask_of_ground()`'s median over the 118 region bodies went from about 30 µs to
  about 2.55 µs per ask, days 9, 10 and 14 alike (`tests/probes/siting_ask_cost.gd`).
- A stale comment in `tests/test_resistance.gd` named `_mark_shows()`, which B2 removed, and now names
  `_drawing_shows()`.
