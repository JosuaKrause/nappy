# rosy-hawk — Where a plan's body stands is chosen once · 2026-11-21

**What was asked.** The entry rosy-hawk, a finding of the design of curly-stork (a planned body's pieces laid out once):
the point a plan's body is stood from was chosen in three places (`WalkGround._stands_at()`,
`RouteRigPlanner._planned_position()`, `PlanBuilder.body_stands_at()`), each the route's first point if the plan
had a route and where it was placed if not; a fact derived in more than one place is the smell **designing-classes**
names (inbox #1156 in gray-dolphin). Built by a Sonnet agent as #1606.

**What was built.** One static, `BodyStance.planned_body_at(map, plan)`: where a plan's body stands, `stands_at()` of
where the plan was placed (`placed_at()`). The walk's ground and the route rig ask it, their two copies gone, and
`PlanBuilder.body_stands_at()` stays a one-line delegate for the two placement rules that hold only the builder. It
sits on `BodyStance`, not the plan, because a plan holds no map; both are the model.

**The route's first point is dropped.** Every plan made with a route is made at the route's first point, so for a plan
with a route the two choices agree; a throwaway sweep of five cities over fourteen days found placed plans with a route
for only two rows, the street-corner beats (the homeless man yelling, the chatting mother), each standing at the same
point either way. The plan builder places its across-street and along-street routes at the tile's center with the
route running from its ends, where the two could differ, but none of the three callers asks about those rows. This
agrees with curly-stork's design (its F3: a plan's pieces are built at where it was placed).

**Measured.** curly-stork's sweep (`tests/probes/curly_stork_piece_layout.gd`) and `probes/escape_density.gd` print
the same on main and the branch. Nothing a player sees changes.
