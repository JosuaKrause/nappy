**Built (PR #415).** The `burning_building` row has no body and no danger of its own on the street:
`intensity` 0, `obstructs_radius` 0, no body shadow, so the fire truck it calls in is the danger;
its telegraph and radii stay for siting. `docs/COSTS.md` is regenerated. `EventInstance._draw_fire()`
draws taller flames against the building's facade, with the basement vent's steam pictures as
smoke. The row now sits `AT_THE_FRONT`, the one face the city draws a facade on, where
`AGAINST_THE_BUILDING` could site it by an east or west wall with no facade behind it; that is the
agent's placement choice, open to overturn. The last night's stairwell fire keeps its body through
`InteriorEvents._indoor_fire()`, a copy with the old 30px body and field, since it closes a
stairwell. The flames' size is fixed rather than read from the building's height, so a one-tile
building can burn under flames taller than its roof; open to overturn. The still is
`docs/evidence/building-burns-2026-09-27/fire-on-facade.png`; no still shows the fire truck
arriving, since a forced fire never summons one and the summon is covered by the suite.
