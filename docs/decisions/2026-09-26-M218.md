# M218 — The burning building burns · built 2026-09-27

*([PLAYTEST-143](../playtests/PLAYTEST-143.md), statement 4: "the fire of the burning building is on
the street -- the building itself is not burning" · [PLAYTEST-144](../playtests/PLAYTEST-144.md),
statement 12: "The fire goes on the building. The challenge is the fire truck not the fire")*

**Built (PR #415).** The `burning_building` row has no body and no danger of its own on the street:
`intensity` 0, `obstructs_radius` 0, no body shadow, so the fire truck it calls in is the danger;
its telegraph and radii stay for siting. `docs/COSTS.md` is regenerated. `EventInstance._draw_fire()`
draws flames across the burning building's whole facade, edge to edge and never on a column
another roof covers, with a smoke plume about every three tiles from the basement vent's steam
pictures ([sandy-egret](../playtests/2026-10-03-sandy-egret.md), statement 3: "the fire should be on
the whole building not only the door"). The row now sits `AT_THE_FRONT`, the one face the city draws a facade on, where
`AGAINST_THE_BUILDING` could site it by an east or west wall with no facade behind it; that is the
agent's placement choice, open to overturn. The last night's stairwell fire keeps its body through
`InteriorEvents._indoor_fire()`, a copy with the old 30px body and field, since it closes a
stairwell. The flames' size is fixed rather than read from the building's height, so a one-tile
building can burn under flames taller than its roof; open to overturn. The stills, the fire
along the whole front and the fire engine parked in front of it, and a burst of the flames are in
`docs/evidence/sandy-egret-fire-whole-building-2026-10-03/`.
