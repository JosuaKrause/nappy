# M215 — The power station's chimneys stand in front of her · 2026-09-27

*([PLAYTEST-143](../playtests/PLAYTEST-143.md), statement 1: "the chimneys of the power plant render
behind the player. they should be in front.")*

**What was wrong.** Buildings draw in their own layer (`Buildings`, z 1 in
`scenes/world/city.tscn`) beneath the y-sorted `Entities` layer (z 2) that holds her, the crowd and
the events. That is safe only while nothing walkable lies behind a building's drawing. Each stack
is 192px tall and stands two or three tiles inside the lot's north edge, so it reaches over the
street north of the station, where she walks, and she was drawn over it.

**What was built (PR #405).** Only the stacks leave the building: `Building.StationStack`, a
`Node2D` inner class of `building.gd`, draws one stack standing on its own origin, and
`City._spawn_buildings()` puts one at each of `Building.stack_feet()` in `Entities`, where the
ordinary y-sort draws it over anything north of its foot. The hall and the yard stay in the
building layer (`_draw_power_station()` became `_draw_station_yard()`). Moving the whole station
into `Entities` was rejected: it brings back a building drawn over the sidewalk beside it, the
thing the separate layer exists to prevent. `tests/test_power_station.gd` builds seed 7140 and
checks one stack per foot in `Entities`, none left under `Buildings`, and a stack picture reaching
past the lot onto walkable ground; it fails with "0 stacks, 2 feet" without the change. The stills
are in `docs/evidence/m215-stacks-in-front-2026-09-27/` (seed 61400, before on `main` and after).

**Open to overturn.**
- No still shows her in front of a stack, because it cannot happen: south of a stack's foot, within
  its width, is the station's own lot, which nobody walks on.
- A mark drawn over a head (her exclamation mark, a warning caret over a walker) is hidden with its
  owner when the owner stands behind a stack, since the marks sort with their owners. It happens
  only inside a stack's 32px width on the one street north of the station. The alternative is a
  layer for those marks above `Entities`.
