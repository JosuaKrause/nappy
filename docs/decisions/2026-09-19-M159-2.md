## Demand and the reuse boundary

The [method and input/write audit](../evidence/m159-danger-prediction-reuse-2026-09-29/README.md)
names every caller and effective sample input. An accepted rendered baseline audit executes all
original samples and counts repeated input tuples; its instrumented timing is not performance
evidence. Events make 54,849 gross requests and 18,172 eligible loops; 12,199 repeat the previous
inputs, including 9,086 in the same frame. Crowd bodies make 141,933 gross requests and 59,642
eligible loops; 11,114 repeat inputs, including 9,327 in the same frame. Each loop has twenty
samples. The 530-frame route travels 551.997 px north, with 200 walkers, 34 cars and median 52
events; visible medians are 18 walkers, seven cars and four events. These counts establish
actual equivalent demand, rather than assuming repeated function calls have identical inputs.

Each event or crowd body caches one future integral with its effective inputs copied by value:
source/query positions and player/effective source velocities. Event keys also include freshly
computed effective intensity, mutable definition intensity/falloff/core fields, effective body
axis, shape kind, spine length and rectangle extents. Shape radius affects the fresh reach guard,
not sampled field distance. Crowd keys include kind and every jolt field. Horizon/kernel tuning
inputs are immutable constants. A changed tuple recomputes samples using the original arithmetic
and operation order. No identity-only or approximate key is used.

Reach, phase/lifecycle guards, current contribution and effective velocity/intensity stay live
on every request. Current-rate subtraction, sensitivity and shared decay/total netting run after
the integral. Mutable per-bird flock geometry bypasses reuse. No source query, warning, lethal
projection, meter contribution or halo selection is skipped; existing plain contribution and
caret caches retain their semantics. Sampling has no simulation or RNG side effects. Gameplay,
population, horizon, sampling steps and visual cadence remain unchanged.

Small choices, open to overturn: one integral per source, exact value-array comparison rather
than setters on every mutator, reuse across frames when complete inputs match, flock exclusion,
and no caching of cheap-zero/net/lethal/caret answers. The queue's within-frame mechanism is a
proposal, not a player-authored limit; the audit shows repeats both within and across frames.
Complete effective inputs establish the same result in either case. Key costs are measured.
