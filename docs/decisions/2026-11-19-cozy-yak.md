# cozy-yak — The crowd asks a focus source where its patch stands, and set_focus goes · 2026-11-19

**What was asked.** The player answered the board question `crowd-focus-source` with "A" (grassy-bison
#1561): the crowd is handed a focus source that says where its patch of simulated streets stands, so
its tests stop needing `Crowd.set_focus()`, a member only tests called (the rule that such a member is
a loophole is curly-rabbit #1098). Filed as the entry cozy-yak, whose one item this pull request
(#1566) builds.

**What was built.** `CrowdFocus` is a typed interface with one method, `where() -> Vector2`.
`Crowd.use_focus()` hands it in, and the crowd's patch follows it once per tick in both `step()` (what
the test rigs tick) and the live tick, where it runs after the world advances and before the crowd
meets her, so the order against the shoves and strikes is what it was. `StrollerFocus` answers with
her position and is handed in by `WorldSimulation.meet_her()` beside `use_player()`; `main.gd` is
unchanged. `Crowd.set_focus()` is deleted, its line left `tools/test_only_members.txt`, and every rig,
suite and probe that called it hands `tests/fake_crowd_focus.gd`, a source a check moves.
`test_crowd_coverage` holds that the patch follows the source on `step()`; `test_stroller_focus` holds
that the game's source answers where she stands and that a crowd handed it is simulated around her.
Play does not change. That closes minty-quail's `tests-unseen-reads.md` bullet on `set_focus`, which
the filing (#1562) pointed here.

**Choices the design left open, each the builder's and open to overturn:**

- `use_focus()` places the patch at once, so handing a test a new source behaves as `set_focus()` did.
- The game's source is handed in `meet_her()`, and a null player hands none, which keeps `main.gd`
  out of the change.
- A source the crowd holds when `start_day()` runs moves the patch off `start_day()`'s focus on the
  next tick. In the game the two agree, since she stands at the doorstep. The two shared-city rigs
  (`crowd_rig.gd`, `crowd_closures_rig.gd`) hand `use_focus(null)` before each `start_day()`, since a
  source left from an earlier test put the day's crowd elsewhere (the rigs' wall-density check read
  no walkers) and CI shuffles suite order. Rejected: `start_day()` dropping the source, which would
  lose her on the game's next day.
