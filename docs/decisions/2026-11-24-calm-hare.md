# calm-hare — The event director sends what the walk's spec lets it send · 2026-11-25

The player: "the escape is just like a normal day with some special settings" and "all should be
implemented through the regular game engine's means" (inbox #785 in minty-lemur); "there should be
no dog walkers in escape it's not a calm night where people walk their dogs" (inbox #1733 in
spry-ferret); after being told where the escape's dog walker came from, "this sounds like what is
implemented is pretty far off from what we wanted" (inbox #1737 in minty-hare).

**What was wrong.** The event director (it places events just ahead of her as she walks) was started
for the escape like any day and never read the escape's `DaySpec`: it filled an ordinary day's route
bag by the run's day number. A direct boot (`--start-escape`, `?escape=1`) is day 1, so day one's rig
sent two dog walkers among her first three director marbles; a run's escape is day 14, where the
sprinkled charging dog is rolled one escape in four. The escape's dawn was already right.

**What was built.** `DaySpec.director_sends()` answers a `DirectorSends` value: `THE_DAYS_ROWS` for a
day, `NOTHING` for both of the escape's sections (set in `DaySpec.for_section()`, where their shared
settings live). `EventsAhead` hands it to `EventDirector.start_day()` (new required
parameter `sends`, no default: a default was the silent path the bug came through, so every caller
says what its walk sends), from `start_day()` and from
`start_a_given_walk()`. With `NOTHING` the director has no route bag, owes nothing from the plan and
rolls neither the day-one rig nor the sprinkled dog, whatever the day number is. **The escape sends
no dog walker in every boot, the direct one and a run's**, which is why calm-walrus's dog-walker item
is deleted by this change; the player's ask stays cited in calm-walrus's README. Ordinary days are
unchanged.

**Open point taken the plainer way.** Whether the escape's director sends none of an ordinary day's
rows or draws a bag of its own was open; the escape has none, since every other danger of the escape
is placed before she sets off. The hand-off is a value of the spec and not a flag; how the escape's army trucks on her route
(misty-plover) are carried through it, a further value or the marbles, is that entry's builder's to design.

**Rejected:** a yes-or-no on the spec (the trucks would reshape it); keying the director on whether
the walk is "planned whole" (a scene recipe's walk of an ordinary day is planned whole and sends its
day's rows).

**Tests.** `tests/test_event_director.gd` (the escape's setting on day 1 and day 14 over twelve seeds
sends and owes nothing, an ordinary day 1 and 14 owe) and `tests/test_walk_siting_spec.gd` (the
hand-off through `EventsAhead.start_a_given_walk()`); both fail with the fix removed.
