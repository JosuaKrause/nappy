real manager and plain plans, with a real city only in a suite whose header gives the reason (inbox #1156 and #1159 in

manager suite's cases run on drawn streets with plans of their own; three that start a day through the manager use the
generated map; the one case that asks the city's own dawn is the only one in a city. The fire suite's director cases
stand on drawn streets with a staircase route; its dawn cases moved unchanged to the scheduler's suite. A unit case
keeps a city, since a crew stands in front of blank walls only a city's buildings know. The pursuit suite's case that a
pursuer sent from off screen is warned first moved unchanged to the manager's suite. The writing-tests skill's sentence
that the manager's suite runs against a real generated city says what it now stands on.
