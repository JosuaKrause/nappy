# grassy-beaver — Day-one ideas set aside, kept to rethink · 2026-10-24


Every idea from the day-one discussion of 2026-10-24 that grassy-beaver (a day one that teaches) does
not build: what it was, whose it was, why it was set aside or left undecided, and what would bring it
back. The player asked to keep them all, the rejected ones too, "so we can look at them later and
rethink again" (curly-moose #829), somewhere that turns up in research without cluttering the
context (curly-moose #831). A set-aside idea comes back as a question to the player, never as a plan
(**playtest-feedback**). The chirp study's ideas not adopted are in coral-newt's README, "Not
adopted".

## A face instead of, or beside, the two bars

**What:** the player's toolbox idea, "we can get rid of the two bars and replace them with a more
obvious indicator (eg diegetic face)" (curly-moose #819); the orchestrator then proposed keeping the
bars and adding a bigger reacting face over the pram, and the research favoured that pairing (players
read a continuous value better from a HUD; a HUD icon paired with an in-world display did best).

**Set aside:** "I don't want to add the face yet especially if also keeping the bars. I don't want
the UI to be cluttered and also take away more visual space" (curly-moose #819). "Yet": it is not
ruled out.

**Would come back** if new players still misread how wound up or how sleepy she is after
grassy-beaver ships (its milestone counts), or if the bars themselves go.

## Another parent to follow to the park

**What:** the player's toolbox idea, "we can have some NPC walking towards a park that the player
can follow"; the orchestrator proposed a fellow mother with a pram walking ahead to the park, and the
research rated it the strongest of the subtle options (a figure that sets off catches the eye; in a
wayfinding study every participant used characters to find their way). The player first said
"following NPC let's explore that" (curly-moose #819).

**Set aside:** "maybe that makes a fellow mother obsolete. we already have a fellow mother but she's
an obstacle. so we don't need two types with different purpose which makes it unclear whether
approaching is good or not" (curly-moose #819); the orchestrator proposed dropping it and the player
agreed ("the rest sounds good").

**Would come back** with a guide that cannot be mistaken for an obstacle (not a mother, not a
pedestrian kind that costs), if day one still loses players on the way to the park.

## An opening shot on every day, showing the open parks

**What:** the player asked "is the opening shot only on day one? should we use it every day to
highlight parks that are open?"; the orchestrator proposed a quick glance at the open parks on days 2
and on, with no path, so the route stays the puzzle.

**Set aside:** "for the day 2 and on opening shots -- how would showing the park tell anything if
it's not placed anywhere via route? I think we can leave the opening shot to day 1 for now"
(curly-moose #819). Day 1's shot is an experiment in grassy-beaver.

**Would come back** if the day-1 shot works and players on later days walk to parks that are closed.

## A first-day line pointing at the tinted sidewalks

**What:** a commenter on the player's Reddit post suggested, in rough summary, a short first-day
line of text that teaches the player to notice the yellow-tinted safe sidewalks without a quest
arrow; the orchestrator offered to file it.

**Set aside:** "I agree that we need a better day one experience but I don't think the "warm
pavement" line is it", and "I deliberately don't focus on the pavement since the pavement itself is
an attempt to patch up issues from getting lost" (curly-moose #819).

**Would come back** only if the tint is kept as a teaching tool, which the player's reading of it
argues against.

## A more obvious sidewalk: fallen leaves and the like

**What:** the player's toolbox idea, "we can use more subtle hints (eg make the pavement more obvious
by eg also adding fallen leaves or something)" (curly-moose #819).

**Set aside:** the research found a faint cue spread over many kerbs does not stand out while the
player is busy, which is why the tint goes unnoticed, and the orchestrator judged more of the same
subtlety likely to fail the same way; the player's own reading of the tint as a patch (see
`text-about-the-tint.md`) points the same way. Not explicitly rejected by the player.

**Would come back** as reinforcement of something already taught (a subtle cue confirms a lesson; it
does not teach one), or as a moving cue (`moving-cues.md`).

## On-screen text as a briefing at dawn

**What:** the player's toolbox idea, on-screen text "(eg "this street seems to be blocked let's turn
around")" (curly-moose #819), read as either a briefing or a hint at the moment.

**Set aside as a briefing:** "on-screen text -- when it becomes relevant otherwise players will
forget and it will make the intro a slog. we want to start right into the action" (curly-moose
#819); the thought bubbles "might even make the text obsolete". Text at the moment it applies stays
in grassy-beaver as the rare fallback.

**Would come back** if a lesson cannot be said with an icon.

## Something moving toward the park at each junction

**What:** the orchestrator's proposal from the research (a thing that starts moving catches the eye;
landmarks at junctions cut navigation errors): petals or leaves drifting toward the park, birds
lifting off in its direction, at the junctions of day 1's route.

**Not decided:** the player answered the signs and the bubbles but not this; it was left out of the
summary the player agreed to, so it is neither built nor rejected.

**Would come back** as a question, beside the junction signs, if signs used sparingly leave players
lost at a junction.

## Sound that teaches: the baby fussing, birdsong near a park

**What:** the orchestrator's own idea: the baby fussing louder as her excitement rises, and birdsong
growing as she nears a park, so audio becomes a teaching channel. The game has no audio in play yet,
and audio is never the only channel for a warning (`docs/EVENTS.md`, "Audio is never the only
channel").

**Not decided:** not answered in the discussion; the sound tools come first (coral-newt).

**Would come back** with the first in-game sounds (M100's per-act beds and per-event cues).

## A lost day's summary shows her route beside a quiet one

**What:** the orchestrator's own idea: on a lost day 1, the summary draws the route she walked over
the map next to a quiet route.

**Not decided:** the research's simpler version, freezing on what caused the loss, is what the
player agreed to ("showing what causes a loss is also a good idea -- show not tell", curly-moose
#819, built as grassy-beaver's `the-loss-shown.md`); the map was not answered.

**Would come back** if showing the cause alone leaves players repeating the same route into the same
loss.
