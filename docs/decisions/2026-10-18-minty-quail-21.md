# minty-quail — Events step on the physics tick, drawn interpolated, and nothing they step calls their look (events-composed 3b/4) · 2026-10-30


minty-quail's `events-composed.md`, the second half of its third PR, after 3a's looks
(minty-quail-19). The item: every `EventInstance` stepped itself once per drawn frame in its own
`_process()`, while `EventManager` told them where she was and checked catches, hard fails and
detentions on the physics tick, so an event's step and the check of what it did ran on two clocks;
and the step called the look (the redraw and the move at the end of a tick, `transition()`'s
`hide_the_scenery()`, `EventCarDriving.end_frame()`'s redraw, `EventBarrierPost`'s `move_nodes()`).
The approved plan put the simulation on the physics tick in the manager's order, with `_process`
only redrawing; the orchestrating session picked interpolation A, so events stay as smooth as they
were.

**Built.**
- **The manager steps its instances once a physics tick, in a fixed order**
  (`EventManager._tick_the_events()`): it retires what finished and brings back what repeats; with
  her in the world it looks through the camera, sites and streams, places what is owed, summons
  what was sighted, watches the encounters, runs the warnings and tells every instance where she is
  (`_tell_them_where_she_is()`); then every live instance takes its step (`_step_them()`), with or
  without her; then the officers, the ground she is on, the doors and the detentions; and last the
  hard fails, on the tick whose step caused them. The step runs over a copy of `_instances`, so an
  event made during it (a successor, an officer, a summoned row) takes its first step on the next
  tick.
- **`EventInstance.step(delta)` is public** and is the whole simulation of a tick (a car's proposed
  frame around it, as before). `_process()` only runs the look's redraw gate
  (`EventLook.redraw_if_the_picture_changed()`), which also moves the drawing nodes and shows or
  hides the scenery from the state the last tick left; it is timed under the frame record's
  `events` bucket as the step was, and the step is timed inside the manager's own physics tick.
- **Nothing the simulation runs calls the look.** All of 3a's list went: the tick's three
  `redraw_if_the_picture_changed()` calls and its `move_nodes()`; `EventCarDriving.end_frame()`'s two
  redraws; `EventBarrierPost`'s `move_nodes()` from `back_at_the_band()`, `stand_as_the_barrier()`
  and `come_back_as_the_barrier()`; and `transition()`'s `hide_the_scenery()`, which is gone from
  `EventLook` and `SpreadLook` (a finished crash's or burst main's scenery is hidden by
  `SpreadLook._update_scenery()`, reading `is_finished`, on the next drawn frame). What the tick still
  touches that is drawing: it puts a pursuer that warns over the street on the warning draw layer
  (`_take_the_layer_it_warns_on()`, a z index on the event's own node, since a look never writes the
  event), `_be_done()` asks its own node for a redraw when a vehicle parks, and the behaviours reset
  the event's physics interpolation where it jumps.
- **Interpolation A.** The instance no longer opts out of physics interpolation: it is drawn between
  two ticks as she is, with `reset_physics_interpolation()` where it is placed or jumps (`_ready()`,
  `resume()`, `move_to()`, `EventBarrierPost.back_at_the_band()`, `stand_as_the_barrier()` and the
  guard's step out to his post, `_set_off_from_the_near_post()`). The far guard's node
  (`RoadblockLook.FarGuard`) places itself at `RoadblockFarGuard.at` on its own physics tick, after
  the manager's (`process_physics_priority` 1), interpolated, and is reset when it is made. The band
  and a spread's slices stand still and stay off.
- **A held event is held by a flag.** `EventInstance.held_by_hand` keeps the manager from stepping
  it; `SceneRecipeRuntime` sets it, and interpolation off, on the event it walks by hand each drawn
  frame, in place of `set_process(false)`.
- Tests: every instance `_process()` call became `step()`. Where a test drove the manager's physics
  tick and also stepped instances by hand, the hand-step is dropped, so nothing is stepped twice:
  `tests/test_full_run.gd` (`_simulate()`), `tests/test_loose_dog_arrives.gd` (`_run()`),
  `tests/test_patrol_vanish.gd` (`_frame()`, whose `her` is now a `Stroller` that reports running, so
  the manager tells the officer she runs, and whose lights are off at the manager),
  `tests/test_interior.gd` (the masked man's meeting, the shaft's respawns, his catch, and the three
  vent cases), and the probes `escape_stairwell_guard_trigger.gd`, `m179_fire_on_her_way.gd` and
  `m159_tick_sweep_events_cost.gd` (whose split mode times the step as its own column). The pelican
  `tests/test_encounters.gd` holds still is held by `held_by_hand`. The redraw gate's own test and
  the frame record's timed-body test run a drawn frame (`_process()`) where they ask about the gate
  and its timing.

**Verified** headless on macOS with Godot 4.7.2, at VERIFIED_AT.

VERIFICATION

**Choices made where the design was silent, open to overturn:**
- Interpolation A (the orchestrating session's pick): smooth as before, at the cost of every event
  being drawn up to a tick behind where it stands, as she is.
- An event made during the step takes its first step on the next tick.
- The far guard's node moves itself on the physics tick rather than being moved by the look on a
  drawn frame, because an interpolated node has to be moved on the tick.
- The warning draw layer stays set from the tick.

**Behaviour changes on purpose, for the remeasuring step:** events step on the 30Hz physics tick
with a 1/30s delta, in the manager's order, instead of once per drawn frame; a catch or hard fail is
checked on the tick whose step caused it, against where the step put the event; the redraw gate and
the drawing nodes follow once per drawn frame, from the state the last tick left, so a finished
crash's scenery hides on the next drawn frame rather than mid-tick; the instances and the far
guard's node are drawn interpolated between ticks; a scene recipe's held event is held by
`held_by_hand`. **One fairness margin moved:** the masked man's tightest runner line (walking until
he runs, then running, turning plain left at the centre of the row below the second flight's head)
was cleared by 0.6px on the tick, at 28.6px, because the catch was checked before his frames moved
him; checked after his step it is 26.4px, inside his 28px catch, and she is caught there
(`tests/probes/escape_stairwell_guard_trigger.gd`). Every other line keeps its outcome.
