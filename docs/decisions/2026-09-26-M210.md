# M210 — The brief between two days is the coming day's · built 2026-09-27

*([PLAYTEST-142](../playtests/PLAYTEST-142.md), statements 1 and 2: "we should make it strictly the
next day -- the title, day number, and brief should be for what's coming. also the nerves should
show." · [PLAYTEST-144](../playtests/PLAYTEST-144.md), statement 10: "You can keep the she feel
asleep after xx:xx and then do the rest about the next day.")*

**Built (PR #410).** `DaySummary.show_day()` keeps one line for the day that ended as its title
("She fell asleep after 1:24.", or "Dusk. You are still out." when the clock ran out), and
everything under it (the "Day N of 14" line, the nerves, the morning line) reads `GameState.day`,
the coming day. The function no longer takes the finished day at all, so nothing can hand it the
old number; every caller in `src/main.gd` and the tests changed with it. One screen carries both,
so the two-screen fallback the item allowed was not needed. The brief shows the nerves the
coming day starts with, drawn as the HUD draws them (M217).

**Open to overturn.** The summary's "You got her home." line on a won day is gone: it described
the day that ended, and the title already does that. If a won day should keep a line of its own,
it comes back under the title.
