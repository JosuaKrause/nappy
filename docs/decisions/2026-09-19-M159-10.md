# M159 — Nothing off screen is redrawn or animated · 2026-10-12

[velvet-marten](../playtests/2026-10-11-velvet-marten.md), #700: "Skipping redraws for of screen
elements. Also we don't really change things with animation if it's of screen. If we have a bounding
box that extends a little bit into of screen then it won't be noticable."

**Built.** `DrawnView` (`src/ui/drawn_view.gd`) is the one box: the world the viewport's canvas shows,
grown by 64 world px (two tiles), worked out once a frame. A crowd agent none of whose drawing
(`CrowdAgent.DRAWN_REACH`, two tiles round its feet and a car's doubled caret) is in it asks for no
redraw and no caret projection; an event out of it (its footprint, spread and largest caret, a
roadblock's band and a street fire's whole burning front and its smoke included, worked out once
since a fire never moves) works out no picture key, no caret projection and no redraw, the pictures
that redraw every frame included. Each redraws once on its first frame back, so a caret
owed on that frame is drawn on it, and every crowd redraw records whether it put a car's mark up, so
a mark that drops the next frame comes off. `DangerEdge` redraws only while a badge is up and once as the
last comes down; `HomeArrow.hide_arrow()` only when hiding an arrow that was showing. What things do,
where they are and their timing are unchanged off screen, and the animation clocks keep running,
since they are cheap and some feed play; nothing off screen is drawn, so nothing there is visibly
animated. The **cues** skill says a gate that skips off screen redraws on its first frame back.

**Measured before building**, desktop, seed 67: 12.9 of 16 crowd redraws a frame were off screen on
day 1 and 4.5 of 5.3 on day 14; off-screen car caret projections cost 219 µs and 130 µs; off-screen
event picture keys 400–502 µs, with 3 of day 14's 44 live events near the screen; 2.5 of 2.8 event
redraws a frame were off screen. **Not built, measuring nothing:** the meter bar (0.01 of its 0.16
redraws a frame drew an unchanged picture), and a car's bob moved out of its picture (about one
redraw a frame on day 1, against a canvas item more per car in view while the renderer is the
phone's largest cost).

**Before and after**, three rotated rounds of `--frame-record` on days 1 and 14, headless and
windowed, medians of three ([m159-offscreen-2026-10-11](../evidence/m159-offscreen-2026-10-11/)):
day 1 windowed, the game's own `_draw()` calls fell from about 18 to about 3 a frame, the `draw`
bucket from 544 µs to 138 µs and the frame mean from 8.37 ms to 7.07 ms; day 14 windowed, about 9 to
about 2, 376 µs to 220 µs, 6.27 ms to 5.75 ms. `events` fell about 0.4 ms on both days; `cues` rose
0.13–0.22 ms, since the halo's projection now pays for work the event carets used to leave it, and
the two together still fell 0.13–0.28 ms. `crowd` rose about 60 µs (3%) on headless day 1, the box
test's cost at 234 agents, and fell on headless day 14 and both windowed days. The fire's front and
the car's mark were fixed on review, after the measured commit, and were not measured again. Draw calls and render objects are unchanged. Every branch
run beat every base run on `draw` and `events`; one run beside two other engines was retaken. A
desktop on a shared machine gives the whole frame's direction, not its size, and nothing about the
phone. Two stills of one day-14 moment differ only inside walkers' boxes, since the walk follows the
wall clock.

*Open to overturn, chosen by the implementer:* the 64 px margin; the box read from the canvas
transform rather than `Tuning.VIEW_HALF_EXTENT`; the crowd's picture key still worked out off screen,
so its state does not depend on the camera; the animation clocks left running; the badge edge and
the arrow built despite measuring about 2 µs a frame each.

**Verified** by `tests/test_drawn_view.gd`: a red-marked car and cyclist walked in from off screen a
step at a time each ask for their redraw, caret up, no later than the first step any of their
drawing reaches the screen; no caret is projected off screen; an unchanged picture still redraws
on return; the badge is drawn on the frame it goes up and once as it comes down; a 640 px burning
front, panned from its far end, is redrawn on every frame any of it shows, while the fire's own point
is still out of the box. Disabling the gate
or dropping the redraw on return fails them.
