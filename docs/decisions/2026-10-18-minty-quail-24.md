# minty-quail — The events manager split into its parts, handed the player and the run (events-composed 4a/4) · 2026-10-30

minty-quail's `events-composed.md`, the first of three PRs that build its fourth, after 3b's
physics tick (minty-quail-21). The item: `EventManager` was 2,270 lines that streamed the day's
plan, made every instance, ran the warnings, placed what the director owed while she walked and
the poster tear's pursuer, and stepped the lot; it found the player through the `player` group;
`CheckpointController` hid her, held her, moved her and turned the camera itself; and the manager,
the resistance director, its happenings and its contact read the run off the `GameState` autoload
(`main-and-state.md`'s carry-over). The player asked for dependency injection throughout (minty-lemur
#794) and that no legacy shape be kept for a clean design (inbox #852 in minty-dolphin). The orchestrating session
approved the plan for 4a, 4b and 4c and its picks (open to overturn): a fifth part for the
director's half (`EventsAhead`), the doors' intents carried out by the manager, the player handed to
the resistance too.

**Built.**
- **`EventManager` is the face and hands its work to five parts**, each a `RefCounted` handed the one
  `EventWorld` they share (`src/events/event_world.gd`: the live instances, the map, the city and
  its map, the lights, the trace, the run, the player, the day, the visible view, the navigator):
  - `EventStreaming` (`event_streaming.gd`): the day's plan and the live instance each plan streams
    in as, carrying its state out and back, a set piece's group spent, the repeats, the masts, a
    finished plan spent;
  - `EventSpawner` (`event_spawner.gd`): every instance made (its scar and block arc, its body's
    footprint, the bang outside), a row warned of before it exists (`spawn_warned()`), what a
    finished row leaves, the rows summoned on sight, the run's pelican bag;
  - `WarningBoard` (`warning_board.gd`): the screen-edge badges up for something not in the world yet
    (`warn_first()`, `pending_warnings()`) and the mark over her head;
  - `EventsAhead` (`events_ahead.gd`): the director and what it puts in front of her while she walks
    (sited on her way, owed ahead, down her line or her heading), the men a stopped vehicle lets out,
    the poster tear's pursuer, a task's mast or row placed after dawn, the fire lit at dusk;
  - `CheckpointController`, as before, now handed the world and its guard's row
    (`EventCatalogue.door_guard()`) rather than looking it up by id.
- The manager keeps `setup()`, `setup_world()`, `start_day()`, `start_planned()`, `start_recipe()`,
  `stand_down()`, `clear()`, the tick in its fixed order, the excitement queries, the visible view,
  the hard fails and `what_struck_her()`, and answers every method a caller outside the events
  (the resistance, the city, the run flow, the dev rigs, the UI) uses by asking the part that owns
  it. A static moved with its owner and has no forward: `EventSpawner.age_when_warned()`,
  `as_warned()`, `obstructed_footprint()`, `where_the_summoned_row_stops()` and `_body_axis()`, and
  `EventSpawner.PelicanRig`; tests reach a part's own members on the part. It is 832 lines.
- **The player is handed in.** `EventManager.use_player()` replaces the `player`-group lookup:
  `main._put_her_in()` hands her to a city's events whenever she is put into a world, the
  building's events are handed her where they are built, and `DayDirector.use_the_world()` hands
  her to the events and the resistance it is given. `ResistanceDirector.use_player()` hands her to
  it and its contact (`ContactPoint.use_player()`); neither looks her up any more. Handed in, she
  is still out of play while she stands aside for the title screen (`Stroller.stand_aside()`, out
  of the `player` group): `EventWorld.has_player()` answers false for a `Stroller` out of the group,
  so behind the title nothing streams around her, notices her, warns her or strikes her, as the
  group lookup had it; a contact, which stands in the city that plays on behind the title, is
  touched by her only while she is in the group too.
- **The doors say what they do to her.** `CheckpointController` reads where she stands and her
  outright moves off the world and emits `takes_her_in`, `holds_her` and `lets_her_out`; the manager
  carries each out on the player it was handed, synchronously, in the controller's order (hidden and
  the camera on the door on the tick the hold starts; moved, shown and the camera back on the tick
  it ends, before the latch and the crossing count).
- **The run is handed in.** The manager takes `City.run` in `setup()` and a run in `setup_world()`
  or `use_run()`; `ResistanceDirector.setup()` and `ResistanceHappenings.setup()` take `run` or the
  city's. Nothing under `src/events/` or `src/resistance/` reads `GameState`; the comments that named
  it name `RunState`. That was all `main-and-state.md` still held once main-and-state 4c had built
  the rest (minty-quail-23), so the item's file goes, met as the one conflict when main was merged
  into this branch, and the entry's order no longer names it.
- New suite `tests/test_event_parts.gd`: streaming a plan out and back on its own, a warning creating
  its row once its time is up and one withdrawn, a scar recorded once over three stream-ins, the
  pelican bag drawn from the handed run and not the game's, the doors' intents with nothing moving
  her, and the manager seeing her only once she is handed in and not while she stands aside.
- **The parts go with the manager.** A warning still up holds the callbacks that create its row or
  owe it again, which hold the part that raised it, which holds the board: a ring of references
  that, with the parts `RefCounted` rather than the manager's own `Node`, kept the parts, the world
  and its map alive after the manager was freed (`test_pursuer_on_foot` and
  `test_recipe_structures` reported 3456 and 478 leaked instances at exit). The manager drops its
  warnings when it is deleted (`NOTIFICATION_PREDELETE`), which breaks the ring.

**Choices made where the design was silent, open to overturn:**
- The parts share one `EventWorld` rather than each being handed the map, run, player and
  instances separately, so a world handed over once is the world every part sees.
- `spawn_warned()` is the spawner's rather than the board's, so the two never hold each other: the
  board creates nothing, and the spawner warns of a summoned row on the board.
- `obstructed_footprint()` and `_body_axis()` sit on the spawner until 4c gives the picture-size
  geometry its own class.
- The manager's face keeps every method an outside caller uses; a method only tests used
  (`sent_pursuer()`, `a_pursuer_is_after_her()`, `walk_siting()`, `rolls_a_pelican()`) is asked of
  its part.
- The resistance director and happenings take an optional `run` beside the city, for the rigs that
  build them with no city.
- "In play" stays the `player` group's answer for a `Stroller` (`EventWorld.has_player()`, and the
  contact's touch), as `Stroller.stand_aside()` documents, rather than the run flow handing the
  events null while the title is up, which would race the day director that hands her in again.
- `CheckpointController` is handed its guard's row by the manager from
  `EventCatalogue.door_guard()`, a catalogue accessor like `mark_guard()`, rather than looking the
  row up by id itself.
- The moved code's comments are moved as they stood, names repointed; the class comments of the new
  parts and every sentence the move made untrue are rewritten (comments.md applied to the code this
  PR wrote).

**Behaviour changes: none intended.** The run, the player and the spawner are the same objects at
the same moments as before; the doors do to her what they did, in the same order on the same tick;
behind the title, where she stands aside, the events and the contact leave her alone as the group
lookup did. One edge differs: the lookup kept the first player it found, so a manager that had
found her before a later title kept seeing her behind it, where now nothing does, as
`Stroller.stand_aside()` says.

**For the remeasuring step:** nothing is meant to change. One test, `test_route_rig`'s day-11
mast target, had read its director's player off the `player` group, which handed it the suite's
own walker wherever earlier tests had left her rather than the fixture's player at the doorstep;
handed the fixture's player, day 11 puts its mast up beside the mark on open ground, and the check
now states the rule for either foot (the open foot itself, or the open ground beside a body's tile).
