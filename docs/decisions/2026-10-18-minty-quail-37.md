# minty-quail — The park cordon's officers ask SpawnSiting's named rules; the shows callable goes (shared-components B3) · 2026-11-01


minty-quail's `shared-components.md`, the item's last bullet, after every other placement moved onto
`SpawnSiting`'s named rules (minty-quail-29, minty-quail-30): `ParkCordon`'s officer placements still
checked their own copy (a tile an officer may stand on, `in_bounds` and `passable` for his `Mover`, and
the camera's view as a `shows(row, feet)` callable `ResistanceDirector._drawing_shows` built and
`ResistanceHappenings.tick()` carried to the cordon alone).

**Built.**
- `ParkCordon` is handed a `SpawnSiting` (`ParkCordon.new(map, navigator, city, block, siting, blocked)`;
  `take_the_park()` hands it the happenings' siting, or one over the map and navigator with no view for a
  rig that has none). Where an officer may stand (`_stands`, `_may_walk`) asks `Rule.OPEN_TO_IT`, which
  is exactly the old test, and whether any of him would be seen (`_seen`) asks
  `Rule.DRAWING_OFF_THE_VIEW` of his figure's footprint. No new rule was needed.
- `shows` is gone from `ResistanceHappenings.tick(delta, her, velocity)` and `ParkCordon.tick(delta, her)`
  and from every function between them; `her` stays where it is used for something other than sight.
- Removed with it: the rig fallback "no camera, so within `Tuning.OUT_OF_SIGHT` of her counts as seen"
  and the `_maybe_gone` clause that kept a walk with no player and no camera from ending. Every play path
  hands the cordon the city's siting, whose view is the camera's, so the fallback was only reachable in
  the cordon suites. Those suites now look the city's view through, about her or about nothing
  (`Rect2()`), as the camera does; the ground search for the way out (`_search_away`) keeps its own
  `Tuning.OUT_OF_SIGHT` distance test, which was never part of `shows`.
- Open to overturn: `OFFICER_ROW`'s footprint is read once when the cordon is made, not on every call.
- Open to overturn: the removed rig fallback means a rig with no siting gets one over the map with no view, where nothing counts as seen.

**Verified at test parity.** `tests/probes/cordon_placement_fingerprint.gd` (new, committed with the
change): 41 seeds, day 12 from the swing's touch, standing at the swing and walking to just outside the
last gap, printing the last gap, the deadline, when the first officer is in view, the way out, and every
officer's phase and position every 15 frames until the cordon is closed and its officers gone. The 21083
lines are byte-identical before and after (`diff` empty). The new test
`_test_the_officers_are_placed_by_the_siting_they_are_handed` asks `SpawnSiting.allows()` of the officers a
cordon placed under two cameras and fails if the cordon keeps a view of its own.
