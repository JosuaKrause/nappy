# M159 — The physics tick is cheaper: the influence sweep and the events' step · 2026-10-12


[jolly-trout](../playtests/2026-10-04-jolly-trout.md), #542: "Also what about the physics twice per
frame? Should we fix that?", then "Sure we can look into making the tick cheaper". The crowd's step
was the first part ([M159-6](2026-09-19-M159-6.md), [M159-8](2026-09-19-M159-8.md)); this is the
influence sweep and the events' step. The tick rate stays 30 and catch-up stays the engine's.

**Built, without changing behaviour.** `EventInstance.contribution_at()` answers zero for a point
beyond `EventDef.silent_beyond()` before reading intensity, velocity or shape: the body's reach plus
the largest radius the field reads and a pixel, that sum over 0.5 for a moving field's stretch (the
body's reach is not stretched; the steam's is 16 + 91/0.5, 198px). A flock and an instance that is
leaving skip the bound, since their fields are not centred on the node; the
planners' `field_reach()` is an extent, not a bound, hence a second figure. `Crowd.excitement_sources_at()`
skips an agent beyond `CrowdAgent.silent_beyond()`, which covers both kinds' outer radii and the
widest jolt any agent has had, a maximum that only grows. The events' step works a point plan's
distance out inline in `stream_around()`, tests a door's range before the boom-carriageway scan in
`_hold_that_would_begin()`, and reads `hard_fail` first in `is_lethal_at()`; each is a pure filter,
so the order changes no result. `docs/EVENTS.md` ("The emission model") and `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`
say why a far source is cheap and the sums do not move.

**Measured**, headless on a desktop, four revisions over three rotated rounds, seeds 478156010 and
4242, days 1, 9 and 14, two walks ([m159-tick-sweep-events-2026-10-12](../evidence/m159-tick-sweep-events-2026-10-12/)),
medians per tick, before → after: the influence sweep 288–379 → 148–156 µs on day 1, 129–167 → 75–110
on day 9, 198–225 → 96–145 on day 14; the events' step 341–420 → 295–372, 390–726 → 313–444 and
527–778 → 331–495. Day 1's step gains least, within this machine's noise. A pilot round run beside
a `check.sh` is kept only as an order-and-load table. The phone ran these systems about twice as
slowly, so the ratios carry over only roughly; whether two-tick frames get shorter needs a new phone
frame record. **Not built, measuring too little for what each would need:** `_summon_what_has_been_sighted()`
(about 40 µs) and `_watch_the_encounters()` (about 30 µs), each needing a kept plan index; the rest
of `stream_around()` (about 120 µs), needing kept positions or a spatial index, which the events
rules rule out as a spatial hash.

*Open to overturn, chosen by the implementer:* the probe's seeds, days and walks, with crying ticks
left out of the sweep's figures; `silent_beyond()` worked out on every call rather than cached, since
tests change a row's radii after an instance has read them; the crowd's widest jolt a maximum that
only grows, so a rig writing a huge jolt slows later sweeps but never changes an answer.

**Verified** by `tests/probes/m159_tick_sweep_events_cost.gd`, which keeps a running SHA-256 over
every event, agent, her alert and the baby's meters after every tick: across 864 runs every tick
matched the code before. `test_events_emission` checks the events' bound for every non-flock row at
every bearing, standing and moving (removing the stretch fails it on 8,176 samples), and
`test_crowd_contributions` the crowd's for every kind, pace and jolt. `Baby.reset()` leaves the
baby's own clock running across days, which moves nothing in play beyond rounding; the probe builds a
new baby for every run.
