# minty-quail — The route rig walks the escape's building: `--route` follows a run from the building boot to a city exit · 2026-11-01

minty-quail's `route-from-the-building` item. The open question was whether the rig should walk the
building too or only press its brief and leave the win to a person; the player: "building is always
the same so it's pretty trivial to set up. let's do it" (inbox #936, on the Nappy board under
route-rig-building).

**Built.**
- `BuildingWalker` (`src/dev/building_walker.gd`), built by `main` with the building section when
  `--route` is given and handed the building's map, the player and the section's clock. It presses the
  building's first brief through the same continue the city's rig uses, plans the way out when the
  section is planned, and follows it by pressing the move actions the thumb presses
  (`TouchControls._set_axis()`), so the doors' fade, the events, the meter and the clock run as for a
  player. It never moves her and never calls the win; it leaves the process running, so the city's rig,
  built when the city stands, carries on to an exit.
- `InteriorMapPlan.way_out()`: the legs of the cheapest walk from a tile to the service exit, each a
  walk inside one part ending on a door's threshold or on the exit, with the door's carry between
  legs. `walk_avoiding()` is `shortest_walk()` with ground to keep off.
- `BuildingPlan.fire_tile()` (the fire's cell, which `_place_the_fire()` now reads too) and
  `BuildingPlan.pursuer_ground_below_the_first_floor()` (the masked man's line from the foot of his
  shaft up to the first floor's landing). The walk keeps off both.

**Why the walk is the shape it is.**
- The fire closes the left shaft's flight from the second floor, so the way crosses at the second
  floor to the right shaft. Planned without keeping off the masked man's lower flights, the cheapest
  way down the right shaft is to the lobby, down the line he runs up, where a tile-for-tile walk meets
  him on his own cells: a flight is two cells wide, 22.6px across the slope, less than his catch.
  Kept off, the way leaves the shaft by the first floor's door, which his line never touches, and
  crosses the first floor to the left shaft: the answer the design gives.
- The shaft he runs is run, not walked, as the player must (`Input.action_press(&"run")`).
- A flight is walked by a sideways press only: the stroller turns it along the slope
  (`Stroller._redirect_along_a_flight()`), and a press with no sideways part is a climb. A waypoint
  on a flight counts as reached anywhere in its tile, and one is dropped once she is past it toward
  the next, so a corner is not walked back to a tile centre.

**Test.** `tests/test_route_building.gd` runs the game headless as the flag is run (`--start-escape
--route tunnel`) and reads the run log's `route` lines: the building is walked out, won, and the run
goes on to the city's exit.
