*([polite-dolphin](../playtests/2026-10-04-polite-dolphin.md), #555: "make them not start *on* the
mark", then "Unread, walk to it (Recommended)"; #557: "the fire truck scene starts too close to the
start -- the building is already on screen".)* This overturns [busy-raven](2026-10-03-busy-raven.md)'s
"the mark already read when the scene begins" and, for those scenes, #513's "the day's task offered
from the start", by the player's own answer.

**What was built** (PR #564). `ResistanceDirector.start_recipe_task()` places the mark where the
recipe pins it and leaves it unread; her touch places the task through the ordinary
`_on_contact_completed()` path, and `scene_task_errors()` reports what the placement refuses at the
read, which stops the scene with the reasons. The scenes of days 6 to 13 start her five tiles (about
160px) from the mark, just outside its 150px `SEEN_DISTANCE`, so the mark is never relocated; at the
first tick the mark is unread and nothing is arrowed, at the read the task is offered, arrowed where
the day arrows it, out of view and at its distance, and the walk then completes it.
`station-door-corner.json` and day 14 are unchanged. The fire-truck scene starts 64px further east, so
the burning lot, its rise and its smoke are out of both the landscape and the portrait view at the
first frame (a new `clear_of_both_views` observation asserts it). Start stills are in
`docs/evidence/busy-raven-scenes-2026-10-04/`.

**Proposed, not asked for, and open to overturn:** the five-tile starts (days 6, 7, 8, 11 and 13 start
west of the mark and turn back along the old route, since the ground east of it is not open); the
target placed from where she stands at the read, up to 36px off the mark, so the distance assertions
are loosened by that reach (540 to 644px); day 10's neighbor pin moved three tiles so it still clears
400px from where she reads; the portrait view read as the turned 720x1280 presentation at the same
zoom; the walks as timed legs, deterministic but needing re-tuning if movement or placement changes.
