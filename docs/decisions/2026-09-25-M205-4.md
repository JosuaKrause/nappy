# M205 — Halos past the cap: the strongest are lit first · 2026-10-04

*(The player, 2026-10-04, after [the 15-rim budget](2026-09-25-M205-3.md) was built: "if we have a
limit with number of halos the selection should be by intensity so only the top k most intensive
halos show at any one time" (inbox #529).)*

**What changed.** [M205-3](2026-09-25-M205-3.md) left two choices open to overturn: "the line
served oldest first rather than strongest first, so a strong pick that arrives while a weaker one
waits is lit after it", and "a re-picked rim that is still fading going on fading while others
wait". Both are overturned by the player's words above. Picking was already by intensity
(`ExcitementHalo.select_sources()` keeps the eight strongest by `contribution_at()`); what changed
is who gets a rim while all `EntityHalo.RIM_BUDGET` (15) blocks are held, lit or still fading:

- A pick that already holds a rim, lit or fading, is lit at once in place, since relighting takes
  no new block.
- Picks with no rim are served strongest first, in `select_sources()`'s order, while blocks remain;
  the rest are told zero that frame. The oldest-first line (`_waiting`, `_lit`) is gone.
- Unchanged from M205-3: freed blocks come back before anybody is served, a holder queued for
  deletion gives its rim back at once and is never lit, no fade is cut short, and the budget is
  never exceeded.

**What it costs.** Under constant turnover a weak pick has no bound on its wait; it is lit within
one fade (`FADE_OUT_SECONDS`, 0.8s) once the stronger picks stop.

**Where it falls short of the player's words.** While the budget is full, the rims of sources that
have dropped out of the top eight go on showing as they fade, and picks inside the top eight stay
dark until a fade ends. That is the opposite of "only the top k most intensive halos show at any
one time", and it is the price of the **cues** rule, the player on 2026-09-08: "all changes should
transition (hue and transparency) instead of immediately showing the actual value". How often it
happens in play is not measured. `tests/test_halo.gd`'s forty-event walk moves her 96px a frame,
about 34 times her running speed, and there the budget was full on 134 of 144 frames before and 139
after, with picks going without a rim on 563 of 1109 picked-frames before and 560 after: on that
stress rig the change is neutral in total. On the same row at running speed (2.8px a frame, 1440
frames) the budget never filled, no pick went without a rim and at most 11 rims existed at once
(9 at walking speed); M205-3's browser run peaked at four.

**"Intensity" is read as `contribution_at()`**, a source's rate at her position this frame, the
ranking `select_sources()` already used. A rim's drawn strength is `net_landed()`, what the source
delivered over the sliding window, so a car that honked and drove on can read red while ranking
low. Ranking by `net_landed()` instead is open.

**Proposed, not asked for, and open to overturn** (PR #531): a weak pick takes a freed block on a
frame when no stronger pick is waiting for it, so it can be lit ahead of a stronger pick that
arrives a frame later, because reclaiming the block would cut its fade short; unserved picks are
told zero every frame. Also open, and not built: when a stronger pick waits for a block, the
weakest fading rim fading out faster than 0.8s, still eased, so the top k show sooner without
breaking the cues rule.
