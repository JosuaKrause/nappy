# cozy-lynx — Every mast is exempt from the two line rules · 2026-10-11


Found while building [bouncy-egret](2026-10-10-bouncy-egret.md), not reported by the player. Day
11's routed second mast is the rigged bag the player set in
[coral-bunny](../playtests/2026-10-04-coral-bunny.md): "day 11 is going to be a x=3". It was put
down on 10 of 30 seeds: on most cities only 1–5 tiles of the day's route tree passed the placement
rules, because `_leaves_the_route_junctions_open` and `_leaves_the_routes_sidewalk_open` failed
wherever the mast's field reached the junction box at the end of its street. The six fixed masts
were already exempt from those two rules; the queued and routed masts were held to them, as
[feathery-marmot](2026-10-03-feathery-marmot.md) said ("the route junctions and sidewalks the day
keeps open").

Asked how the routed mast should get its place, the player chose (inbox #684):

> Exempt like fixed masts (Recommended)

**Every mast is exempt from the two line rules**, overturning feathery-marmot's sentence for the
queued and routed masts. `EventScheduler._counts_against_the_line()` skips every `loudspeaker` plan
(a candidate has no `mast_id` while it is sited, so the row is the test); the mast-site,
door-clearance, unused-calm, spacing and way-out checks stay. `docs/EVENTS.md` and the comments on
`_counts_against_the_line()` and `EventManager.queue_a_mast()` say so. The probe,
`tests/probes/bouncy_egret_day11_mast.gd`, now puts the routed mast down on 30 of 30 seeds, after 40
to 52 seconds of walking the day's route tree on the six it was timed on; she has to walk the tree,
so a day she walks into a corner is a day it waits, as before. `tests/test_resistance.gd`'s day-11
check walks the suite's own seed in full and asks `WalkSiting.ahead_of()` along the longest route on
five more cities that used to be refused (90210, 1234567, 314159, 555555, 8675309), failing without
the exemption, at about the suite time it had; `tests/test_mast_place.gd` checks the rule alone. The
still, [cozy-lynx-mast-2026-10-11](../evidence/cozy-lynx-mast-2026-10-11/), on seed 4242, one of the
refused cities, from a forcing patch not committed that walked her down the longest route, shows the
mast the game's own siting put down.

*Chosen where the entry was silent, open to overturn:* six cities in the regression rather than a
30-seed sweep, to keep the suite's time.
