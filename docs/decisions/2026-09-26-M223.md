**Why.** Of the 590 merges since 2026-09-01, 136 conflicted: 86 in `docs/DECISIONS.md`, 57 in
`docs/TODO.md`, 23 in `docs/HANDOFF.md` and 12 in `docs/REVIEW.md`, against 6 in the most conflicted
code file; and two branches claimed M208 on the same day.

**What is built.**

- **The layout.** A queue entry is a folder, `docs/todo/<name>/`: `README.md` holds the player's
  words, the playtest links and the context, and each item is a file beside it in full prose with
  no checkbox. A decision is `docs/decisions/<name>.md`; a review item is `docs/review/<name>.md`;
  a new playtest is `docs/playtests/<name>.md`. A name is `<date>-<adjective>-<animal>`. The entries
  and records from before keep their numbers: an entry's folder is `<date in its heading>-M<n>`, a
  record's file `<date>-<slug of its heading>`. `docs/TODO.md` keeps its header and the order, which
  links every entry's folder; `docs/DECISIONS.md` says where the records are and how to find one;
  `docs/REVIEW.md` keeps its header and how a run is set up; both of its lists, the next run and
  what no person has tested yet, are files *(2026-09-26: "Split every file where appropriate. Even
  the review file.")*.
  `docs/HANDOFF.md` is written by the session-cleanup pass only.
- **`tools/migrate-queue.py`** (with `tools/lib_queue.py`) moved the three files, reading them from
  a revision so that a branch meeting a `main` still on the old format re-runs it
  (`--rev MERGE_HEAD --replace`) instead of merging them. It asserts every structural anchor and
  verifies after. On the `main` it last ran on: 23,833 lines and 430 record headings in
  `DECISIONS.md` became 430 files, 23,390 lines in records plus 2 of header plus 441 blank and `---`
  separator lines accounting for all of them, each record checked line for line against its source
  with only its relative links moved; 76 record headings and 3 entry headings carried no date and
  took the author date of the commit that first wrote the heading line (not `git blame`'s last
  touch, which gives a record moved from `HANDOFF.md` the date of the move); 27 entries became
  folders and 78 items files, none mid-way; 112 review items, the next run's and the untested
  list's, became files. The two records headed "Amber otter", filed under the name of the dated
  playtest they answer, became files by the same rule as every other existing record, their date
  and heading: `2026-09-26-amber-otter-fence-graphics-accepted.md` and
  `2026-09-26-amber-otter-the-park-fence-has-upright-supports-and-mounted-signs.md`. A name is
  only ever a date and two words, so neither is read as a second use of the playtest's name, and
  `tools/new-name.sh` and the lint count that playtest's pair as taken.
- **`tools/new-name.sh`** draws a pair from `tools/names/` unused across the four folders, writes the
  thing with its heading and date and prints the name; `--entry` names a decision or a review item
  after its entry, `-2` and on for a second. **`tools/decisions.sh`** lists the records by date and
  finds them by title, then by text, with `rg`; `--in` searches the queue, the review items or the
  playtests. A citation "`DECISIONS.md`, M129, <title>" in the code comments resolves with it, so
  none was rewritten.
- **`tools/lint.sh`** governs the queue's files and the review items like any doc, spares the
  records and the playtests as history, and rejects a pair of name words used for two things, a
  checkbox in a queue file, and a link in `TODO.md` to a missing entry folder. The path-rules hook
  brings playtest-feedback to `docs/todo/**`, `docs/review/**` and every playtest.
- **`tools/convert-queue-edits.py`** converts an open pull request that still edits the old files,
  mid-merge: it migrates the three files at the merge base and at the branch tip and applies the
  difference to `main`'s side, three-way where `main` changed the same file, with `TODO.md`'s list
  of entry links merged as a list. Dry-run against every open pull request on 2026-09-26 (#362,
  #365, #368, #370, #372, #379, #384, #385, #386), each in a scratch worktree merging this
  branch's head as the `main` it will meet, then carried out there and linted: every one
  converted, none refused. Their other conflicts are not the queue's: `HANDOFF.md` (#368, #372),
  which no pull request edits now, and the using-tools catalogue and the CLI tests (#368, #370,
  #384). #385's two files under `docs/plans/` are not queue files and the tool leaves them where
  they are; the links to them from the item and the record it converts still resolve, since the
  migration moves every relative link to the file's new depth.
- **Retired**: `tools/resolve-decisions-top.sh`, since nothing inserts at the top of one file any
  more, and with it `tools/update-pr.sh`'s special case for that conflict.

**Proposed, not asked for, and open to overturn** (the orchestrator's; the player saw and accepted
the split of the old records, the name-making command and the duplicate-name lint, but not these):
no checked-in index, with `tools/decisions.sh` instead; an undated heading taking the date of the
commit that first wrote it; a review item's steps written as prose. The plainer alternative to each
is to leave it out.

**The overhaul stays queued as M223's one open item**: every open entry and review item re-read
against its playtest and rewritten in full, with the ones that no longer apply listed for the

**Choices made where the entry was silent, open to overturn**: a decision written for an item
while its entry stays open takes the entry's name with the review items' `-2` suffix and on, so
the entry's closing record is not always the bare name; the header lines of `TODO.md` and `REVIEW.md` were rewritten by the script
from text it holds, and it refuses when `main`'s old header differs; the `# The archive` preamble
of the old file became a record of its own, while the quoted superseded documents near its end
stay inside the record that quotes them; item file names are the first six words of an item's
bold lead; the grouping lines of M100's context ("Defects, each a few lines once found:") stay in
its `README.md` with the items that followed them now files beside it.
