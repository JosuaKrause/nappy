**Built.**
- **`ExcitementSource`** (`src/world/`): the ledger of what landed and its five-second window
  (`WINDOW`, `net_landed()`, moved from `ExcitementHalo`), the wall cache, where she is, the cached
  present rate, the five-second projection with its sampling loop, and the caret's strength. A type
  states only its own field, reach, projection inputs, clock, lethal course and caret key.
  `EventExcitement` (`src/events/`) and `CrowdExcitement` (`src/crowd/`) are composed by each body as
  `excitement`; `CrowdExcitement` holds the body's clock, the jolt a bump or a horn leaves and its
  kind's field radii. The event's field is public as `EventInstance.field_at()`, its distance as
  `field_distance_from()`, which the projection and the ordinary field both end in.
- **`ExcitementSources`** (`src/world/`) answers a set as one, with the door rule once in `all_at()`
  (checking every set before summing); `EventSources` (`EventManager.sources`) keeps the
  strongest-barrier rule and tells the others they are outranked; `CrowdSources` (`Crowd.sources`)
  skips bodies out of earshot and tracks the widest jolt per crowd, the process-wide static gone.
- **`SourceRim`** (`src/ui/`): one rim's lifetime; an event's `EventInstance.Rim` adds the guard's band.
- **`WorldContext`** holds `excitement_sources: Array[ExcitementSources]`, clearing minty-quail-25's
  fork; `ExcitementHalo` is handed the same sets; the pelican's signal is `EventSpawner`'s, through a
  first-landing callback. No forwarders.
- The four places the old copies differed keep their behaviour per type: the wall cache key, the
  event-only per-age cache, the private signatures, the pelican emit.
- New suite `tests/test_excitement_source.gd`. Docs, skills (cues, events, crowd-traffic) and comments
  naming the old per-body methods name the source.

**Verified at test parity** (inbox #813 in curly-moose), headless on macOS with Godot 4.7.2. An
excitement fingerprint (`tests/probes/excitement_fingerprint.gd`, committed first against the unchanged
code) prints per tick every source's charge, projected gross and net, lethal course, landed total, caret
strength and rim, exactly, over days 1, 9 and 14 on two seeds, the escape's city and the building on two
seeds: byte-identical before and after. `check.sh`, `lint.sh`, `ci_telemetry_kinds.py` and
`cost-table.sh --check` pass; the touched suites report no failure and no `SCRIPT ERROR`.

**Cost.** The first build made the halo's frame about 21% dearer (day 1 about 2.1 to 2.6 ms), from
cross-object reads in the projection; the orchestrating session did not accept it, since the player's
"eat the cost" was about the crowd tick (minty-quail-16) and the phone's lag is a live entry
(bouncy-swan). A second agent moved the jolt, clock and radii into `CrowdExcitement` and read each
projection's fixed inputs once; the halo's frame is now 10–31% cheaper than base on every walk measured
(median of three, base and head alternated), the crowd's tick within noise (284 to 282 µs), its position
chains identical.
