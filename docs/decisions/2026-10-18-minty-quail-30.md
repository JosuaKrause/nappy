# minty-quail — Every other placement asks SpawnSiting's named rules; legal ground tests the map's bounds (shared-components B2) · 2026-10-31


minty-quail's `shared-components.md`, its fourth PR (PR #887) under the approved plan, after `SpawnSiting`
(minty-quail-29). The item: every placement entry point other than the sent pursuers still checked its
own copy of the rules (the director's guard spots, roadblock ground, door, swing, mast and neighbor
starts and `_pick_reachable`; `EventsAhead`'s officers' doors; `PendingWarning`'s places down her line,
along her heading, on a route and in a lane, which `EventSpawner`'s summoned stop uses;
`ResistanceHappenings`' day-10 raid and day-13 column), and legal ground did not test the map's bounds,
which only a scene recipe's exterior reaches.

**Built.**
- New named rules on `SpawnSiting`: `GROUND_EVEN_IF_HELD` (a district door's crossing), `OPEN_TO_IT`
  (on the map and passable for the thing's `Mover`, no soft seal, no standing body),
  `DRAWING_OFF_THE_VIEW` (no part of the box about its feet on the camera's whole view, replacing the
  director's `_box_shows`, `_guard_shows`, `_mark_shows` and `_drawing_shows`), and the lists
  `CONTACT_GROUND`/`HELD_CONTACT_GROUND` the resistance asks of every tile it places on; helpers
  `ask_of_ground`, `ask_of_drawing`, `ask_in_sight`, `SpawnSiting.over(map, view)`,
  `lead_up_a_lane(box)`.
- `is_legal_ground()` and the new `is_open_to()` test `map.in_bounds()`.
- Every entry point above asks the rules, keeping its own candidates, order and random draws; the raid is
  handed the siting (`ResistanceHappenings.setup(…, spawn_siting)`) and the column's lead comes from
  `lead_up_a_lane`. `PendingWarning`'s statics keep their signatures and build `SpawnSiting.over()`
  inside rather than being handed a siting (open to overturn: about 25 test and probe sites would change
  otherwise).
- `ResistanceDirector.set_sight(sees)` takes only what she sees; the on-screen answer is gone, with
  `_open_to_her`, `_walled_alleys`, `_box_shows`, `_shows`, `_mark_shows`, `_guard_shows`,
  `PendingWarning._is_its_ground` and the guard spots' `walled_alleys` parameter, no forwarders.
- Kept as they were, open to overturn: a siting over no map answers yes to the ground rules (a world with
  no director letting the officers out); `_reachable_from_home` stays the director's (its own grid);
  `ResistanceDirector.target_ground()` calls the static `is_legal_ground` directly for dawn planning.
  `ParkCordon`'s officer placements were not on the list and still check their own copy; the entry
  carries them.
- The orchestrator's queue move fixed four comments the move made untrue (`EventManager.on_screen()`,
  `EventWorld.visible`, `VisibleView`, `scene_recipe_runtime.gd`'s `_box_on_screen`).
