# spry-llama — Influenced counts the game's real catch and hold · 2026-10-05

Inbox #586 in [olive-hedgehog](../playtests/2026-10-05-olive-hedgehog.md), said in a session on 2026-10-05. Asked whether an encounter's "her inside its lethal
reach or its hold" should stay a distance test — so that a guard through a wall, or a cyclist still
only a warning badge, counted — or use the game's real catch test, the player answered: "use the
real catch code."

**Built.** The reach and hold arms of **influenced** ask the game's own tests rather than a
distance. The catch is `EventInstance.is_lethal_at(her)`, the predicate the manager's hard fail
already asks: the instance is live, not waiting, not telegraphing, she is within its reach, and a
pursuer has a clear line to her. The hold is `EventManager._hold_that_would_begin(at)`, the part of
`_check_detentions()` that picks which hold would begin, moved out of it unchanged so the manager
and the watch ask the same question; the manager's behaviour is the same. The watch only reads:
everything both tests call reads state, and starting a hold stays in `_check_detentions()`. The 10%
landed and the chasing arms are as spry-llama built them.

**Chosen while building, open to overturn:**

- A hold counts only for the one instance the manager would pick: the nearest in range, none while
  another hold runs, release latches and booms respected.
- The watch runs before `_check_detentions()` in a frame, so on the frame a door releases her it
  sees her where she stood before the release.
- A live pursuer is chasing, and a chase counts as influenced (inbox #577, "let's count chases and
  catches as influenced always"), so a guard who chases her with a wall between them still counts
  — through the chase, not the catch. What the real catch test removes is a reach counted while the
  thing could not catch her: a cyclist or lorry still telegraphing or waiting, and a pursuer that has
  not begun its chase with a wall between them.
