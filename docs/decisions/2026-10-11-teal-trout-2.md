# teal-trout — The masked man waits standing · 2026-10-11


The [teal-trout audit](2026-10-11-teal-trout.md) found the masked man on the escape's stairs drawn
inverted: `_draw_masked_pursuer()` (`src/events/event_instance.gd`) drew `GUARD_LUNGING_BY_VIEW`
whenever `_stands_still_now()` was false, and that predicate covered his notice and a stop but not
his wait, so the waiting man read as charging and he was drawn standing only after he noticed her.
He runs a fixed line up the shaft rather than at her, so he is outside the three-stage rule; the
item was the inversion alone (*proposed, not asked for*, in the entry).

**Built.** `_stands_still_now()` is also true while `is_waiting()`, so he stands while he waits,
stands through his notice, and lunges once the notice is over. The predicate stays the one source
both its readers share; the other, `_draw_policeman()`, has no `pursues_within` and never waits, so
it is unchanged. *Open to overturn:* putting the wait inside the shared predicate rather than in
`_draw_masked_pursuer()` alone was the implementer's choice.

**Verified** by `_test_masked_man_is_drawn_standing_until_he_goes` in `tests/test_event_views.gd`,
which pins the picture of each stage. No clip: two `tools/shot.sh --start-escape stairwell:left`
captures landed on the escape's briefing card, which neither `--press key:enter` nor `--press
pause` dismissed.
