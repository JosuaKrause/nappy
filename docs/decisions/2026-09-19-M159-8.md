# M159 — A cheaper crowd step: the agents' update, huts by line, integer lane keys · 2026-10-11

From [make-the-physics-tick-cheaper.md](../todo/2026-09-19-M159/make-the-physics-tick-cheaper.md)
([jolly-trout](../playtests/2026-10-04-jolly-trout.md), #542: "Sure we can look into making the tick
cheaper"), on the phone records of [velvet-marten](../playtests/2026-10-11-velvet-marten.md), where
the crowd took 2.9 ms a frame on day 9 and 5.5 ms on day 14 and a tenth of day 14's frames ran two
ticks.

**What is built, every agent identical tick for tick.**
- **The agents' own update:** a walker's redraw computes `velocity()` once, `velocity()` skips the
  pocket factor for walkers (always 1.0), and `_has_left_the_field()` reads the rooms beyond the map
  once and builds the field's bounds inline.
- **Huts and gates by line:** once a tick they are bucketed by column and row (128 px), and each
  walker or car looks only at the buckets its line covers; ties still go to the first hut in the
  day's order and the gate loop keeps its car order.
- **Integer lane keys:** `lane_key()`, `make_lane_key()`, `turn_lane_key()` and `TrafficIndex` use
  packed integers rather than formatted strings, `CrowdAgent.lane_key_parts()` is their inverse, and
  `tests/test_crowd.gd` checks every axis, corridor, lane and direction packs to its own key.
- **The probe** (`tests/probes/m159_crowd_tick_cost.gd`) runs day 14 too and a second walk that
  chases the nearest walker, so the player half is in its hash.

**Measured** (Apple M2, headless, the probe's hash chain matching in every case;
[m159-crowd-tick-2026-10-11](../evidence/m159-crowd-tick-2026-10-11/README.md)): the whole crowd
tick's median fell 24–44% on day 9 and 42% on day 14 across two seeds; on day 1 the queue resolve
fell from about 122 to 100 µs (the whole tick's 15–17% there carries about five points of the runs'
fixed order, which the evidence discloses); the agents' own frame about 16%. Whether the phone's two-tick frames shorten is for a new phone record.

*Chosen by the implementer, open to overturn:* the bucket width and rebuilding the buckets every
tick; the lane key's packing (`-1` for no turn); `has_stretch()` kept as a call, since storing it
measured inside the noise; three smaller calls left unmeasured.
