# M159 — The readout says its resolution and refreshes less often · 2026-10-11


[velvet-marten](../playtests/2026-10-11-velvet-marten.md), #700: "We need to more resolution of the
debug readout, yes", read back as both the resolution and the refresh and answered "Both
(Recommended)".

**Built.** The frame record's `environment` carries the window size (CSS pixels on a page, pixels on
the desktop), the device pixel ratio (`devicePixelRatio` on a page, the screen's scale natively)
and the size actually drawn (the canvas's backing store on a page), read when the record is saved
(`FrameRecorder.view_size()`, `src/telemetry/frame_recorder.gd`); a page that cannot read its window
or ratio records `-1` for the three, and the readout says `window unknown`. The readout shows them as
`window` and `drawn` lines; the spike view sits lower to clear them. The readout rebuilds its text
every 0.25 s (`Main.READOUT_INTERVAL`) rather than every frame, at once on the frame the layer is
switched on, and its slow-frame line shows the latest slow frame at each rebuild; the record keeps
every slow frame and the spike view is fed every frame. The phone analyser
(`docs/evidence/m159-phone-frame-record-2026-10-04/analyse.py`) fits sections 5 to 7 within day 1's
first 40 s when the record holds enough of it and otherwise within the stretch it holds most of,
says "(no day 1 in this record)" in section 3 when there is no day 1, prints `section N skipped` for
a section it still cannot fill, and goes on; only empty-data errors are caught, so a mistyped column
still raises: velvet-marten's day-9 and
day-14 records, on which it stopped in section 6, now print all ten sections, and the 2026-10-04
record's report is unchanged. `docs/TELEMETRY.md` says so.

*Open to overturn, chosen by the implementer:* the refresh is a plain quarter-second timer rather
than "only when a line changes"; on a page `window` is in CSS pixels, so window × ratio is about
`drawn`; sections 5 to 7 fall back to the record's most populated stretch rather than being skipped.

**Verified** by `tests/test_frame_record.gd` (the keys and the ratio, a headless window reporting
0×0, and the five keys in the saved record's environment) and `tests/test_main.gd` (the refresh), a desktop still of the readout
([m159-readout-resolution-2026-10-11](../evidence/m159-readout-resolution-2026-10-11/)), and the
analyser's output on velvet-marten's day-9 record before and after.
