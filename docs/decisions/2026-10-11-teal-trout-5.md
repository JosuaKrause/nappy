# teal-trout — The chargers' stages are wired: the dog and the guards stand for their notice · 2026-10-17


The player (inbox #748 in [woolly-penguin](../playtests/2026-10-17-woolly-penguin.md)): the robber
walks toward her while warning, the guards lift a hand, nobody else walks, and the dog is wired with
its approved SVG poses while its gallop keeps its comic picture.

**Built (PR #757).** A new field, `EventDef.stands_for_its_notice`, is set on `charging_dog`,
`roadblock` (its hunting copy reads it) and `door_guard`: during the notice `_chase()` closes no
ground, and the lunge still fires the moment she walks inside the stand-off along a clear line. It is
read only on an instance that waited for her or set off beside her, so day 3's dog, sent down her
heading, gallops at her throughout; `validate()` refuses it on a row that can never read it.

- **The charging dog** sits while it waits (`charging_dog_resting_*`), stands still facing her in its
  alert (`charging_dog_alert_*`), then gallops. Rest and alert are drawn from their SVGs, the gallop
  from its comic PNG; the player judges the switch in play.
- **The hunting roadblock's guard** raises his hand (`guard_alert_*`) and holds still at his post for
  his notice, then lunges.
- **The door guard** holds the same alert where he stepped out, then lunges; the masked man, the
  van's guard and both guardsmen never draw it, and a test pins that.
- **The alley robber** (and the chalk-mark guard and day 13's guard, the same row) waits, walks at her
  in his alert, then lunges; the robber a task sends never shows the alert. His alert is the two-frame
  walk drawn in PR #750, wired here; the player's look at it in play is the PR's question 2.

Each stage's picture is a query (`charging_dog_pictures()`, `robber_pictures()`,
`chaser_pictures()`, `masked_pursuer_pictures()`); twenty SVGs joined the atlas membership.

**The fairness re-check**, no `Tuning` constant changed (`tests/test_events_pursuit.gd` for the day-5
dog and the heat-4 roadblock, `tests/test_checkpoints.gd` for the door guard): walking in still meets
the lunge at the full stand-off (101px of 104 for the dog, 103 of 106 for the guard); nothing is
lethal during the notice; running at the lunge still escapes (68px and 70px at the closest); walking
away is still caught, later than before (the dog at 17.9s, was 6.6s; the roadblock guard at 8.75s, was
3.88s; the door guard at 4.73s); standing still is caught later (the dog at 5.25s, was 0.77s; the
guard at 2.57s, was 0.78s); running away still shakes both off, at 1.53s. With its 4.5s notice the
dog's lunge can come from off screen at a player walking away, under its screen-edge badge.
