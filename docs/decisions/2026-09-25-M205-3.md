# M205 — The man shouting lights his halo on the web · built 2026-10-04

*([PLAYTEST-140](../playtests/PLAYTEST-140.md), statement 4: "the yeller has no effect on the meter
and its halo doesn't even turn on", v0.18.0 on a phone; [PLAYTEST-144](../playtests/PLAYTEST-144.md),
statements 21, 24 and 27, the last with no halo in desktop Chrome on the released page. Asked on
2026-10-04 whether they had checked it again, the player: "hmm, I gave you all the info on that
already. what else do you need?" (inbox #514), so it was reproduced from the recorded evidence.)*

**Built (PR #525).** In desktop Chrome, on the released page (v0.23.0) and on local release exports,
the man shouting charges the meter as his row says (the readout's `incoming` line reads 18.6 to
29.4/s with him at 94 to 147px, and nothing past his 210px reach), but **his rim never draws**,
while a walker's and a car's rims beside him do.

**The cause is an engine limit the Web export alone has.** The Compatibility renderer gives every
canvas item that draws with an `instance uniform` a 16-`vec4` block, first free block first, and its
canvas shader reads only 256 `vec4`s of them (Godot 4.7.2, `MAX_GLOBAL_SHADER_UNIFORMS` in
`drivers/gles3/rasterizer_canvas_gles3.cpp`), so only sixteen rims can be read at once. A rim given a
later block reads its colour as zero and draws nothing, with no error. `EventInstance` built a rim
for every live event as it entered the tree, about 45 at dawn, so most events, him included, held
blocks the shader could not read; the crowd built its rims only when needed and often got freed low
blocks. Native and headless runs have no such ceiling, which is why every earlier reproduction lit
him, and why the local debug export drew no rims at all.

**The fix.** `EventInstance` builds its rim on its first nonzero glow and frees it once faded or cut,
as `CrowdAgent` already did, and `ExcitementHalo` keeps the rims alive at once within
`EntityHalo.RIM_BUDGET` (15, against `COMPATIBILITY_RIM_BLOCKS`, 16): when more sources drop out of
the picked set within one fade than the budget allows, the extra rims are cut instead of fading,
events first, then the crowd. M89's single shared material and its `instance uniform` stay.
`tests/test_halo.gd` holds the rim count within the budget during a walk past forty live events
faster than a rim fades, and an event's rim held only while picked; both fail on the code before the
fix. The **godot** skill carries the sixteen-rim limit as a trap. Before-and-after stills, the probe
and the scripts that rerun the browser comparison are in
`docs/evidence/m205-yeller-halo-web-2026-10-04/`.

**The meter half did not reproduce.** In every browser run he charges her by the amount his row
says, so his row, the meter and `docs/COSTS.md` are unchanged. The review item asks the player,
after the release, whether the meter moves beside him on the phone.

**Proposed, not asked for, and open to overturn:** the budget of 15, keeping one block for the
throwaway quad `main.gd`'s shader warm pass draws with the same material; cutting a fade short
when the budget is full, which is new; keeping the `instance uniform` rather than moving the rim's
colour to a vertex-colour channel, which would lift the ceiling but tint each rim by its draw's own
colour.
