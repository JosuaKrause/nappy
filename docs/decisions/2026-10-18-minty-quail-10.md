# minty-quail — Events keep one stored phase, the pursuit settings grouped, one warning clock, the doors in a controller · 2026-10-24


minty-quail's `events-composed.md`, its first of four PRs and the refactor's tenth (PR #844). The item:
`EventInstance` had no stored phase. About thirteen definition flags and eighteen instance booleans
were read again each frame (`warns_by_radius`, `is_telegraphing`, `is_standing_for_its_notice`),
four charge triggers and two ways of pausing a notice sat side by side, five warnings were written
five ways, and the door crossing lived in `EventManager`. That is why the charger radii and the
day 9 crossing kept opening new holes, one path at a time (minty-lemur #788). The player left the
shape of the classes to the builder (minty-lemur #797).

**Built.** One stored phase, `EventInstance.Phase`:
- `WAITING`, `WARNING`, `ACTIVE`, `STOPPED` (the policeman a patrol car let out, standing with her
  through his stop), `LEAVING`, `PARKED`, `SPENT` (a roadblock's band once its guard has gone for
  good) and `DONE`.
- `transition()` is the only thing that changes it. It also lets the field cache go, and on `DONE`
  it hides the row's scenery.
- `is_finished`, `is_leaving` and `is_parked` read the phase. `_lunged` and the separate
  spent-guard flag are gone.
- A telegraph or notice whose time has run moves to `ACTIVE` in `_settle_the_notice()`. That runs
  after the tick's notice clock has been held, and whenever `age` is set from outside.
- A lunge, a flock's flush and the masked man's run move it by `transition()` the moment they fire.

One warning clock, `Warning` (`src/events/warning.gd`). It starts, can be held, and is called off or
runs out into an action, always read against its owner's clock. Three things run on it:
- an event's notice (`EventInstance._notice`, against `age`). It replaces the stored notice time,
  the held lunge's pause and the masked man's moving notice start; each of those is now
  `_notice.hold()`;
- a door crossing (against `CheckpointController._crossing_for`);
- the screen-edge badge up alone (`PendingWarning`, against its time up).

The sixteen flat pursuit fields on `EventDef` become sub-resources:
- `PursuitSpec`: speed, `start`, `warns_for`, `stands`, `walk_speed`, `lunge_reach`,
  `catch_reach`. `start` is one enum: notices, sets off beside her, charges at once, arrives
  chasing.
- `NoticeSpec`: `within`, `quiet`, `runs_within` (the masked man's run distance).
- `DaySwitch` (`EventDef.from_day_after`): `charging_dog`'s siting and notice from the day after
  the lesson.
- `EventDef.hunts_as`: the pursuit the roadblock's hunting copy takes in `at_heat()`.

`validate()` loses the pairing checks the grouping makes impossible to write. It gains two: a
notice needs a distance, and a row hunts as a pursuer exactly when it says how.

`CheckpointController` (`src/events/checkpoint_controller.gd`) takes the district doors out of
`EventManager`: the holds, the release and its latches, the door lines and walks under a raised
boom, the crossing's warning and the crossings held behind it, the hut guards' raised hands, the one
guard a crossing sets on her, and a hold ending his chase.
- It is handed the manager's live instances (shared), a spawn callable, the map, the run-log trace
  and the player.
- It hides her and turns the camera to the door on the frame the hold starts, so the instance's
  `player`-group lookup is gone.
- `EventManager` keeps `door_holding_her_at()`, `walks_under_a_boom()` and `door_crossed`
  (forwarded).

**Choices made where the design was silent, open to overturn:**
- The instance stays the Node2D in the world, and its views (PR 3) will be objects it hands drawing
  to, rather than a non-node model with a separate view node.
- The manager will step its instances (PR 3), rather than each running its own `_physics_process`.
- The phase list above. Caught, shaken off, outlasted and cut short stay `ChargeRecord`'s outcomes,
  not phases.
- `is_waiting()` reads the notice clock, so a row that leaves or is gone before it ever noticed her
  answers it as before.
- The masked man's run distance is a notice setting, since he waits and runs his line rather than
  pursuing.
- The controller is a RefCounted, kept current by the manager's setters until PR 4 injects the
  player.
- The readers' questions are `notice_within()`, `chase_speed()` and `starts_by()`, with no flat
  alias of an old field kept.

**Decided for PR 2** (the coordinator's answer to Fork 1, A): the day-3 lesson dog and the guard at
a chalk mark or a task keep their row ids, `charging_dog` and `alley_robbery`. GoatCounter's event
names and the day's loss causes are built from those ids, so new rows would change them. The
director that sends the lesson will mark it, and the guard will be moved with his saved state
rather than retired and spawned again. That fixes the state loss the item names. The item's "own
row" is a direction, since minty-lemur #797 left the shape to the builder.

**For the remeasuring step:**
- **A notice carried across a stream-out keeps its held time** (a held lunge, a charger behind a
  wall at his warning's last moment). It comes back exactly as far run, where it used to come back
  as far further on as it had been held. This is measured only by `test_event_phase`'s round trip on
  the alley robber, not in a played day.
- **The badge's clock counts up to `warned_for()`** rather than down to zero.
  - In arithmetic outside the game, at ticks of 1/60s and 1/30s, the two end on the same tick for
    0.2s, 0.5s, 0.53s, 1.0s and 2.13s.
  - At test steps of 0.1s and 0.05s they can end one tick apart on an exact boundary (0.5s at 0.1s
    steps, 0.2s at 0.05s steps).
  - No test's numbers moved.
- **Every phase change lets the field cache go**, where before only a finish, a departure, a spent
  band and a stopped warning did. A lunge, a notice or a flush mid-tick then answers a later query
  at the same age and point freshly.
  - That the meter is unaffected is read off the code (it asks on the physics frame before the next
    tick moves `age`), not measured.
  - The halo and the caret may differ for that tick.
- **A row that finishes at the end of its departure is `DONE` only**, where it used to answer
  `is_leaving` too. Every reader in `src/` asks `is_finished` alongside it, or meets a finished row
  only on the frame before it is retired.
- No phase-machine bug was found or fixed in this step.
