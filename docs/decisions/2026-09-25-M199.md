# M199 — The closing roadblock stands on the middle of its street and covers all of it · 2026-11-25

**The ask.** The player asked for a roadblock that closes the street fully (PLAYTEST-135 7) and said
there are two kinds, one open and one closing the street (inbox #1491 in silky-lemur). The open kind
is downy-llama's; this builds the closing kind beside it.

**Where it stands, read against the player's words.** One sidewalk must stay open on a street her
routes run along (inbox #747 in woolly-penguin), the open roadblock is sized to one sidewalk and the
road for that reason (inbox #1465 in spry-plover), the board question #1491 answered offered the
closing kind off her routes' streets and the answer did not correct that, and the ground outside
her paths is where blocking events stand (PLAYTEST-17 12). So the closing roadblock stands off her
routes' streets, beside and off them. A view's bag holds only what that view can place
(inbox #1185 in gentle-owl), so the bags of her routes' streets hold no marble of it.

## What was built

- **A row of its own, `closing_roadblock`**, built from the open row's draft
  (`EventCatalogue._roadblock_draft()`): the same guards, hunt, field and silence, with a band
  reaching `EventCatalogue.CLOSING_ROADBLOCK_REACH` (96px) either way, all 192px of the street. A
  row, rather than a width keyed on where a roadblock stands, so a save, the run log and a plan all
  name it, and every rule that reads a row's band reads it. Drawn from day 7 at weight 1.0 against
  the open row's 2.0, never in a junction box.
- **It stands on the middle of its street**: `EventDraft.closing_its_street()` writes it,
  `EventDef.stands_mid_street()` answers it beside `stands_at_its_kerb()` (which no longer holds
  for it), and `CityMap.street_middle_beside()` beside `kerb_beside()` (both through one private
  helper) says where, asked by `BodyStance.stands_without_a_map()`, the one place that decides where
  a body stands, so a plan's `body_at()`, the live body, its look and the guards' posts all follow.
- **The day's rules need no change**: read at its middle with a 96px band, `OneSidewalkOpenRule`
  refuses it on every street of her routes, and `RouteSidewalkRule` and `RouteGroundRule` refuse it
  where it would cut a route. `tests/test_one_sidewalk_open_rule.gd` holds both.
- **No marble on the route**: `EventDef.stands_off_the_route()` (a row that stands only in alleys,
  or one that stands mid street) is what `UnitFills._in_view()` reads to give a row no share of the
  route's bag; it replaced the bare alleys check. `tests/test_unit_fills.gd` holds that day 8's
  route bag holds none of it and that the route street's fill equals the fill without the row; with
  the old alleys-only check the test fails.
- **A picture and a badge of its own** (`EventDef.Look.CLOSING_ROADBLOCK`, appended to the enum so
  no saved value moves): a lower concrete barrier with a coil of razor wire along its top and
  square red and cream blocks on its face, drawn wall to wall at the width it collides at
  (`art/events/closing_roadblock_{segment,segment_vertical,end}.svg`, the open roadblock's
  canvases, so `EventFootprints.guard_post_offset()` clears it the same way), and a badge, one
  block under the wire (`closing_roadblock_block.svg`), whose silhouette has a row of loops where
  the open one's is flat-topped. The catalogue's two tests that no two rows share a picture or a
  badge silhouette hold it with no exemption.
- **Tests**: `test_events_catalogue.gd` finds the band solid from frontage to frontage on each lane
  of a street of each axis; `test_city_map_kerb.gd` holds `street_middle_beside()`;
  `test_body_stance.gd` gains the middle case; the spread-fits-its-ground test admits the half
  street for a row that stands mid street. `test_checkpoints.gd`'s door-gap sweep reads each plan's
  `body_at()`, as `DoorClearanceRule` does: it read `placed_at()`, a lane point where nothing
  stands, and failed on a closing roadblock whose body stood clear of the door.
