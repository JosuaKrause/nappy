# M199 — The closing roadblock stands on the middle of its street and covers all of it · 2026-11-25

**The ask.** "close the street fully" (PLAYTEST-135, statement 7), and "there are two kinds of
roadblocks. one that is open and one that is closing the street" (inbox #1491 in silky-lemur). The
open kind is downy-llama's; this builds the closing kind beside it.

## What was built

- **A row of its own, `closing_roadblock`**, built from the open row's draft
  (`EventCatalogue._roadblock_draft()`): the same barrier, guards, hunt, field and silence, with
  a band reaching `EventCatalogue.CLOSING_ROADBLOCK_REACH` (96px) either way, all 192px of the
  street. A row (not a width keyed on where a roadblock stands) so a save, the run log and a plan
  all name it, and every rule that reads a row's band reads it.
- **It stands on the middle of its street**: `EventDraft.closing_its_street()` writes it and `EventDef.stands_mid_street()` answers, beside
  `stands_at_its_kerb()` (which no longer holds for it), `CityMap.street_middle_beside()` beside
  `kerb_beside()` (both through one private helper), asked by `BodyStance.stands_without_a_map()`,
  the one place that decides where a body stands, so a plan's `body_at()`, the live body, its
  look and the guards' posts all follow.
- **The rules need no change**: read at its middle with a 96px band, `OneSidewalkOpenRule`
  refuses it on every street of her routes (it closes both sidewalks where one could stay open),
  and `RouteSidewalkRule` and `RouteGroundRule` read the same body. It stands on streets beside
  and off her routes. `tests/test_one_sidewalk_open_rule.gd` holds both.
- **Tests**: `test_events_catalogue.gd` finds the band solid from frontage to frontage on each lane
  of a street of each axis; `test_city_map_kerb.gd` holds `street_middle_beside()`;
  `test_body_stance.gd` gains the middle case; the spread-fits-its-ground test admits the half
  street for a row that stands mid street.

## Open to the player's overturn

- **Which roadblocks are the closing kind, and how many**: a catalogue row drawn from day 7 beside
  the open one, weight 1.0 against its 2.0 and at most 4 a day, on carriageway lanes only. The open
  kind keeps day 13's roadblock phase, the task's swap, the escape's roadblock and the region wall.
- **Where its two guards stand**: as the open row's do, in the road on either side of the band at
  the street's middle (`EventFootprints.guard_post_offset()`), not on a sidewalk as the region wall's
  do. Not shown to the player with pictures first.
- **Its picture**: the open row's, at the width it collides at; the two catalogue tests that no two
  rows share a picture or a badge silhouette exempt `stands_mid_street()` rows.

## Rejected

- **A variant of the open row under the same id** (like a heated copy): a plan saved and restored
  by id would lose it.
- **A new look and badge for it**: the same concrete at a wider band reads as the same thing.
