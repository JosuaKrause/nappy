# minty-quail — The crowd and the visible view handed what they read; event_pictures' stale names (shared-components D1) · 2026-10-31


minty-quail's `shared-components.md`, its sixth PR (PR #889) under the approved plan, after G
(minty-quail-31). The item: the carry-over paragraph the orchestrating session wrote rather than the
player. The crowd (simulation) read the view layer's `DrawnView` and `ScreenOrientation` through
`CrowdView`, the frame record's statics through `CrowdTiming`, and found her through the `player` group;
`VisibleView`, on the world layer since minty-quail-29, read `TouchControls`, `HelpText`, `ControlsMode`
and `ScreenOrientation`; and comments in `src/events/` named crowd members the crowd's split had moved.
The layering rules are the player's (minty-lemur #785, #794): a component is handed what it needs, and
a layer never depends upward.

**Built.**
- `CrowdView` is the crowd's own interface (`drawn_box()`, `camera_square()`, the `beyond_every_view`
  rule); `ScreenCrowdView` (`src/ui/`) answers it from the viewport, `DrawnView` and `ScreenOrientation`.
  `Main` owns it and sets `city.crowd_view`, and `City.build()` hands it to the crowd. A city built with
  no view (a bare test city) draws every agent and uses the day's out-of-sight radius.
- Handed an interface asked at the moment of use rather than a `Rect2` pushed each frame, as the brief
  suggested (the builder's choice, open to overturn): the camera updates at its own place in the tree's
  order and `DrawnView` snapshots at the first agent's process, so a pushed value could be a frame stale
  for some agents and shift when they redraw.
- `CrowdTiming` is the null sink; `FrameRecordTiming` (`src/telemetry/`) forwards to the same bucket and
  draw counter, so the frame record is unchanged.
- `Crowd.use_player()` replaces the `player` group lookup; `Main._put_her_in()` calls it.
- `VisibleView` is handed a `ScreenCover` (`src/world/screen_cover.gd`: the covered corners, the design
  size, whether the cover is in force), which `TouchControls.covering` keeps current and `Main` hands to
  each `EventManager` (`use_controls()`), from three places since the first world is put in before the
  touch controls exist. `look()`, `around()` and `visible_share()` take a cover, null meaning the tap
  scheme's whole view, in place of the joystick flag; `covered_left()` and `covered_right()` moved to
  `TouchControls`. Nothing under `src/crowd/` or `src/world/` names a UI, telemetry or group class.
- `event_pictures.gd` names `WalkerLook.VIEW_BY_SECTOR` and `CarLook.CANVAS_BOTTOM_MARGIN`.
- Skill fix: the **telemetry** skill said the crowd reads the frame record through a `CrowdTiming` that
  wraps the statics, and now says the crowd is handed the sink.
