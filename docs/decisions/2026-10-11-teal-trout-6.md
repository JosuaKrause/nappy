# teal-trout — Every charger that does not arrive charging warns first, by radius · 2026-10-17

The player (inbox #764): "Van guardsmen that are about to charge should hold up their hands. It's
one simple rule of an event doesn't spawn already charging it always needs a warning. If it will
never charge it won't need a warning." Told a warning ended on a timer: "Warnings are a different
radius not time", then "A warning happens when the player comes close. They can turn around. If
they keep getting closer the charging starts. There are exceptions like van and police ie events
that spawn with the sole purpose of charging", "There it should be an indicator that they are
about to charge", "With the same heads-up as off screen chargers", and, confirming the reading,
"Yes 1s. Only change the robber while slowly walking will stop if the player gets out of the
radius".

**Built (PR #770).** Three kinds of charger, each documented in `docs/EVENTS.md`, "Every charger
that does not arrive charging warns first":

- **One that waits for her** (the waiting dog from day 4, the alley robber and every guard drawn as
  him, the hunting roadblock's guard): it warns while she is inside its notice distance
  (`pursues_within`) and charges only when she crosses its stand-off; there is no notice clock
  (`EventInstance.warns_by_radius()`). Back outside the radius it waits again; the robber stops
  where he is, the roadblock's guard goes back to his band. Walking or running away from a warning
  is never charged; walking in still meets the charge at the full stand-off.
- **One that appears only to charge** (the man the patrol car and the vans let out): a raised hand
  for `Tuning.WARNING_ALONE_MAX` (1.0s, was 1.8s), standing, then the charge whatever she does. New
  pictures: `art/events/policeman_alert_*`, five views, for the policeman through day 8.
- **One that arrives charging** (the guardsman a torn poster sends, the robber a task sends):
  unchanged, warned of by its badge.

**The forks, answered** (inbox #764):

- **The checkpoint guard**, who steps out of a hut when she walks under a raised boom: "when the
  player approaches the boom gate the guard should raise the hand (warning radius is around the
  boom gate not the guard) and when the player actually goes under it should immediately start
  charging". The guard at the hut across the road raises his hand while she is within 128px of a
  raised boom (or of an alley's post); going under, he charges at once (`EventDef.charges_at_once`).
  **The pursuit fairness contract does not hold for him, by the player's choice**: from the hut
  across the road (about 80px) walking on is caught at 0.43s and standing at 0.45s; going under at
  a run escapes, and so does turning to run within 0.3s. At an alley's post he stands beside her,
  so only the warning answers the charge.
- **Day 13's task guard**: "yes, we can do a 1s on screen and 1s off-screen warning if they were
  not visible". In her view, 1.0s raised hand then the charge; out of view, retired unseen and sent
  at her with a 1.0s badge (`EventCatalogue.task_guard_sent()`).
- **The masked man on the escape's stairs**: "player should have to run to the door". He warns
  while she is in the stairwell and runs his line once she is within 150px (`EventDef.charges_within`),
  with a 1.0s warning first if he appears that close. Measured on the stairwell as built: from 90
  to 150px a walker turning back for the first floor's door is caught and a runner gets through;
  from 155px a walker gets through too.
- **The robber just inside an alley**, who cannot see a walker passing the mouth: the player said
  that is meant ("we specifically made it so"), so it stays.

**GoatCounter's charging counters** (inbox #764: "Dog-chased implies charge. Add a new warning
level for those event telemetries", "Ending a day and a charging counter are not the same thing",
"Charging is independent of an ended day"): every charger sends `nappy-day-N-<row>-warned`,
`-chased` at its charge, then exactly one of `-caught`, `-shaken` or `-outlasted`; a catch sends
`-caught` whether or not the day ends (`EventBus.pursuit_caught`). The dog keeps its `dog-*` names.
Told the day number does not tell the tutorial dog apart ("No it doesn't"; `charging_dog`'s
`max_per_day` 3 applies on day 3): the first dog made on day 3 is the lesson's
(`EventInstance.is_the_lesson`) and sends `dog-tutorial-*` instead. The masked man has no counter,
since he runs a line rather than chasing her.

**Verified:** `check.sh`, `lint.sh` and every charger suite locally (0 failures), and CI on the
merge with `main`. No in-game capture of the hut guard's hand, day 13's guard or the masked man's
warning: a raised boom needs live traffic a scene recipe cannot time, and recipes cannot stage the
escape building; the tests pin when each shows.

**Choices open to overturn:** the masked man draws the guard's own `guard_alert_*` rather than a
picture of his own; a warning ends on distance only, not when the line of sight is lost; running
counts toward shaking a charge off from the charge; the roadblock guard's radius is measured from
his band's centre; the boom's warning radius is 128px and the hand is raised at the hut across the
road; GoatCounter's `dog-chased` fires at the charge, not the warning.
