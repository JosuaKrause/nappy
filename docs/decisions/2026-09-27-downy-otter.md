**Built (PR #414).** Day 9's mark reads "Cross at this district door. See if they let you through."

**The sealed alley was not reproduced.** Mark placement, at dawn and on every relocation, draws only
from alleys reachable from home with the day's closures, seals and bodies counted. The test now also
checks each relocation target against a plain walk from the doorstep around all of them; that check
fails if the relocation's reachability refusals are removed. A probe found no unreachable mark in 48
dawn marks and 20,661 relocation targets. Most dawn marks on days 9 to 13 are reached only through a
district door, which may be what read as sealed; a door always lets her through after its hold, so
it is a path and such a mark stays, as the player's answer allows. The review item for M213 asks the
player to watch for a mark with no path to it.
