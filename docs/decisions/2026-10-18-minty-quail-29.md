# minty-quail — SpawnSiting with named rules, Navigator.reaches, one warned arrival (shared-components B1) · 2026-10-31


minty-quail's `shared-components.md`, its third PR (PR #886) under the approved plan, after the excitement
source (minty-quail-27) and the walk field (minty-quail-28). The item: placement had about 25 entry
points in four classes (about fifty by the time this PR read the code), with self-declared copies: `EventsAhead._where_the_sent_pursuer_starts` copied the
director's arrival search without its refusal of any start on the camera's whole view;
`EventsAhead._a_clear_run_at` folded the boundary into "a clear run" where the director refused a start
through the boundary even as its fallback; the warned-arrival closures were repeated between
`send_a_pursuer` and `_send_at_her`; `_his_walk_reaches_her` simulated the chase step by step. The plan's
pick F2, the orchestrating session's, open to overturn: the poster tear's pursuer takes the director's full
rules, a change to play measured here.

**Built.**
- **`SpawnSiting`** (`src/world/spawn_siting.gd`) decides where something sent at her may start. Its
  rules are named (`SpawnSiting.Rule`, asked through `allows()`/`allows_every()`): legal ground (static
  `is_legal_ground()`), out of sight, off the camera's whole view, clear of the region's wall and door
  bodies, a clear run, reaches her. One search, `start_out_of_sight(ask, bearings)`, takes the caller's
  bearing list, so the director keeps its random draws and the poster its hash; `across_the_street(ask)`
  finds the start opposite a front door. `PendingWarning`'s sight statics and the director's
  `beside_distance` moved in, taking the drawing's box and a `Mover`. "Registered" is read as a named enum
  each search asks in a stated list, not a runtime registry (open to overturn).
- The city builds it (`City.spawn_siting`) from its map, navigator, the events' view and
  `City.siting_region()`, which hands the walled alleys and each wall and door body as plain rects and
  discs, so the world layer holds no region plan; it is handed to `EventsAhead` through
  `EventManager.setup()` and to `ResistanceDirector` through `setup()`. It takes a `CityMap`, since legal
  ground needs the held streets, the home block and the walled alleys.
- **`Navigator.reaches(from, to, mover, limit, avoid)`** walks the straight chase, sliding along walls,
  replacing `_his_walk_reaches_her`.
- **`WarningBoard.send_from_out_of_sight(def, her, search, arrive)`** is the one warned arrival both
  senders call.
- `VisibleView` moved to `src/world/`; it still reads four view classes for what the controls cover,
  carried to the next PR that hands the crowd and the events plain screen values.
- Removed with no forwarders: the director's `is_legal_ground`, `beside_distance`,
  `_runs_through_the_boundary`, `_a_clear_run`, `_boundary_bodies_near`, `_across_the_street`,
  `_his_walk_reaches_her`, `WALK_PROBE_STEP`, `_view_from` and `_navigator`; `EventsAhead._a_clear_run_at`;
  the four `PendingWarning` statics. The director keeps `set_sight()`'s on-screen answer for the
  placements the next PR moves.
- New suite `tests/test_spawn_siting.gd`; the events skill's arrival sentence names `SpawnSiting`.

**The change to play (F2), measured.** Over 40 seeds × days 4–14, five tears a day (2,200 tears), no
poster pursuer's start moved, and 39 tears (1.8%, none before) now send nobody, all on region-wall days
(day 9: 7, 10: 9, 11: 3, 12: 5, 13: 9, 14: 6): each could start him only on a run through a wall or door
body, which the old poster rule took as its fallback and the director's rule refuses. The camera-view rule
refused no poster start in the sample. For the remeasuring step: those tears, and the run-log line for a
tear that sends nobody now covering the boundary refusal.
