# M159 — The crowd's physics step at half the cost · built 2026-10-04

*([jolly-trout](../playtests/2026-10-04-jolly-trout.md), #542: "Also what about the physics twice
per frame? Should we fix that?", then "Sure we can look into making the tick cheaper".)* The
phone's frame record ([m159-phone-frame-record-2026-10-04](../evidence/m159-phone-frame-record-2026-10-04/README.md))
put an extra 30/s physics tick at about 3.8ms, the crowd's share about 1.7ms.

**What was built** (PR #549). `tests/probes/m159_crowd_tick_cost.gd` times the real
`Crowd._physics_process()` whole and split by call, on seeds 478156010 and 4242, days 1 and 9, and
hashes every agent's position and speed after every tick so two revisions can be compared tick for
tick. On day 1 the most expensive calls were the junction negotiation, the checkpoint-door pass
(which does nothing on a day with no hut), the player half and the queue resolve. The changes, in
`src/crowd/crowd.gd` and `src/crowd/crowd_agent.gd`: the door pass returns at once on a day with no
checkpoint hut; the player half skips a walker more than 116px from her (the 96px make-way distance,
the 19px contact release and 1px), clearing its contact flag as the skipped calls would; loops over
cars only or walkers only walk lists rebuilt from the agent list each frame; the junction
negotiation reads each car's distance and axis once and `junction_occupied()` builds no array.

**Measured** (headless, desktop, three alternating rounds; `docs/evidence/m159-cheaper-tick-2026-10-04/`):
median crowd tick on day 1 from about 870µs to 400 to 445µs, about half; on day 9 down 16 to 20% by
median, with the rounds overlapping (in one round on seed 478156010 every after run was slower than
that round's before runs).
Every one of the 144 runs ends on the same position-and-speed hash before and after, so no agent
moves differently on any tick of the probe's walk. That walk never touches a walker or draws a
horn, so the make-way and bump calls the change skips for a walker past 116px (the cars' strike and
horn checks are untouched) were checked by a variant the PR's review wrote and did not commit, which
drops her onto an agent every 20 ticks and matched there too
([the review](https://github.com/JosuaKrause/nappy/pull/549#pullrequestreview-5407683243)). These are desktop times; whether the phone's two-tick frames get
shorter is for the next phone frame record.

**Proposed, not asked for, and open to overturn:** the seeds, days and the fixed walk the probe's
player follows; parity checked by the evidence's `analyse.py` across revisions rather than by a
suite test; `Crowd._meet_the_player()` and `Crowd._book_the_turns()` extracted so the probe times
the real calls; the car and walker lists rebuilt every frame rather than kept, since suites add
agents straight to the agent list.
