# minty-quail — Three suites drive public seams: the ground floor, the car views and the walker views · 2026-11-01


minty-quail's `tests-public-seams.md`, slice 2: `tests/test_ground_floor.gd`, `tests/test_car_views.gd` and
`tests/test_walker_views.gd`. The player allows the item in slices alongside the other steps "as long as the final
result is the same" (inbox #813 in curly-moose); the item is done suite by suite. The item's rule for a seam is that
the game itself can use it: a public member only a test reads is not a seam, however it is named.

**Counts, before -> after.** Private reads are the lines `rg -v '^\s*#' <file> | rg -o '\._[a-z]\w*'` finds; direct
frame calls are `rg -v '^\s*#' <file> | rg -c '\._(physics_)?process\('`.

**Built.**
- `Building`'s queries that `_draw()`, `City` and the posters already ask of a facade are public, under the names
  they had without the underscore: `draws_window_at`, `column_under_door`, `ground_floor_texture`, `is_covered`,
  `extension_rows`, `extension_is_seamless`, `portico_is_drawn`, `has_visible_storefront`, `entrance_door_texture`,
  `civic_entrance_cols`, `hall_cols`, `window_texture`. `City.assign_roof_extensions()` is public too, since
  `City.build()` calls it and the suite hands it a hand-made lot list. Their callers in `city.gd`,
  `test_city_decay.gd`, `test_acts.gd` and `tests/probes/lazy_scenery_measure.gd` follow the rename, and so do the
  mentions in `docs/CITY.md` and `docs/GRAPHICS.md`.
- Two pure rolls of `Building` are static and public, so a test can replay them against the same stream:
  `roll_fire_escape_extras()` and `door_column_from()`.
- `Building.ground_cell(col)` is the ground-floor cell of a column, which `blank_ground_floor_cells()` (read by
  `PosterWalls`) and `_cell()`'s other callers build their rect from. A test finds a blank cell's column with it.
- `Building.fire_escape_texture(col, row)` answers `&""` where no balcony was rolled for that column and row, the way
  `ground_floor_texture()` answers `&""` for a column nothing is drawn in. `_draw_fire_escape()` already asks it for
  every balcony it draws, and asks only about balconies that exist, so the game's drawing does not change. It is the
  seam the suite reads a front's escapes through: which columns carry one, and whether a pot was rolled for a balcony
  (a pot or a plain balcony both draw a picture; only an unrolled one answers nothing).
- `LaneMover.take_the_turn(turn, run_up)` and `drop_the_turn()` are the one place a car commits to a turn and gives it
  up. `CarStrategy` calls them instead of writing the mover's two turn fields, and `_choose_lane()` and
  `_enter_at_a_stretch_end()` drop the turn through the second. A suite that wants a car on an arc hands it the arc.
- `LaneMover.end_the_frame(delta) -> bool` is public (`CrowdAgent` answers it): the tick's end, and whether the agent
  asked for a redraw. The suites read the redraw gate through its answer. `tests/test_drawn_view.gd` and
  `tests/test_event_redraw.gd` follow the rename.
- `tests/view_rig.gd` is the test helper the two view suites share: one generated city, crowd agents placed by the game's
  own `setup()` and `setup_at()` on its lanes (a suite asking for a heading rolls placements until one points that
  way), and a `WalkerLook` or a `CarLook` over a placed agent.
- No other public member was added. `CrowdAgent.look` stays private (`_look`), and `EntityHalo` is unchanged.

**How a suite reads what an agent would draw.** `WalkerLook` and `CarLook` are public classes a look-reader builds with
`new(agent)`; `AgentLook`'s doc says a look reads the agent and never writes it, holding only the state a picture alone
keeps (the sector last drawn, the stride, the ground travelled). A suite builds its own look over a placed agent and
drives it with the agent's own `velocity()`; the agent is stopped (`step_aside()`, `crashed_into()`), turned
(`take_the_turn()`) or placed again (`setup()`) through its public API, and the look reads the result. Where the
agent's own gate is the subject (`end_the_frame()`), the suite advances the agent's picture with
`advance_the_picture()`, the call the crowd's tick makes, and the look beside it with the same velocity and tick; both
are asked once per tick in the same order, so they hold the same sector.

**Rejected.**
- A public `CrowdAgent.look` (the first version of this change): no game code reads it from outside the class, and the
  suites wrote through it (`look.view`, `look.travelled`, `look.gait_phase`), the shape the item names a defect in
  `SceneRecipeRuntime.walk_spec`.
- `EntityHalo.lift()` and `traced_body()` (the first version): the only reader was a test comparing a callable with the
  look's own method, which is a private field reached through a public getter. Headless runs never call `_draw()`, so
  what the halo asks its owner for is not observable without a draw pass (see below).
- `Building.storefront_rolls()`, `door_column_rolled()`, `fire_escape_columns()`, `fire_escape_has_pot()` and
  `roof_furniture()` (the first version): no game reader outside the tests. Each fact is read where the game shows it
  instead (the parity table).
- Counting pots through a `fire_escape_has_pot` that answers `false` for an unrolled balcony: it cannot tell a pot
  stream that ran and came out all plain from one that never ran, which the old `_fire_escape_pots.is_empty()` could.
- A test-only accessor for each private field: the nodes the game draws (`RoofObject`s) and the textures it asks for
  are the public structure.

**Parity: old check -> new check.** `test_ground_floor.gd` keeps its 36 tests and their names. How a fact is read:

| Fact | Old read | New read |
|---|---|---|
| a commercial front has one complete storefront pair | `_storefront_variant.size() == 1` | the second column of the pair draws nothing, the first a storefront, the odd last column plain wall |
| the fire escapes of a front (column, count, gap) | `_fire_escape_cols`, in rolled order | the columns whose first-floor balcony has a picture (`fire_escape_texture(col, 1)`), ascending; an expected list from `roll_fire_escape_extras()` is sorted before it is compared, so the order the escapes were rolled in is no longer checked (drawing order between two escapes four columns apart shows nothing) |
| the pot rolls, whole | `_fire_escape_pots == expected_pots` | every column and every floor line from the ground's to one past the top's is asked, and the pictures read back as `{col: {row: pot}}`, compared whole with the replay: a balcony rolled for a column or row outside the expected ones fails it |
| a home building, a two-story front and a covered front roll no pot stream | `_fire_escape_pots.is_empty()` | no column and no floor line has a balcony picture at all (`_rolled_balconies() == 0`), which an all-plain pot roll fails as the old check did |
| the home flag and coverage move no storefront roll | `_storefront_variant/_awning/_shutter_severity` equal | the whole ground floor drawn on every day of the run is equal: the picture of each pair shows its variant and its awning, and the day it shutters shows its fixed severity. The coverage test covers the first pair only and compares what is drawn after it, because a fully covered front draws nothing to compare; a covered pair's own roll is not read, but a roll it skipped would move every pair after it |
| the storefront stream replays | the rolls equal the replay | the replayed ground floor equals the drawn one on every day of the run |
| the door stream replays | `_door_col == replay` | `entrance_door_col()` equals the replay, on residential fronts; on a commercial front, whose door is unused while a storefront draws, the check is made on the same front with its odd columns covered, so no pair draws and the door shows |
| roof furniture (late extension, shallow roof, pool, lip rule, main's layout) | `_roof_furniture` entries (cell, span, kind) | the `RoofObject`s the building stands on its roof (the nodes the game draws), read back as cell, span and picture from where each stands; `main`'s pinned layout is by picture (`WATER_TANK`, `SERVICE_BULKHEAD`, `EXHAUST_FAN`) in place of the internal kind number |
| the cell of a column | `_cell(col, 0)` | `ground_cell(col)` |

`test_walker_views.gd`: the selector, table and SVG checks are unchanged (they touch no agent). Walker checks run on a
walker placed on a lane heading (`setup()`) and, for a diagonal, sidestepped (`step_aside()`) so that its heading is
the swing the game gives a walker nudged aside; a new check asserts the heading is within 22 degrees of the sector's
centre. A stopped walker is stopped by `step_aside()` along its own line of travel. Fresh heading clears the hold:
placed again by `setup()` until it points north. Gait: the same checks, through a look over the walker.

**Checks dropped or narrowed, and why.**
- The two halo wiring checks. `CrowdAgent._build_rim()` builds the halo with the look's `draw_body` and `bob`; that the
  halo holds them is observable only in a draw pass, and headless runs never call `_draw()`. A public getter on the halo
  for a test was the only way left, and is the accessor the item forbids. The wiring is one line, and each end is held:
  `tests/test_halo.gd` holds the ring a given bob and body produce, this suite the bob and the picture of a car.
- The covered-storefront roll of the covered pair itself, as said in the table. Nothing the game does separates it.
- The order the escapes were rolled in.

**Needs remeasuring.** Nothing is measured per step (the entry's README). The change adds a method call where
`CarStrategy` wrote two fields (once per committed turn, not per frame) and renames `_end_the_frame` and the
`Building` queries without changing them; none of it runs per frame beyond what already did, so the crowd tick measured
for the mover base (`minty-quail-16`) is not expected to move. The final remeasuring step reads it with the rest.
