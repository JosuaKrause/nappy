# M213 — The chalk mark's robber stands at the far end of its alley · built 2026-09-27

*([PLAYTEST-142](../playtests/PLAYTEST-142.md), statement 5: "Let's always place the river at the
other end of the alley"; [PLAYTEST-144](../playtests/PLAYTEST-144.md), statement 11: "It's fine if
the Robert doesn't get triggered every time")*

**Built (PR #414).** The guard a chalk mark sets is drawn on a bearing toward the alley's far mouth
from the mark (`ResistanceDirector._far_alley_mouth()`, from `CityMap.alley_rects`) rather than
anywhere round it, so walking in from the mark's own end stays outside his `pursues_within`.
Tests over six cities check the bearing and that a bare guard walked in from the near end and back
does not wake at least once. A guard on an alley `CityMap` does not track keeps the old full-circle
draw; that is the agent's choice, open to overturn.
