# M213 — The chalk mark's robber stands at the far end of its alley · built 2026-09-27

*([PLAYTEST-142](../playtests/PLAYTEST-142.md), statement 5: "Let's always place the river at the
other end of the alley"; [PLAYTEST-144](../playtests/PLAYTEST-144.md), statement 11: "It's fine if
the Robert doesn't get triggered every time"; [crisp-moose](../playtests/2026-09-27-crisp-moose.md),
statement 3: "robber at inner end of the courtyard is fine. I encountered it in game and it worked
well for me. you just have to lure the robber out first.")*

**Built (PR #414).** In a through-alley the guard a mark sets stands at the alley's far end from the
mark: on the far end tile, or up to three tiles in where the alley leaves room, and never nearer the
mark than 176px, his 140px `pursues_within` plus the 36px touch reach. So a walk in from the mark's
own end, a touch and a walk back out never wakes him. In a courtyard passage he stands at the
courtyard's inner end, the courtyard tile farthest from the mark, never within 66px of it (his 30px
catch plus the touch reach): a touch may wake him, and she lures him out. He is the one thing the
day places on unused calm ground, which events otherwise leave alone
(`EventScheduler._calm_to_leave_alone`); the player approved that spot.

**Measured** over six cities with a probe that walks in from the street to 34px short of the mark
and back: he stays asleep on 38% of through-alley marks and 29% of passage marks with the old
whole-circle band, 47% and 54% with the PR's first pass (a bearing toward the far end inside the old
band), and on every mark now. The test walks every through-alley mark through the placement the
game uses and fails with the old band wired back in; a passage mark's test checks the robber stands
at the courtyard's inner end and outside his catch of every touch, and its asleep rate is the
probe's.

**Tried and rejected:** leaning only the bearing toward the far end, which the review measured still
waking him on most marks; keeping marks out of courtyard passages, which the player overturned.
