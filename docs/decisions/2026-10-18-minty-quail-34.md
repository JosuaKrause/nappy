# minty-quail — Every mover's move proposed, checked, then committed; day 12's tape waits for a person still standing (shared-components C2a) · 2026-10-31


minty-quail's `shared-components.md`, its eighth PR (PR #891) under the approved plan, after C1
(minty-quail-33). Two items: the carry-over from events-composed (minty-quail-26), movement proposed,
validated, then committed for every mover as a car's frame was (minty-quail-17), which C2b's separation
pass over every mover needs (the player's answer on who gives way, inbox #884); and the one bullet of
`likely-bugs.md`, day 12's tape going up through a person not yet sent off (the review of PR #840),
whose file goes with it.

**Built.**
- `MoveProposal` (`src/world/`) is one mover's move for one tick, owned by every event, crowd body and
  cordon officer; `ProposedMoves` (`src/world/`, held as `WorldMap.moves`, beside the map's record of what
  is solid) is the map's one list of every mover's move, which a mover joins on entering the world and
  leaves on going, holding no reference to a mover.
- `EventInstance.step()` proposes, ticks, then commits. The car's `begin_frame()`/`end_frame()` became
  `note_the_pose()`/`check()`, the car's own check, and a car's route end is acted on only once its pose
  is committed. A figure is still checked by the navigator as it moves, so its proposal reaches the commit
  already checked and it acts on its route end at once.
- `LaneMover` proposes its along and cross step, keeps it off a tile the body may not stand on
  (`_keep_out_of_a_body()`), then commits. Each cordon officer's walk is proposed, walked through the
  navigator, then committed before he is drawn. Catches are checked after every event has committed.
- Left as the builder chose where the design was silent, open to overturn: the list on the map; each
  mover owning its move, so a rig can step an instance out of the tree; C2b's point of adjustment per
  mover, between the proposal and the mover's own check; a move holding a position only; no move of
  their own for the roadblock's far guard, the flock's birds and a crowd car following a turn's arc.
- Open for C2b: whether its pass runs at each mover's commit, which this PR supports as it stands, or
  the event and crowd loops split into propose all, pass, commit all, which changes play, since movers
  read each other's poses within a tick.
- Day 12's tape: the cordon's list of people it waits for (`ParkCordon._walkers()`) also holds each
  escort's person while he stands waiting, until he is sent off, so no stretch goes up through him.
  `test_park_cordon_seeds` checks it and, on main's code, failed 78 of 1,397 checks, every one this
  check: buskers in the three edge rows on many seeds, never the inner spot, 153 stretches in all.
- Skill fixes: **events** said only a car's frame is proposed, checked and committed, and now says every
  event's step is, the car's check its own; **crowd-traffic** named `CrowdAgent._keep_out_of_a_body()`
  holding the step the agent started the frame on, and now names `LaneMover`'s, holding the proposed step
  before the commit.
