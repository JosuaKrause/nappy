**What was found first.** The entry azure-squirrel (record `2026-11-17-azure-squirrel`) expected
a straight chase to stop at the buildings where a walker turns the corner. On the escape's real
streets it does not: the escape roadblock rig, made to turn the walker round the first corner she
comes to once he charges (the building she walked beside ending at a cross street or an alley mouth

corner with him far enough behind: his slide keeps only the sliver of his step that runs along the
face, and from round a narrow building's far side the slide points away from her. A hand-drawn
street that ends at a corner, round a building two tiles wide, shows it: at main he loses her on
all three walks of the new finale case.
