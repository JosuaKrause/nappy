# minty-quail — The crowd yields to event figures (shared-components C2b) · 2026-11-01


minty-quail's `shared-components.md`, its ninth PR (PR #895) under the approved plan, after C2a
(minty-quail-34): movers did not see each other, event figures passing through walkers and cars and
back. Asked who gives way, the player: "(a) the crowd yields. a charging dog doesn't care but a
pedestrian doesn't want to get overrun. same applies to other events." (plaid-raven #884). Read as:
walkers step aside or are pushed and cars stand for an event figure, while every event figure keeps its
course and timing.

**Built.**
- `SeparationPass` (`src/world/separation_pass.gd`, `WorldMap.separation`), gathered once a crowd tick
  in `Crowd._advance_the_world()`: the movers given way to are the moves that do not yield, have a body
  and have moved since the pass first saw them. Each crowd body asks it at its own step, between its
  proposal and its check (`LaneStrategy.give_way_on_the_step()`), the seam minty-quail-34 left; the
  loops are not split into propose all, pass, commit all (the orchestrating session's pick for the fork
  that record left, open to overturn).
- Walkers: `Crowd._make_way()` moved to `WalkerStrategy.make_way_for()`, still run for her and now for
  every figure; the proposed step is pushed clear of each figure's body and the walker keeps going the
  way he was pushed. Squeezed against a wall, an overtaken walker is moved on; one met head on walks on
  and the figure brushes past, the one overlap the design allows. Walkers are not startled.
- Cars brake for a figure in their lane ahead, or coming into it within 15 ticks, and hold their step
  short of its body; they never swerve, and stand only for figures that moved within the last 30
  ticks (this limit was later removed, see the second round). Across the lane a figure's reach counts for at most `Tuning.CAR_STRIKE_HALF_WIDTH`, since
  without the cap event cars in the next lane stalled day 13's junctions for up to 40s. A figure beyond
  a junction box counts as a stopped car, so a car that can still stop holds at the line instead of
  entering a box it would stand in; a car already within its braking distance of the box counts as in
  it and is not asked to hold, and one already in the box stands there for a figure that comes into
  its lane after it entered.
- Cordon officers are given way to like any figure.
- Left as the builder chose, open to overturn: only movers that have moved count (a standing object
  or vehicle is now walked round, see the second round); the 15-tick look-ahead,
  the 6px standing gap and the lateral cap; walkers not startled; officers given way to.
- `LaneMover.setup()` never set its move's radius, so every ordinary walker and car carried 0; both
  setups now take the shape.
- Two scene recipes re-timed for the new crowd: `task-09-crossing.json`'s crowd check from tick 370 to
  385, and `task-10-neighbor.json`'s from 342 to 320 with its walkers lowered from 2 to 1, a weaker
  check, since no tick of the new run has two walkers and a car moving in its picture together (open to
  overturn).
- `SceneRecipeRuntime`'s hand walk of a held leaving event places its move after each step, keeping
  minty-quail-34's rule that a move stands where its mover does.
- Skill addition: **crowd-traffic** says only the crowd moves between it and an event figure.

**Second round (review findings, PR #895).** The player answered what the first round left open.
Asked about a figure waiting on the map: "if it's people or dogs it's fine. if it's a restaurant or
car they should avoid" (inbox #905). Asked about a car behind a figure that will not move: "cars
should initiate a turn around if they stand behind a figure that won't move" (inbox #909), read by
the orchestrator as: wait behind a figure that is only stopping, turn round behind one that has
parked.
- Walkers: `EventDef.stands_as` (SOMEBODY, SOMETHING, NOTHING), carried as `MoveProposal.stood_round`.
  A walker gives way to a figure that moved in the last `SeparationPass.UNDER_WAY_TICKS` (10) ticks
  and to a standing object or vehicle, and may walk through a standing person or animal, a dog waiting
  or crouched before its charge included.
- Cars: `EventDef.when_it_stops` (PARKS, GOES_ON), carried as `MoveProposal.parked`; a row that drives
  to where it stops parks only once there (`Phase.PARKED`). The 30-tick limit is gone: a car stands for
  a figure ahead in its lane and never drives through it, and turns round behind a parked one
  (`divert()`). At a junction it holds at the stop line for a figure standing past the box. Rows that
  park where it matters: `fire_truck` and the neighbor's walk home.
- Mover list: `EventInstance.take_out()` leaves the list of moves before freeing, used by
  `retire_finished()`, `stream_out()` and `clear()`, which the first round's probes needed (finished
  events stayed as ghost figures); `ProposedMoves.keeping_their_course()` lets `gather()` walk only
  the figures. A step over `SeparationPass.PLACEMENT_STEP` (32px a tick; the cat covers 8) is a
  placement, with no velocity and not walking.
- `test_move_poses` runs day 12 invincible, with a check that the day still reaches the swing, since
  a car ended the day at 21s on a walk crossing roads before the swing at 25s.
- Also open to overturn, beyond the list above: walkers step round a standing object with the same
  give-way they use for a moving figure; the 10-tick window; a walker pushed back steps to the side
  with room (`WalkerStrategy._side_to_step_to()`); a walker squeezed against a wall behind a standing
  object waits; the head-on brush-past stays only for a moving figure; `when_it_stops` overlaps
  `stops_where_it_arrives` and `validate()` checks they agree instead of collapsing them; a car turns
  round only once the event says it has parked; a figure placed that never moved is left to the map's
  solid tiles, so a never-moved standing thing with no solid body is not stood for by cars; a car
  stands only for figures ahead of its centre; a car on a turn's arc stands for nobody; a car brakes
  and holds only along its own lane and never swerves; there is no cap on a car's wait behind a
  GOES_ON figure (no deadlock, no box held); the neighbor's walk-home copy PARKS and her base row
  GOES_ON; a placement keeps counting as standing.

**The events' poses moved on one walk, explained.** With the day's events live, the cadence probe's
`walkers` walk heads her for the nearest walker each tick, so once walkers step aside she walks
elsewhere, and the events stream in and out round her position (`EventStreaming.stream_around()`, in
the world within 900px, out beyond 1160px): on seed 1001 a market stall came in a tick earlier on day 9
and a delivery van stayed on day 1. Replaying base's walk on head, the events' poses are identical in 48
of 48 runs, so nothing in the events reads the crowd. The probe records and replays walks
(`CADENCE_RIG_RECORD`, `CADENCE_RIG_REPLAY`).
