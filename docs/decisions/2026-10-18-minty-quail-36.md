# minty-quail — The crowd yields to event figures (shared-components C2b) · 2026-11-01


minty-quail's `shared-components.md`, its ninth PR (PR #895) under the approved plan, after C2a
(minty-quail-34): movers did not see each other, event figures passing through walkers and cars and
back. Asked who gives way, the player: "(a) the crowd yields. a charging dog doesn't care but a
pedestrian doesn't want to get overrun. same applies to other events." (plaid-raven #884). Read as:
walkers step aside or are pushed and cars stand for an event figure, while every event figure keeps its
course and timing.

**Built.**
- `SeparationPass` (`src/world/separation_pass.gd`, `WorldMap.separation`), gathered once a crowd tick
  in `Crowd._advance_the_world()`: the movers given way to are the moves that do not yield and have a
  body (`ProposedMoves.keeping_their_course()`), with the rules below for which of them a walker and a
  car stand for. Each crowd body asks it at its own step, between its proposal and its check
  (`LaneStrategy.give_way_on_the_step()`), the seam minty-quail-34 left; the loops are not split into
  propose all, pass, commit all (the orchestrating session's pick for the fork that record left, open
  to overturn).
- Walkers: `Crowd._make_way()` moved to `WalkerStrategy.make_way_for()`, still run for her and now for
  every figure; the proposed step is pushed clear of each figure's body and the walker keeps going the
  way he was pushed. A walker gives way to a figure that moved in the last
  `SeparationPass.UNDER_WAY_TICKS` (10) ticks and to a standing object or vehicle
  (`EventDef.stands_as`, carried as `MoveProposal.stood_round`), and may walk through a standing person
  or animal, a dog waiting or crouched before its charge included. Squeezed against a wall, an
  overtaken walker is moved on; one met head on walks on and the figure brushes past, the one overlap
  the design allows. Walkers are not startled.
- Cars brake for a figure in their lane ahead, or coming into it within 15 ticks, and hold their step
  short of its body; they never swerve and never drive through a figure, however long it has stood.
  Behind a figure that has parked (`EventDef.when_it_stops`, carried as `MoveProposal.parked`; a row
  that drives to where it stops parks only once there; the `fire_truck` and the neighbor's walk home
  do) they turn round (`divert()`), behind one that is only stopping they wait. Across the lane a
  figure's reach counts for at most `Tuning.CAR_STRIKE_HALF_WIDTH`, since without the cap event cars in
  the next lane stalled day 13's junctions for up to 40s. A figure beyond a junction box counts as a
  stopped car, so a car that can still stop holds at the line instead of entering a box it would stand
  in; a car already within its braking distance of the box counts as in it and is not asked to hold,
  and one already in the box stands there for a figure that comes into its lane after it entered.
- Cordon officers are given way to like any figure.
- A figure that goes leaves the list of moves at once (`EventInstance.take_out()`, used by
  `retire_finished()`, `stream_out()` and `clear()`), so no finished event stays on it as a ghost
  figure. A step over `SeparationPass.PLACEMENT_STEP` (32px a tick; the cat covers 8) is a placement,
  with no velocity and not walking.
- Left as the builder chose, open to overturn: the 15-tick look-ahead, the 6px standing gap and the
  lateral cap; walkers not startled; officers given way to; the 10-tick window; a walker pushed back
  steps to the side with room (`WalkerStrategy._side_to_step_to()`); a walker squeezed against a wall
  behind a standing object waits; the head-on brush-past stays only for a moving figure;
  `when_it_stops` overlaps `stops_where_it_arrives` and `validate()` checks they agree instead of
  collapsing them; a car turns round only once the event says it has parked; a figure placed that
  never moved is left to the map's solid tiles, so a never-moved standing thing with no solid body is
  not stood for by cars; a car stands only for figures ahead of its centre; a car on a turn's arc
  stands for nobody; a car brakes and holds only along its own lane and never swerves; there is no cap
  on a car's wait behind a figure that is only stopping (no deadlock; a car already in a box when a
  figure stops in its lane after it entered stands there as long as the figure stands, measured at
  1.0 s for the patrol car and 2.1 s at most for any figure); the neighbor's
  walk-home copy parks and her base row does not; a placement keeps counting as standing.
- `LaneMover.setup()` never set its move's radius, so every ordinary walker and car carried 0; both
  setups now take the shape.
- Two scene recipes re-timed for the new crowd: `task-09-crossing.json`'s crowd check from tick 370 to
  385, and `task-10-neighbor.json`'s from 342 to 320 with its walkers lowered from 2 to 1, a weaker
  check, since no tick of the new run has two walkers and a car moving in its picture together (open to
  overturn).
- `SceneRecipeRuntime`'s hand walk of a held leaving event places its move after each step, keeping
  minty-quail-34's rule that a move stands where its mover does.
- Skill addition: **crowd-traffic** says only the crowd moves between it and an event figure.

**The events' poses moved on one walk, explained.** With the day's events live, the cadence probe's
`walkers` walk heads her for the nearest walker each tick, so once walkers step aside she walks
elsewhere, and the events stream in and out round her position (`EventStreaming.stream_around()`, in
the world within 900px, out beyond 1160px): on seed 1001 a market stall came in a tick earlier on day 9
and a delivery van stayed on day 1. Replaying base's walk on head, the events' poses are identical in 48
of 48 runs, so nothing in the events reads the crowd. The probe records and replays walks
(`CADENCE_RIG_RECORD`, `CADENCE_RIG_REPLAY`).
