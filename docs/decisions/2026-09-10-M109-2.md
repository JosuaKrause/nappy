**Built (PR #406).** Both dog families draw their comic PNGs in the game: fifteen pictures for the
normal dog (first step, rest and opposite step in five views) and ten for the pursuing dog, the
west views mirroring the east. `docs/evidence/comic-dogs-2026-09-27/install.py` copies each accepted
candidate byte for byte under `art/illustrated/svg-transfer/events/`, the five third-pose SVGs join
the events atlas page, and a test requires a PNG beside every picture the three dog rows draw.
`EventInstance.dog_picture()` walks the normal dog first step, rest, opposite step, rest, a beat
being a quarter of the gait phase it shares with its walker, so the dog changes picture as the
walker does (545ms at the dog walker's pace against the preview's 450ms). A standing dog shows its
rest pose. Both are the builder's choices, open to overturn.

**The seam.** At the player's request, the normal dog's side opposite step (`dog_c`) loses the black
outline where its lighter hind leg meets the body: a named step, `SEAM` in
`walked-revision-5/assemble.py`, blends four pixels between the body and the leg and refuses to
build if the artwork there is not the expected shape. The darker hind leg keeps its line, the
pursuing dog is untouched, and the rebuild is still byte for byte. It is this one seam, not a
permission to repaint limbs (`.claude/skills/illustrated-png/references/leg-contact-corrections.md`).

**Open.** The five third-pose SVGs still carry the comment "Unbound third pose …; no atlas or
runtime binding", which is no longer true; their bytes are pinned by the evidence's frozen input
manifests, so correcting the comment means re-freezing the recipe. The player has further comments
on the pictures to come.
