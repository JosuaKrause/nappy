# merry-elk — More posters, from the first day they appear · 2026-10-05

Filed from [busy-quail](../playtests/2026-10-04-busy-quail.md) (inbox #563): "the poster density
should be higher -- on the first day with posters it's very hard to find one -- increase the
probability throughout -- even the end doesn't have many posters".

**Built.** `PosterWalls.DAWN_SHARE_ON_ROUTES` went from `[0, .10, .30, .45]` to `[0, .55, .65, .80]`,
`DAWN_SHARE_OFF_ROUTES` from `[0, .06, .08, .12]` to `[0, .30, .35, .40]`, and `ACT_MORNING_SHARE`
(the share up on an act's first morning) from .10 to .25. A worked wall in acts II and III carries
two to four sheets (it was one to two, and one to three); act IV and the uniform sheet are as they
were. The marble bag, the tear draws and the save's rewind of the tears are untouched.
`tests/test_posters.gd` pins the first poster day's floor: on seeds 4242 and 90210, a sheet in view
on at least 45% of a route's steps and at least two sheets on every route.

**Measured** with `tests/probes/merry_elk_poster_density.gd` (8 seeds, every route of every branch
walked cell by cell, the view taken as `Tuning.VIEW_HALF_EXTENT` around the cell, intact sheets only):

| day | route steps with a sheet in view, before → after | distinct sheets one route passes | routes passing fewer than 3 |
|--|--|--|--|
| 4 | 17% → 58% | 2.9 → 20.3 | 52.8% → 0.8% |
| 8 | 43% → 78% | 13.9 → 46.8 | 12.7% → 0% |
| 14 | 72% → 82% | 45.7 → 66.7 | 0% → 0% |

The steps still without a sheet in view are structural: posters go only on blank wall along an
east–west street's north sidewalk, so a higher rate does not reach them.

**What more sheets cost.** A poster crew pastes what the dawn pastes (`_job_for()` shares
`_sheets_for()`), so a crew now puts up two to four sheets. The sheets she can tear by accident are
those on the sidewalk tiles her route crosses; per route they rise several-fold on the early days
and little on the late ones (day 4 from 0.7 to 4.7, day 7 from 1.9 to 9.6, day 14 from 12.4 to
14.4). Each tear draws from the tears' bag, one pursuit marble in ten, so at an assumed one
accidental tear in ten sheets underfoot a walk on day 4 meets a pursuit about 0.05 times (it was
0.007) and the worst day, day 13, about 0.16 (it was 0.11). The share torn by accident is
assumed, not measured; the before-to-after ratio does not depend on it. Not retuned; whether the
early days' rise is acceptable is the player's to judge in a run.

`tests/test_posters.gd` also pins day 14: a sheet in view on at least 78% of a route's steps and at
least ten sheets on every route. Both floors fail at the old rates.
