# minty-quail — The walk field on the world layer, the task guard's way from the Navigator (shared-components D2) · 2026-10-31


minty-quail's `shared-components.md`, its second PR (PR #885) under the approved plan, after the
excitement source (minty-quail-27). The item, a carry-over from events-composed (minty-quail-26): the
day-13 task guard found his way to her through the red arrow's `ArrowField`, a resistance class in
`src/resistance/`, built and owned by the event instance, where the map's one `Navigator` is the shared
pathfinder. The plan's pick F3, open to overturn: only the day-13 task guard paths round buildings
(velvet-stork's proposal); every other pursuer still chases straight.

**Built.**
- **`WalkField`** (`src/world/walk_field.gd`) is `ArrowField` moved whole, the 4-connected sliced
  walking-distance sweep, with no alias; the red arrow, `WorldMap`, `CityMap`'s comments and the tests
  name it.
- **`ChaseWay`** (`src/world/chase_way.gd`) holds the task guard's way to her: the sweeps and their
  500-tile slice, the refresh shares (2 tiles, a quarter of his walk), the 6-tile lookahead, the ground
  laid once per state of what blocks her, and the chain cache. `Navigator.way_to(mover, sight)` makes it
  and the instance holds it as `_chase_way` (null for every other pursuer); `EventPursuit` asks it
  `keep_fresh()` and `toward()`. It takes both of the instance's movers, since the guard tests his clear
  line along `sight` and steps along `mover` (open to overturn). The old way's state was never streamed,
  so nothing new is carried.
- `ChaseWay.sweep_under_way()` exists for the velvet-stork cost probe, whose stale rig call was fixed.

**Verified at test parity** (inbox #813 in curly-moose), headless on macOS with Godot 4.7.2: a probe
committed first (`tests/probes/minty_quail_walk_field_parity.gd`) prints per tick the guard's position,
heading and the arrow's target on seeds 4242, 90210 and 555, standing and walking away, and the arrow's
target, nearest target and walking length at twelve spread positions on days 6, 11 and 13 on two seeds;
its output was byte-identical before and after. `check.sh`, `lint.sh`, `ci_telemetry_kinds.py` and
`cost-table.sh --check` pass; the touched suites (`day13_guard`, `scene_recipe_stretch`,
`test_resistance`, `pursuit`, `event_manager`) report no failure and no `SCRIPT ERROR`.
