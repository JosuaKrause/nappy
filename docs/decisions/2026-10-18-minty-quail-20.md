# minty-quail — RunFlow carries the run's screens, and the observers hear the day from it (main-and-state 4a) · 2026-10-26


minty-quail's fourth step of `main-and-state.md`, after the `DayDirector` and `LossCause`
(minty-quail-18). The step is built as three PRs, each reviewed and merged before the next: 4a the
run flow and the observers (this one), 4b the run handed to `GameSave` and the remaining readers, 4c
`SummaryModel`, `continued(mode)` and the remaining scene-recipe branches. `main` told its screens
apart by seven members (`_spec`, `_resume_gate_open`, `_run_over`, `_went_back`, `_ending_shown`,
`_escape_from_a_run`, `_in_the_title`, beside `_carried_from_a_checkpoint`), read in order inside
`_on_summary_continued()`, which every summary screen reached through one `continued` signal; and the
director called the readout, the HUD and the route rig inline.

**Built.** `RunFlow` (`src/run/run_flow.gd`, run flow) is a `RefCounted` `main` builds once per boot.
Its `Mode` is the screen in front of the walk: BOOTING, TITLE, BRIEF, WALKING, DAY_OVER,
BACK_TO_CHECKPOINT, INTO_ESCAPE, SECTION_LOST, RUN_OVER, ENDING, EPILOGUE, RELOADING. `continued()`
returns the `Step` `main` carries out and leaves the mode that step leads to: a brief answers ENGAGE
(to WALKING), an ordinary day's end NEXT_DAY (WALKING), a lost day that spent a normal run's last nerve
CARRY_TO_CHECKPOINT, day 14 handed over RELOAD_SCENE into the escape, a lost section RETRY_SECTION
behind its brief (BRIEF), the day that ended the run SHOW_ENDING (ENDING), and the ending and the
epilogue RESTART_RUN; BOOTING, TITLE, WALKING and RELOADING answer NONE. The modes are entered where
the screens come up: the title, the resume gate (the ending when the load spent a hard run's last
nerve, read off `RunHeader.ending` rather than a copy), a section's brief, engaging the day, the
`DayEnd` (handed over, goes back, run over, or ordinary), `section_lost(run_over)`, `escape_won()`, and
`--ending`. `Opening` records how the boot came to the run (fresh, resumed, carried from a checkpoint,
the escape handed over, the escape resumed, the escape by `--start-escape`) and replaces
`_escape_from_a_run`, `_carried_from_a_checkpoint` and the local `escape_resumed_from_disk`; Esc's
guard over the escape's brief and epilogue asks `escape_screen_waits()`. **A scene recipe is the
flow's `authored` configuration** for the branches that are transitions: every continue plays the
scene again, a lost escape section is `RETRY_AUTHORED` (the runtime's `lose_the_attempt()`), and
`escape_follows()` is false, which `main._new_director()` hands the director. **The observers
subscribe**: `RunFlow.day_started(spec)` is sent as a day or section begins, before anything is built,
and `day_planned(spec)` once it is placed; `main` connects `DebugReadout.use_the_day` and
`use_the_plan` (the routes, refreshed from the city when the spec closes streets, which no escape
section does), `HUD.use_the_day` (the clock's precision off the spec; `set_clock_hundredths` is gone)
and `RouteRig.start_day(spec)`. `DayDirector` has no `readout`, `hud` or `route_rig` and calls no
observer. An authored scene's day is begun and never planned.

**The double continue, shown before it was fixed.** A tap on the day's summary holds the continue
button's pressed look for two frames before it continues (`DaySummary._acknowledge_and_continue()`)
and a key continues at once, so a key inside those frames started the next day and the tap's own
continue started it again. `tests/test_run_flow.gd`'s `_test_a_tap_then_a_key_continue_once`,
committed alone at 36eaf548 and run on that commit's unchanged code, failed with `started == [1, 1]`
(3 checks, 1 failure); with the flow, a continue with no screen up leads nowhere and the test passes.

**Choices made where the design was silent, open to overturn.** The orchestrating session's: the
step split into 4a, 4b and 4c; a continue with no screen up ignored, accepted for the remeasuring
step once the test above showed today's behaviour. The agent's: `RunFlow` is a `RefCounted` with
signals rather than a node, and `main` connects the observers at the composition root rather than
handing each the flow; `DayDirector` is still built per call by `main._new_director()`, the flow
being the long-lived owner; `RouteRig.start_day()` takes the planned spec; `TitleScreen.open(again)`
is handed "the flow is in ENDING or EPILOGUE", equivalent to `_ending_shown` and, since no path in
`main` opens the title after an ending on the same boot, false whenever the title opens;
`DaySummary.is_showing_the_epilogue()` is read by `test_finale` alone until 4c's `SummaryModel`.

**For the remeasuring step.** The ignored continue is the one behaviour meant to change. Three calls
move within one synchronous call, read off the code and not measured: the readout and the HUD hear the
spec from the flow just before the director's `use_the_day()` rather than inside it; the readout's
routes are refreshed at `day_planned` instead of right after `City.start_day()`; the route rig starts
after `RunFlow.day_planned`, which follows `EventBus.day_planned` exactly where its call stood.
