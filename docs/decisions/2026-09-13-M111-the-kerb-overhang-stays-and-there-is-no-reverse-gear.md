## M111 — The kerb overhang stays, and there is no reverse gear · decided 2026-09-13

*(2026-09-13, [PLAYTEST-68](../playtests/PLAYTEST-68.md): "Going over the curb is actually quite
realistic. Let's keep doing that. What would be the purpose of reversing? It would make things
more complicated. What happens if there is a car behind etc?")* The one question the build below
left open. A car that must about-face with no junction to reach turns round in the street, and a
half turn between two lanes 32px apart is a 16px arc, so its corners reach 40px from its centre
against 32px to the kerb: 8px over open pavement, every hard blocker still refused, and a strike
counting only on a road tile so it cannot kill her. Refusing the overhang was measured at 33 of
34 cars at a standstill inside ninety seconds. Decided as built: the overhang is the picture,
and a three-point turn — which would need a reverse gear and an answer for the car behind — is
not built. The instant reversal for a car already stopped with less than a half turn's room in
front of it stays as the last resort it was.
