# M204 — The trailer cut from saved scenes · 2026-10-05


The design is [PLAYTEST-139](../playtests/PLAYTEST-139.md); the reimagining and every correction
since are inbox #579 in [olive-hedgehog](../playtests/2026-10-05-olive-hedgehog.md): "with the newly
created scene recipes reimagine the trailer. the main beats / gameplay should still be there but you
have freedom to do whatever you think works best", the video never checked in, the first half told
in captions over the footage, and "no cap" on its length. The player watched the result and accepted
it ("the video itself is okay -- much better than the previous version", "music and audio mix is
okay, too", "okay I like it how you had it -- no changes needed").

**The cut.** `tools/trailer.sh` renders scene recipes and editorial cards from
`tools/trailer/shots.json` and composes them, with fades, black gaps, the ending line and sound, into
the ignored `build/trailer/trailer.mp4`: an opening card ("She won't settle inside." / "Just a walk,
until she sleeps."); a plain walk ("Somewhere quiet."); the wrong turn at a leaf blower ("Outside is
crowded."); the father past the birds into a park ("A park. It will be quiet there."); a close-up of
the mother walking a circle in a park while the baby falls asleep by the game's own rule ("Finally,
she sleeps."); the charging dog; the logo card; three one-second glimpses through black (army trucks
beside the mother, the father at a gatehouse, the mother running with the baby from a guard); and the
city pulling back to its green sides, mountains north and water south. Every shot starts already
moving and settled. The score is original oscillator synthesis with captured game audio under it.

**The same output every time, by construction**, not by rendering twice ("running each render twice
is just wasteful"): fixed recipes and seeds, each recipe's own simulation clock, the movie writer's
fixed frame rate, and an editor whose inputs are recorded in `build/trailer/editorial-settings.json`.
`--check` and `--check-load` remain in the tool and are not part of making the trailer.

**A recipe's checks follow the trailer** ("the tests must adjust to the trailer when we edit the
trailer -- not the other way around"): a trailer edit updates its recipe's observations to what the
shot shows, in the trailer's own framing, so they guard the trailer against later degradation.

**What the trailer needed from the game, kept for everyone:**

- **The city camera has no limits** (inbox #587 in olive-hedgehog: "the camera during normal gameplay
  shouldn't stop -- it should keep the player centered"): it follows her with its 46px look-ahead
  everywhere; interiors keep theirs. M120 had clamped the camera to cure an unpainted column at the
  map's edge; that cure is replaced by the ground following whatever view asks.
- **Ground residency follows the view.** The landscape outside the city — grass east and west,
  mountains north, water south — is streamed and painted for whatever view asks, adding no walkable
  tile, collision or road.
- **The bridge and the tunnel reach the edge of any view**: the bridge deck carries on to the frame's
  edge. A car entering from the north begins under the tunnel roof, while an outgoing car is
  removed once wholly inside the mouth; the two directions use distinct depths. Bridge arrivals
  start beyond the visible picture. Cars do not appear in the open or drive across the mountain.
- **A covered recording window still records**: the movie writer forces a draw when the window
  cannot draw, recording only.
- **A font that does not resolve to the family asked for stops the render**, naming both; nothing
  falls back silently.

**Chosen while building, open to overturn:** the opening card's and captions' wording beyond the
player's own; the score; shot and caption lengths (the player: "we will figure out the exact timing
as needed"); the ending line with the site's address, which covers the city's top rows in the last
frame; the leaf blower one tile further south and the wrong turn cut at 6.8s, so the halo does not
return on the far sidewalk; her first crossing 55px further west of where the old scene had it; the
birds shot authored with the park to the west on its own street, its flock moved off the park's edge
and its industrial building dropped, which the lot rules required. A second birds shot retained the
original staging, where the only legal park lot was too far from the walk to be seen. That option
was offered as `trailer-birds-staged`, with the unused version to be removed. The player answered in
[silky-egret](../playtests/2026-10-07-silky-egret.md): `current`, the westward walking sequence,
is better; the staged attempt looked worse. The staged recipe and shot list were removed. The local
review render uses the current sequence.

**What was tried and rejected.** A pause in the wrong turn ("always keep moving"); trimming action
instead of moving lead-ins; a lead-in crossing the spine; a dog lead-in that let the dog catch her
before the turn. With the recording camera's settle on, four non-trailer scenes (fire-truck, task-06,
task-07, task-13) end with her somewhere else entirely: where the camera starts changes the
simulation, not only the picture (most likely through the crowd it brings in), so the settle is a
recipe option (`settled_camera`), off by default and set on the trailer's scenes only. A first
render's covered window repeated old frames while the simulation ran on, which the forced draw fixed.

At the first handoff the staged-birds render and park-sleep circle had not been verified by eye.
A frame-5 mismatch was once reported in the birds shot under load, with no surviving evidence.

## Camera start and local review cut · 2026-10-07
