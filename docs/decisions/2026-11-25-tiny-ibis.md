# tiny-ibis — A waiting charger warns only where she sees it, the covered corners left out · 2026-11-25

In the joystick scheme the steering ring and the Run disc cover the view's two bottom corners, and what she can see
already leaves them out (`VisibleView`; feathery-lynx #581, which asks every place where visibility matters to follow
it, and the tap scheme to keep the whole view). A charger already in the world was not held to it: the waiting dog, the
alley robber and the hunting roadblock's guard noticed her once the whole of their warning picture was inside the
camera's whole view, corners included, and a vehicle letting men out stopped once it and every place its men may get
out were inside that same rect (bouncy-owl #1774's fix for the vehicle, azure-hawk). So a hand could go up under the
Run disc, with no badge and no warning she could see. The defect is finding 4 of the Opus review of PR #1785.

**"In her view" for an on-screen charger is now "all of it in sight".** `VisibleView.sees_all(rect)` answers it:
inside the view and, while the joystick scheme is in force, meeting neither covered corner.
`EventInstance._would_stand_in_her_view()`, the one check the waiting charger's notice (`EventPursuit`) and the
vehicle's stop (`EventInvestigation`) already asked, asks it of the warning picture, and of the vehicle's drawn box and
each man's raised-hand picture at every place he may get out. A charger under a covered corner keeps waiting until she
has him in sight, inside his radius, or walks on. The check stays the one place the decision is made; nothing repairs a
warning after it has gone up.

**What she can see is handed to the event, not re-derived.** `WhereSheIs`, the tick's word every live event hears,
carried the camera's rect (`Rect2`); it now carries the day's one `VisibleView`, which `EventManager` looks through
the camera at the top of the tick and tells with the rest of the word. The covered corners are laid onto the view once
a frame in the object that owns them, and no event works them out again from a rect and a cover. `WhereSheIs.view()`
answers null where nothing has looked through a camera (a rig that steps an instance directly), which, as before, leaves
the notice to the radius alone. Every test and rig that told `Rect2()` tells null; the views a test told are
`VisibleView`s in the tap scheme, so none of their numbers moved.

**The pop-in rule is untouched.** A thing still never appears or vanishes anywhere in the camera's whole view, corners
included (`SpawnSiting.on_the_view()`, olive-hedgehog #598), so a charger under a corner is neither made to arrive
already charging there nor taken away.

**Rejected:** a second test beside the notice, asking `EventManager.sees()` of the warning picture's corners after the
whole-rect test. It would be two answers to one question, and the point test needs the picture's four corners to stand
for the box (true only because a covered corner touches the view's own corner); `sees_all()` asks the box itself.
Handing each event the `ScreenCover` with the rect was rejected for the same reason: every event would lay the corners
onto the view again.

**Held by the tests:** a robber whose warning picture stands 10px into the bottom-left corner of a view led by her walk up and
to the right (she inside his radius) waits in the joystick scheme and warns in the tap one; the
fully heated unmarked van with its lower door's men 10px into that corner (she inside its notice) does not stop in
the joystick scheme and owes its two men in the tap one. Both cases fail with the corners ignored.

**Day 13's task guard waits while he is half in her sight.** When the done roadblock task sets him on her
(`ResistanceDirector._set_the_task_guard_on_her()`), he warns in place only with all of his warning picture in her
sight (`EventInstance.warning_rect()` asked of `VisibleView.sees_all()`), and is sent under a badge only once he is
wholly off the camera's view (`DRAWING_OFF_THE_VIEW` over his drawing); between the two, under a covered corner or
half past the view's edge, the order is held and asked again each tick. The player's words decide it: the corners
are not in sight anywhere visibility matters (feathery-lynx #581), an on-screen charger warns where she can see him
(bouncy-owl #1774), a second's warning on screen or a second's badge when he is not visible (inbox #764 in
minty-lemur), and nothing vanishes while any of it is drawn (olive-hedgehog #598). Warning in place there breaks the
first two, and a badge there breaks the last. The director's sight is now the day's `VisibleView` itself
(`set_sight()`), handed by `main`, which the chalk mark's notice asks of a point and the guard's order of a box.
`tests/test_day13_guard.gd` holds it: a joystick corner and a view's edge hold the order, the tap scheme under the
same corner view warns at once, and both held cases fail with the old test.

**The checkpoint guard's raised hand, measured.** The hut's guard who raises his hand when she walks across a
district door's line stands 22px beside his hut (`EventFootprints.hut_guard_beside()`), the huts 64px either side of
the road's middle, and she crosses within the boom's 32px of it, so at the frame she crosses the far guard's raised
hand (26x44) reaches at most 131px across from her, and only on the east side of a north-south street. A covered
corner begins 175px across and 35px down from the view's centre. So the hand is under it only with the camera's
centre more than 44px across and 35px up from her at once: her look-ahead alone gives 46px across or 32px up, and
32.5 by 22.8 on a diagonal, so it takes the camera's smoothing trailing a run (up to 28px, `RUN_SPEED` over the
camera's smoothing speed of 6) in a turn. The street camera has no limits (only interiors get them, and they have no district doors), so
it stays within about 74px of her. On an east-west street the far hand
is never under a corner. It cannot be ruled out, and the warning starts with the crossing (inbox #788 in
minty-lemur), so it cannot wait: `CheckpointController._hut_that_warns()` raises the hand of the farthest hut whose
hand is all in her sight, the nearer one when the far one's is hidden. The nearer guard's hand is always in sight at the crossing by the
same numbers (at most 99px across), so both can never be hidden at once; the fallback for none, the nearer once a
view has been looked through and the farthest while none has (a rig), is defensive. Which
hut warns stays the builder's choice, open to overturn: the nearer guard's charge comes from closer.
`tests/test_checkpoints.gd` holds it at a north-south door, the camera up and to the left of her as a turn can put
it: the near guard warns in the joystick scheme and the far one in the tap scheme, and the first fails with the
far hut always chosen.

**The notice is held back rather than the charger sited.** The queue entry asked for a waiting charger sited clear
of the corners. A charger on the map stands where the day's plan put him while her view moves round him, so no
siting can keep him clear of a corner; what can is the check before his warning, which holds his notice until all
of it is in sight. That is what was built.
