# minty-quail — Events grouped, no look or id branches, a car's frame committed (events-composed 2b/4) · 2026-10-25


minty-quail's `events-composed.md`, the second half of its second PR (PR #858), after 2a's
behaviours (minty-quail-13). The item: nine single-row flags on `EventDef` with `validate()` policing
their combinations; behaviour keyed on a row's picture (`def.look ==` for the roadblock's band, posts
and far guard, the van's take, the mouse's dash) and on its id (`"charging_dog"` with
`RUN_TAUGHT_DAY` in `EventManager._create()`, `"loudspeaker"`, and the resistance director's
`"roadblock"`, `"delivery_van"`, `"burnt_shell"`, `"homeless_yeller"`, `"robber_giving_chase"` and eight
`by_id("alley_robbery")`); and a car's frame run by `_drive()` re-running `_process` and rolling it
back (likely-bugs.md). The player answered fork 1 (board `lesson-dog-ids`, inbox #849): the day-3
lesson dog and the task guard get new ids, which overturned minty-quail-13's proposal that they keep
their row ids; asked that #848's two nits be done (inbox #852); and that no legacy shape be kept for a
clean design (inbox #852).

**Built.**
- **Grouped settings.** A sub-resource holds the settings one behaviour or one system reads, and each
  pairing check in `validate()` goes where the pairing can no longer be written:
  - `PursuitSpec` takes `stop_excitement`, `leaves_its_body` (was `body_stays_behind`) and
    `warns_over_the_street`; the roadblock carries the last two on its `hunts_as`, and
    `EventDef.leaves_its_body()` answers for its cold row. "A stop with no pursuit" and "a body left
    behind by a row that never pursues" can no longer be written.
  - `OnHerWay` (`EventDef.on_her_way`) replaces `sited_on_her_way` and `pastes_a_front`
    (`OnHerWay.at_a_front`).
  - `Aftermath` (`scar`, `on_finish`, `on_sight`) replaces `scar_id`, `spawns_on_finish` and
    `spawns_on_sight`; every row has one, empty on most.
  - `VehicleSpec` (`EventDef.vehicle`) replaces `drives` and holds `field_from_its_centre`.
  - `EventDef.solid_when` (`ALWAYS`, `ONCE_IT_STARTS`, `WHILE_LOWERED`) replaces
    `solid_once_it_starts` and `lifts_for_traffic`, which one row could set together.
  - `EventDef.Role` (`SCENERY`, `MAST`) replaces `scenery` and the mast's id.
  - `AbductionSpec` holds the van's take: the 2.5s walk and the 32px offset.
- **A row's picture no longer decides what it does.** The mouse's dash is `PathMode.ACROSS_ITS_ALLEY`;
  the van's take is composed for a row with an `AbductionSpec`; the roadblock is `EventBarrierPost`
  (the body left behind, the two posts, the spent guard, the band standing as the barrier), with its
  far guard a class of its own, `RoadblockFarGuard`. The post's `save()` carries the spent guard and
  his side, so `Planned.guard_spent` and `chaser_side` went. `CHECKPOINT_POST`, `LOOSE_DOG`, the
  robber's and the roadblock's drawn heading only draw and stay; the cyclist's pelican waits for PR 4.
- **A row's id no longer decides what it does.** `ResistanceSteps.Step` carries what the director read
  off a task's row id (`is_a_guarded_band`, `trap_row`, `at_a_front_door`, `lingers_after_handover`,
  `rigs_within`); the alley robber reads are `EventCatalogue.mark_guard()` or `task_guard()`.
- **Three new rows** (inbox #849), each built by its source row's own builder under a new id and
  validated in `EventCatalogue.all()`: `charging_dog_tutorial`, which `EventDirector.due()` hands out
  for the dog `_teach_the_run()` put first on day 3; `task_guard`, the alley robber waiting round day
  13's roadblock; and `task_guard_sent`, the robber giving chase sent from out of her view, whose
  charges count as the task guard's (`EventDef.counts_charges_as`). The task guard is moved with his
  saved state (`EventManager.move_extra()`, `EventInstance.move_to()`), and `notice_her_at()` refuses
  a leaving row, which the move makes reachable.
- **A car's frame is proposed, checked, then committed** (`EventCarDriving`, composed for a row with a
  `vehicle`): `_process` times one `_frame()`, which runs `begin_frame()`, one tick and `end_frame()`,
  with no call of `_process` to itself; the end of a route reached or cut short is acted on only if
  the frame that reached it is kept. Its give-way is flagged as the copy shared-components'
  `LaneMover` takes.
- **#848's nits** (inbox #852): the behaviour test's route is cut by a body on the car's road, so
  `resume()` itself begins the departure; `come_back_as_the_barrier()` resets the departure as
  `stand_as_the_barrier()` does.
- **No legacy shape kept** (inbox #852): no accessor survives under an old flag's name, and
  `EventInstance`'s forwards to its behaviours (`chaser_side()`, `is_a_spent_guard()`, `is_standing()`,
  `is_driving()`, `closes_on()` and the rest) went; callers ask the behaviour. No caller in another
  agent's fence needed a forwarder.

**Choices made where the design was silent, open to overturn:**
- The classes are `EventCarDriving` and `EventBarrierPost`, matching 2a's behaviours, where the plan
  wrote `CarDriving` and `BarrierPost`.
- `stops_where_it_arrives` stays a field of the row rather than of `VehicleSpec`: the neighbor
  heading home parks too and is not a vehicle.
- The three new rows are derived copies outside `EventCatalogue.all()`, which would refuse a second
  row with the same picture and give each a cost-table row; `by_id()` does not find them, and
  `lesson_of()`, `task_guard()` and `task_guard_sent()` do.
- The lesson's dog is the cold derived row, whatever heat the dog it replaces was taken at; on day 3
  resistance progress is 0, so the two are the same.
- `EventCarDriving` saves nothing: whether it stands is decided every frame, and a car streamed back
  in stands, as a car first made does. `EventBarrierPost` saves only a spent or departing guard and
  his side; a guard streamed out mid-chase comes back as he did before.
- `SealPlanner.sealed_variant()` takes the whole aftermath, which also drops `on_sight`; only
  `burning_building` sets it and it is never a seal.
