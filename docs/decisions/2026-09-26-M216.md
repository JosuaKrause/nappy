# M216 — A courtyard block's roof turns its corners instead of stopping in a parapet · built 2026-09-27

*([PLAYTEST-143](../playtests/PLAYTEST-143.md), statement 2: "the small courtyard building needs
the roofs to go around the corner (like I described with the other building types earlier)
currently it doesn't read correctly" — the earlier description is PLAYTEST-138's roof that
extends "from the bottom building above to the roof of the top building", which M203 (this same
PR, #365) builds.)*

**What is built.** A single-block or apartment-complex courtyard is cut into up to four
rectangles around its hole (`CityGenerator._build_block()`'s `COURTYARD` branch, `_subtract_all()`
around `layout.open_rect`), each its own `Building`. M203's own per-column covering mechanism
already extends one piece's roof to fill the wall a neighbouring piece cannot draw, but it always
capped that extension with `ROOF_EDGE_N` exactly where it reached the covered piece's own roof
line — a parapet in the middle of what is, physically, one continuous roof, since the covered
piece's own roof continues right above it either way, uncapped from below. `Building.
roof_extension_seamless` (parallel to `roof_extension_rows`) now marks which extensions skip that
cap; `City._assign_roof_extensions()` sets it wherever the covering and the covered rectangles were
both cut from the same courtyard lot (`_courtyard_lot_of()`, matched against `map.lot_rect(block)`
for every `COURTYARD` block), never for two genuinely separate buildings, where the cap is the
real, visible step M203 always wanted. One mechanism handles both the courtyard's outer corners
(a wing meeting the block's own street-facing front) and its inner ones (two wings meeting across
the hole) alike, since both are the same "same courtyard lot" test.

**Verified against real generated cities**, not a hand-built fixture:
`_test_the_real_sweep_marks_a_courtyard_seam_seamless_and_an_ordinary_one_not`
(`tests/test_ground_floor.gd`) recomputes courtyard-lot membership independently and checks every
covered column in the sweep, courtyard and ordinary alike — every OTHER covered column (M203's
ordinary front-and-back case) must NOT be seamless, or a real parapet step would silently vanish
there too.

Evidence: `docs/evidence/m216-courtyard-roof-corner-2026-09-27/`, seed 247, a small courtyard block
standing in its own interior (walk script `15e1n` from the doorstep) — before (`main`, neither
M203 nor M216: each wing draws its own ordinary front and its own capped roof, stepped below the
back wing's) and after (this branch: the covered wings draw no facade, M203, and the roof over
them continues from the back wing's own roofline with no step in between, M216).

`m216_corner_evidence.gd` are investigative probes (not suites, not discovered by the runner) that
found and inspected real courtyard/covered-front seeds and the walk scripts to reach them; parked
under `tests/probes/` per the verify skill, since a follow-up capture session can reuse them.
