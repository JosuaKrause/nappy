# M216 — A courtyard block's roof turns its corners instead of stopping in a parapet · built 2026-09-27

*([PLAYTEST-143](../playtests/PLAYTEST-143.md), statement 2: "the small courtyard building needs
the roofs to go around the corner (like I described with the other building types earlier)
currently it doesn't read correctly" — the earlier description is PLAYTEST-138's roof that
extends "from the bottom building above to the roof of the top building", which M203 (this same
PR, #365) builds.)*

**What is built.** A single-block or apartment-complex courtyard is cut into up to four
rectangles around its hole (`CityGenerator._build_block()`'s `COURTYARD` branch, `_subtract_all()`
around `layout.open_rect`), each its own `Building`. M203's per-column covering mechanism extends
one piece's roof to fill the wall a neighbouring piece cannot draw; between two pieces of one
courtyard lot, that seam is the middle of one roof, so it draws no lip on either side.
`City._assign_roof_extensions()` marks both sides wherever the covering and the covered rectangles
were cut from the same courtyard lot (`_courtyard_lot_of()`, matched against `map.lot_rect(block)`
for every `COURTYARD` block): the covering column in `Building.roof_extension_seamless`, which
withholds `ROOF_EDGE_N` at the extension's top, and the covered column in
`Building.seamless_cover_cols`, which withholds `ROOF_EDGE_S` on that piece's first roof row.
`Building.roof_cell_edges()` decides every roof cell's four lips in one place, and `_draw()` paints
exactly its answer. A covered piece with no roof rows of its own still caps, since nothing of its
own carries on above the extension. Every piece of one courtyard lot takes its first piece's tint
(`Building.tint_variant`, wall and roof), since the pieces are one building. Two genuinely separate
buildings keep both lips and their own colours, the real, visible step M203 draws. One mechanism
serves the outer corners (a wing meeting the block's street-facing front) and the inner ones (a
wing meeting the strip across the hole).

**Found in review, and fixed.** The first build (reviewed at 00284bdb) withheld only the covering
piece's `ROOF_EDGE_N`; the covered piece still drew `ROOF_EDGE_S` on its first roof row at every
column, so each seam kept the full parapet band, pixel for pixel the same run as a real
roof-over-wall edge, and its after-still showed it. The three rectangles of the pictured courtyard
also carried three roof tints. Its test checked the flag and not the drawing, which is how the band
got through; the tests now ask `roof_cell_edges()` on both sides of a seamless and an ordinary seam,
on fixtures and across the real sweep, and check the shared tint.

**Offered and rejected.** Sharing `variant` across the pieces to share the colour: `variant` also
seeds the windows, the front, the door, the fire escape and the roof furniture, so every piece
would roll the same building. **Open to overturn:** the shared tint covers the walls as well as the
roofs, since a wall is its roof's colour in shade and a wing in another hue under the shared roof
reads as another building.
