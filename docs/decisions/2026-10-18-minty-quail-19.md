# minty-quail — Events drawn by looks, pictures as data, the protest handed its target (events-composed 3a/4) · 2026-10-25


minty-quail's `events-composed.md`, the first half of its third PR (PR #861), after 2b's grouped
settings (minty-quail-17). The item: `EventInstance` held about 2,300 lines of drawing and 870 of
picture tables beside what an event does, and the protest looked the `ResistanceDirector` up through
the `"resistance"` group from a draw path. The approved plan split PR 3 into 3a (the drawing apart,
with the protest's lookup, which sits on the draw path) and 3b (the physics tick), each its own PR;
the player asked for dependency injection throughout (minty-lemur #794) and that no legacy shape be
kept for a clean design (inbox #852).

**Built.**
- **`EventPictures`** (`src/events/views/event_pictures.gd`, static only) names every picture an
  event is drawn in, as its region on the baked `events` page, with the view tables
  (`*_BY_VIEW`, `_B`, `_C`, `WHEELS_*`, `EIGHT_VIEW_BY_SECTOR`), the checkpoint kit's anchors and
  boom geometry, the mark's sizes, the rules that pick a picture from data (`icon_for`,
  `icon_wheels_for`, `dog_picture`, `wide_scene_texture`, `protester_texture`, the segment and
  stationary-vehicle pictures, `canvas_margins`, `boom_arm_span`, `gate_runs_north_south`,
  `largest_mark_rect`) and the atlas lookups (`ATLAS_GROUP`, `drawn()`, `native_size()`,
  `steam_grate()`, `family_sources()`). The sizes placement reads (`box_of`, `footprint_of`,
  `solid_footprint_of`, the guard post's offset and heading, `warning_guard_rect`) stay on
  `EventInstance` for PR 4's spawner.
- **Looks** (`src/events/views/`): `EventLooks.for_row()` gives each row the look for its picture
  family, the one place a picture picks a class. The base `EventLook` (a `RefCounted`, after the
  crowd's `AgentLook`) reads the event's public state and never writes it; it holds the eight-view
  sector, the redraw gate and its key, the drawn reach, the caret and its cache, the shadows and the
  shared draws, and hooks through which the event builds, attaches, moves and frees the drawing
  nodes. Nine families: `FigureLook`, `EventVehicleLook`, `PursuerLook`, `FlockLook`, `SpreadLook`
  (the slices a long picture is drawn in and a crash's or burst main's scenery), `RankLook` and
  `RoadblockLook` (both `SpreadLook`s; the roadblock's band and far guard nodes), `FireLook`,
  `CheckpointLook`; the base draws nothing for `NONE` and `BURNT_SHELL`.
- **A look reads the event and never writes it.** The far guard's node is the roadblock look's: it is
  made, moved, shown, redrawn and freed from `RoadblockFarGuard`'s public walk (`at`, `heading`,
  `walked`), and the behaviour keeps only the walk. The rims behind a slice are the instance's to
  give back: the slices' look tells it it is letting go of them (`letting_go_of_the_slices`) and the
  instance releases them itself.
- **What still calls the look from the simulation, for 3b to retire:** the instance's tick asks the
  look to redraw if the picture changed and to move its drawing nodes
  (`redraw_if_the_picture_changed()`, `move_nodes()`); `transition()` tells it to hide its scenery
  once done (`hide_the_scenery()`); `EventCarDriving.end_frame()` asks for the redraw again after a
  frame it takes back; and `EventBarrierPost`'s `back_at_the_band()`, `stand_as_the_barrier()` and
  `come_back_as_the_barrier()` call `move_nodes()`.
- **`EventInstance`** keeps `look`, an `_draw()` that counts the frame record's draw and calls
  `look.draw()`, the halo's lifecycle (its ring built from `look.draw_body` and `look.current_bob`,
  as `CrowdAgent` builds its own), and read-only accessors for what the drawing read privately
  (`runs_vertically()`, `stands_side_on()`, `gait_phase()`, `gait_is_moving()`, `clock()`,
  `idle_offset()`, `world_map()`, `rim()`); `birds()`, `clear_line_to()`, `spread_axis()`,
  `spread_at()`, `guard_post_offset()`, `guard_post_heading()` and `add_beside_it()` are public. It is
  2,665 lines, from 6,050. No forwarder was kept: every caller asks the new owner.
- **The protest is handed its target.** `EventInstance.points_at`, a `Callable`, asks
  `EventManager.points_at` when the protest is drawn; `ResistanceDirector.setup()` hands the city's
  events its `pointable_objective` and no longer joins the `"resistance"` group, which nothing else
  looked up. With none handed in (the escape building, a bare rig) a protest points at nothing.
- Comments in the moved code state the rule in the present tense, and every comment and document
  that named a moved member names its new owner: the events and cues skills, the SVG headers that
  name the drawing, and `tests/probes/m159_prediction_audit.py`'s instrumented line (`birds()`).
- `tests/test_protest.gd` checks the wiring: a protest the city's events make, after a director is
  set up on day 6's perform step, asks the director's objective and its rank points at it; with no
  director it has nothing to point at. With the hand-in removed from `ResistanceDirector.setup()`,
  both of the first two checks fail.

**Choices made where the design was silent, open to overturn:**
- Nine families and a base, split by drawing family rather than one class per row.
- The instance field is `look`, beside the row's `def.look`, the picture enum.
- The gait stays on the instance as what its walk covered, since `EventCarDriving` takes it back with
  a frame it does not keep; the view sector is the look's.
- A drawing node is built, moved and freed by the look through hooks the instance calls from its own
  tree callbacks, the far guard's included (the first review's pick, over leaving it with
  `RoadblockFarGuard`).
- The look tells the instance through a signal when it lets go of its slices, and the instance
  gives back the rims standing behind them.
- The protest's target is asked through the manager when it is drawn, so an instance made before the
  resistance is set up points too.

**Behaviour changes: none intended.** 3a moves code; every row draws as before.

**For the remeasuring step, what 3b will change:** no behaviour, nor the instance's tick or
`transition()`, calls the look (the list above); events step on the 30 Hz physics tick, in the
manager's order (tell them where she is, step them, then catches and hard fails), instead of once per
drawn frame, with a 1/30s delta; a catch or hard fail is checked on the tick that caused it; the
redraw gate runs per drawn frame against state that changes per tick; the instances and the far
guard's node are drawn interpolated, as she is (the orchestrating session's pick, so they move as
smoothly as today); a scene recipe's held events are held by a flag rather than by
`set_process(false)`.
