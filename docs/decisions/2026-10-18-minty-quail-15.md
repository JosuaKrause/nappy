# minty-quail — One screen-edge badge handler, testable on its own, and the stairwell badge flake found · 2026-10-25


minty-quail's observers layer, done ahead of the remaining refactor steps because a flake fails PRs at random (PR #854), answering board `stairwell-badge-flake` and inbox #852. On `main` after a docs-only PR, four checks of `tests/test_interior.gd` failed: the escape building's stairwell masked man must have his screen-edge badge up from the frame he runs until he comes into view, and it dropped early ("plain left from row 10 ... down on 31 of 156", rows 11 and 9 likewise). The same code had passed on two earlier runs. The player's answer: "there should be a central handler that provides those badges that can be tested in isolation" (inbox #852).

**Built.** `BadgeModel` (`src/ui/badge_model.gd`, a `RefCounted`) decides which dangers get a screen-edge badge, when it goes up and when it comes down. `step(delta, here, subjects, sees, walk_speed)` is handed plain `Subject` records (the row's facts the rule reads, whether it was created under a warning, whether it is charging, whether any of it is in sight, a logged name, an opaque payload for the drawing; a warning with nothing in the world yet is a `Subject` with `pending`, a place and the seconds left), her position, the clock and a `sees(point, margin_px)` Callable. It owns the per-instance state (last position, smoothed approach, hold, seen) and answers `coming` and `announcing()`. The thresholds are the same numbers (`CLOSING_SPEED` 20, `LEAD_TIME` 5, `HOLD` 0.8, `SMOOTHING` 6, `SCREEN_MARGIN` 130, `MOST_AT_ONCE` 3). `DangerEdge` keeps the drawing and the per-frame hand-off: `_measure()` builds the `Subject`s from the event source's `instances()` and `pending_warnings()` and the view, and calls the model; it keeps no forwarder of the model's constants or functions, and the tests and the m207 probe call `BadgeModel`. `step()` is two paths: `_announce_the_warnings()`, the design (a warning is put up before the danger is placed), and `_measure_dangers_in_the_world()`, which the stairwell masked man and the army truck still need and which goes as a deletion in the follow-up that places those two after their badge. Per the player (inbox #852), a raised badge stays up until its danger is in sight or has left the world: `HOLD` is gone, and the margin band only decides whether a badge goes up. The call site (`main._add_danger_edge()`) is unchanged. The seam for events-composed's planned `WarningBoard`: it hands the model `Subject`s and a view Callable and reads the badges back; the model knows no `EventInstance`, `PendingWarning`, `VisibleView` or node.

**What varied.** The stairwell checks depend on `Engine.get_physics_interpolation_fraction()` at the moment a case starts. `project.godot` has physics interpolation on at 30 ticks per second, so the camera's view centre, which the badge asks, is shifted by a wall-clock fraction of a camera step (tens of world px) in a suite that steps the camera by hand. Two paths then lose his badge, and both come from the out-of-sight margin band (130 screen px, 65 world px past the view's edge):
- **The path CI's four lines show.** They read `first up 0 frames in, down on N`: the badge was up on his first frame and came down later. He starts beyond the band, runs into it while still out of sight, `seen` is already true for anything not created under a warning so the margin stays, and only `HOLD` (0.8 s) keeps the badge; she walks away at 92 px/s while he runs at 112, so he stays in the band for longer than `HOLD` and it drops.
- **A second path**: he sets off from inside the band, where the rule never raises a badge, so it stays down until he leaves the band (`first up -1` or a positive frame).
Measured, on one machine (macOS, Godot 4.7.2), before the change: plain `tools/test.sh --serial interior` passed 5 of 5 and a local `--shard 5/8` passed (125380 checks, 0 failures). Sleeping inside the loop, camera smoothing speed 0.1x to 10x, and the idle-frame delta reaching the suite (0.005 to about 0.29 s) changed nothing. With a temporary, uncommitted loop starting each of the 12 stairwell cases after a random 0 to 90 ms pause and a process frame, 4 of 7 runs had failures; in the 3 failing runs where the fraction was logged, every failing case started at 0.906, 0.928 or 0.928, while the roughly 40 logged passing cases covered 0.19 to 0.95. The failures were the row 10 and 11 walk-pace cases and a row 11 run-pace case, the same shape as CI's. With the test camera's `physics_interpolation_mode` off, the same loop over 6 runs of 12 cases had 0 drops.
Shown: the failure follows the interpolation fraction in this loop, and removing the camera's interpolation removes it. Shown by the review's scratch model and by `tests/test_badge_model.gd` now: with her walking away at 92 px/s and him running at 112 from beyond the band, the model without the charger rule gives `first up 0, down on 148 of 299` frames, CI's shape. Suggestive, not proven: that the failing starts cluster at a high fraction (three logged data points), and that CI's failure was this fraction (CI's was never observed);

**Verified at test parity** (inbox #813 in curly-moose): at `3b23d38c` (after a clean merge of `main` at `88bd9667`), `check.sh`, `lint.sh`, `ci_telemetry_kinds.py` and `cost-table.sh --check` pass, and the whole suite shard by shard (`TEST_SHARD_TIMEOUT_S=2400`, one at a time) had 0 failures and no `SCRIPT ERROR` line: shards 1 to 8 ran 86599, 58065, 492324, 16499, 117520, 134890, 268260 and 122521 checks. Tests changed only as far as the code moved: `test_danger`, `test_events_catalogue` and `test_drawn_view` call `BadgeModel` and `DangerEdge.subject_for_def()` where they called the removed forwarders (and `test_danger`'s check that `HOLD` is positive is gone with `HOLD`); `test_interior`'s badge counter stops counting at the first time he is in sight after the badge went up, since leaving sight again raises a new badge past the band. New `tests/test_badge_model.gd` (253 checks, no scene) covers approach from off screen, walking away or too slow, a lethal vehicle and a cyclist kept while she stands still and while it runs away and dropped in sight and when it is finished or no longer handed over, a fourth kept badge waiting for a slot, a seen thing in the band, a charger setting off inside the band, the masked man's run from five starts past the view edge (5, 25, 45, 64 and 90 px), CI's shape (the view moving with her at 92 px/s, him running at 112 px/s from 100 px past the edge, out of sight in the band for longer than the old 0.8 s hold), warned arrival, a pending warning, the cap of three and its order, pruning and which rows are worth an arrow. With the keeping rule taken out (a badge ends when its condition lapses) 24 of them fail, CI's shape among them; the earlier model, with a 0.8 s hold and no charger rule, gave `first up 0, down on 148 of 299` for that case. This deterministic case is the regression check. In `tests/test_interior.gd` the stairwell badge checks run with the test camera not interpolated, and a guard runs the four walk cases with interpolation on from 44 seeded starts across the tick and checks the fractions differed; it is a probabilistic extra that exercises the game's own interpolated camera (with the earlier charger rule taken out it failed in 2 of the review's 3 runs), not the regression check.

**Choices made where the design was silent, open to overturn:** the name `BadgeModel` and one `Subject` record for live things and pending warnings; no forwarder stays on `DangerEdge` (the player: no legacy shape kept), and `tests/probes/m207_warning_lead.gd` steps a `BadgeModel` instead of copying the rule; a kept badge over the cap of three is not drawn and takes a slot, soonest arrival first, when one frees; a danger shown again after leaving sight is raised anew past the margin band; the guard's pauses are a fixed list rather than random per run; and the fix is both the handler rule and the deterministic test, the orchestrating session's pick, not the brief's.

**For the remeasuring step:** deliberate behaviour changes, all in the shared rule and keyed on no row. (1) A raised badge stays up until its danger is in sight (the view proper) or has left the world (finished, or no longer handed over): no timer ends it, and neither does she walking away, the danger turning away or slowing, or the approach test lapsing. It reaches every badged danger: the pursuers, the cyclist, the lethal vehicles. Before, the badge came down 0.8 s after its condition lapsed or after it entered the margin band. This is what the player asked for (inbox #852: "a badge shouldn't disappear before the entity is visible"). (2) A charger (`EventInstance.has_charged()` with its own approach above zero, once, on its first such frame; `has_charged()` is true for the whole charge) has margin 0 until it has been in sight again, so a charger that starts within 65 world px past the view's edge has its badge from the frame it runs; before, it ran without one until it left the band. (3) `HOLD` no longer exists; the margin band still decides only whether a badge is raised, so a thing seen and then sitting just outside the view is not badged until it is past the band, and one shown and then out of sight again is raised anew. (4) Over the cap of three, a kept badge is not drawn. Nothing else is meant to change. The cues skill's sentence on the badge ("whenever something ... is out of sight and closing") does not say a raised badge is kept until the danger is seen.
