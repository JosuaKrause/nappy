# minty-quail — Picture geometry as EventFootprints, picture keys as row data, every clock on the walk's tick (events-composed 4c/4) · 2026-10-31


minty-quail's `events-composed.md`, the last of three PRs that build its fourth (PR #881), after 4a's
split of the manager (minty-quail-24) and 4b's one `start_day(spec)` (minty-quail-25). It closes
events-composed: the item's file goes. The item: siting read picture-size geometry off `EventInstance`
and the spawner; `def.look ==` decided behaviour in seven places outside the views; `at_heat()` flipped
behaviour flags on a copy and `has_a_spread` was a list of pictures; the tick set a drawing property;
and the day's countdown and the resistance's clocks ran on the drawn frame while the events stepped on
the physics tick. On the clocks the player asked that every clock be frame-locked, "otherwise someone
with a faster computer could get lower times over someone with many lag frames", agreed that on a
lagging machine the game slows down instead of the clock running on, and that a tick counts only when
the walk's simulation stepped on it (#866 in minty-dolphin). The orchestrating session's proposals,
open to overturn: the traffic signals and the encounter watch on ticks too, a test that a long drawn
frame advances no clock, and the escape's hundredths label shown advanced by the interpolation fraction.

**Built.**
- **`EventFootprints`** (`src/events/event_footprints.gd`), stateless, holds the picture-size geometry
  the spawner and siting read: `box_of`, `footprint_of`, `drawn_box_of`, `solid_footprint_of`,
  `solid_axis_of`, `body_axis` (the spawner's own copy of the rank test gone), `stands_side_on`, the
  roadblock guard's post (`guard_post_offset`, `guard_post_heading`, his side), `warning_picture_size`,
  `warning_guard_rect`, `picture_drop_of`, `LOOSE_LEAD_REACH`, and `hidden_by_its_hold(event)` (the
  alley post hidden by its own hold). The instance keeps `drawn_box()` and one public `solid_axis()`;
  `_spread_is_vertical` and `_centred_on_the_pavement_band` stay on it, since rigs read them.
- **Picture keys are row data.** No `def.look ==` remains under `src/` outside `src/events/views/`; the
  rows carry `stands_along_its_street` (the protest, the firefight), `lies_across_its_street` (the
  burnt-out car), `warning_picture` (the dog's alert, the robber's, the guard's raised hand),
  `trails_a_lead` (the loose dog), `hides_in_its_hold` (the checkpoint post), `may_be_a_pelican` (the
  cyclist) and `noun`/`noun_plural` (policeman, guardsman); the run log's wording is unchanged. A
  catalogue test checks each field is set on exactly the rows that need it.
- **`at_heat()` attaches a spec.** `HuntSpec`, authored on the row as `EventDef.hunts`, is a vehicle
  that lets men out (the patrol car one, the two vans two) or a man who pursues her himself (the
  roadblock guard, `ends_the_day`); `at_heat()` attaches it to the copy as `EventDef.hunting`, and
  `investigates()` and `lets_out()` read it. `hunts_as`, `hunts_on_foot`, the `investigates` flag and
  the `lets_out` field are gone; the patrol rung's `hard_fail = false` is a `validate()` rule beside
  three new hunt rules. Every heated copy is field for field what it was. `has_a_spread` is an exported
  field the eleven spread rows set.
- **The tick writes no drawing property.** A pursuer that warns over the street takes the warning draw
  layer in `EventInstance._process()`, the draw side, from the phase the last tick left.
- **Every clock counts the walk's ticks.** `main._physics_process()`, which already counted play time
  on every tick the walk stepped (minty-quail-23), runs `DayController.tick()` and
  `ResistanceDirector.tick()` on that tick; their drawn-frame clocks are gone. `DayController._process()`
  changes no state: between ticks it hands the HUD the countdown advanced by the interpolation fraction,
  so the escape's hundredths move every drawn frame as before. The arrow's sweep runs its 1000 tiles per
  tick; the park cordon's officers move on the tick and are drawn interpolated. The traffic signals and
  the encounter watch already counted physics ticks; only the signals' doc sentence changed. Tests: one
  walking tick moves the countdown and the resistance clock by exactly one tick and a five-second drawn
  frame moves neither (`test_main`); the hundredths between ticks (`test_day_controller`).
- The orchestrator's queue move fixed the sentences the move made untrue (`docs/MECHANICS.md`'s
  checkpoint, `docs/TELEMETRY.md`'s `--invincible`, two in `docs/GRAPHICS.md`, two comments in
  `region_planner.gd`, one in `tuning.gd`); the builder fixed the events skill's `footprint_of()`.

**Behaviour changes on purpose, for the remeasuring step.** At a steady 30 or more frames a second
nothing a player sees changes. Under lag these slow with the game rather than running on: the day's
countdown, dusk and the goal check (now landing on a tick), the task deadline, the note's lingering
rider, the mark's sight dwell, the arrow's half-second retarget, the raid, market and column
happenings, and the park cordon. At 60 frames a second an arrow field takes about two thirds of a
second to sweep rather than a third; the cordon officers' stride steps at 30 Hz.

**Verified at test parity** (inbox #813 in curly-moose), headless on macOS with Godot 4.7.2:
`check.sh`, `lint.sh`, `ci_telemetry_kinds.py` and `cost-table.sh --check` pass; the touched suites
report no failure and no `SCRIPT ERROR`; CI runs the full suite on the merge result.

**Choices open to overturn:** the field names above; one `HuntSpec` with two shapes; `hard_fail`
still written on the roadblock guard's hunting copy, from the spec's `ends_the_day` (the builder's
fork: whether it should be derived from the hunt with no write); `main` pushing the tick rather than
each node gating its own `_physics_process`; `DayController` emitting the hundredths rather than the
HUD computing them; the sweep's budget kept at 1000 tiles, now per tick.

**Carried on:** the instance's `ArrowField` pathfinding, the car's copy of the crowd's give-way and the
traffic signals advancing behind the title screen go to `shared-components.md`; `--route` from the
building section is `route-from-the-building.md`.
