# M221 — A failed day leaves no chalk mark behind · built 2026-09-27

*([PLAYTEST-143](../playtests/PLAYTEST-143.md), statement 7: "chalk marks don't get properly reset
on failed days accumulating more and more chalk marks in the same alley"; [crisp-moose](../playtests/2026-09-27-crisp-moose.md),
statement 1: "Stays crossed, until the day ends")*

**The cause.** Touching a mark whose next step is guarded calls `_begin_step()` twice in one
completion: the second overwrote the director's hold on the touched mark, which then stood for the
rest of the run, one more on every attempt.

**Built (PR #414).** The director keeps its own reference to a read mark, and frees it when the day
starts or is retried, and at no other time: a read mark stays where she read it, crossed through,
until then. A task expiring mid-day does not free it. The mark's own robber is never retired while
he is awake or on her screen, so reading a mark never makes him vanish in front of her or ends a
chase. Tests check the read mark survives the day and is gone on the retry, and that a guard in view
or awake is held.
