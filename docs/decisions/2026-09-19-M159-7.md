# M159 — The frame record splits draw at the renderer and counts redraws · built 2026-10-04

*([jolly-trout](../playtests/2026-10-04-jolly-trout.md), #541: "Can we get more granular mobile
measurements or would that break up things that are currently drawn together into separate draws?
We wouldn't want that I guess", then "Yes and let's start work on it to go into the next release"
and "Yeah a patch release for after 25".)* The phone's frame record
([m159-phone-frame-record-2026-10-04](../evidence/m159-phone-frame-record-2026-10-04/README.md))
had `draw` as the largest share, mostly unexplained by the counters it kept.

**What was built** (PR #553), only while the record is on: a `render` bucket from
`RenderingServer.frame_pre_draw` to `frame_post_draw`, so `draw` now ends at pre-draw and `draw`
plus `render` is the old `draw` (headless has no pre-draw, so `render` is 0 there); a per-frame count
of the game's own `_draw()` calls by kind (`draws_crowd`, `draws_events`, `draws_halos`,
`draws_scenery`, `draws_badges`, `draws_player`, `draws_other`), one guarded counter at the top of
each override; and `render_cpu_usec`, the engine's measured render CPU time, with
`environment.render_cpu_reported` saying whether any row held one. Nothing is timed per `_draw()`
and nothing drawn changes: draw calls, render objects and primitives match the base within run
spread on seed 67's route. The schema is version 2; `docs/TELEMETRY.md` has the columns.

**Measured on the desktop** (Apple M2, three runs per condition, weak because single runs vary from
7.8 to 10.4ms): the record off costs nothing measurable; on, a few tenths of a millisecond above
base on the median. `draw` was 0.47 to 0.70ms and `render` 2.85 to 3.26ms, `render_cpu_usec` about a
third of `render` (the rest is submitting and the buffer swap); the crowd redrew 10 to 12 of its 234
agents a frame on average. Whether the web build reports `render_cpu_usec`, and what the phone's
split is, waits on the next phone record ([M159-3](../review/2026-09-19-M159-3.md)).

**Proposed, not asked for, and open to overturn:** `render` as a new bucket with `draw` keeping its
name for the part before pre-draw, so an old file's `draw` compares only with the new sum; the seven
kinds; microseconds read at post-draw; `wait` moving from column index 8 to 9.
