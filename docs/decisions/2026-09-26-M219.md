# M219 — On a pedestrian street a blocked walker turns round · 2026-10-11


[PLAYTEST-143](../playtests/PLAYTEST-143.md), statement 5: "on the pedestrian street people don't
turn around when their path is blocked so they accumulate on obstacles"; re-reported on v0.27.1,
seed 67, day 9 ([velvet-marten](../playtests/2026-10-11-velvet-marten.md), #696): "I noticed people
still pile up in the pedestrian zone instaed of turning around at barriers."

**The cause.** On a precinct `CrowdAgent._footway_is_shut()` never answered true, since a precinct
is stepped round across all six lanes rather than shut lane by lane, so `_turn_round()` was never
reached there. When stepping round gave up, the walker slowed to a stop at the obstacle and the
ones behind it queued.

**Built.** `_footway_is_shut()` (`src/crowd/crowd_agent.gd`) treats a precinct's footway as its whole
six-lane width and answers true when all six lanes are taken by a body at that point along the
street; `_cannot_go_on()` then reports a wall and the existing `_divert()` → `_turn_round()` path turns
the walker round, as on an ordinary street. A body in fewer than six lanes is still stepped round
across all six. `docs/MECHANICS.md` says so beside the ordinary footway's rule.

*Open to overturn, chosen by the implementer:* "blocked" means all six lanes taken at one point, the
ordinary footway's rule applied to the precinct's whole width; only bodies in
`CityMap.obstructed_tiles`, closed tiles and soft seals count, and bollard posts, which have no crowd
effect, stay so.

**Verified** by `_test_a_precinct_blocked_across_its_width_turns_walkers_round`
(`tests/test_crowd_bodies.gd`): the seed-4242 precinct walled across its width, six walkers, one per
lane, walked at the wall for 20 seconds. On the code before the change no walker turned and 6720 of
7200 walker-frames were spent within 1.5 tiles of the wall; after it every check passes. No capture
of seed 67's day 9 was taken.
