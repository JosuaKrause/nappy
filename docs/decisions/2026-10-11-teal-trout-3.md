# teal-trout — The chargers' rest and alert poses are drawn in five views · 2026-10-17

Every charger the player triggers, and that is not already charging, gets a rest pose and an alert
pose ([polite-toad](../playtests/2026-10-11-polite-toad.md), #722). Drawn in two review sheets, not
yet wired into the game.

**The first sheet, front and side** (PR #726,
[teal-trout-poses-2026-10-11](../evidence/teal-trout-poses-2026-10-11/README.md)). The player, on
the pull request on 2026-10-12 ([the comment](https://github.com/JosuaKrause/nappy/pull/726#issuecomment-6123236064)): "graphics look good", and merged it. That
approves the front and side of the poses as drawn; it answers none of the open questions below,
and the back and diagonals had not been drawn yet.

**The second sheet, the other three views** (PR #745,
[teal-trout-views-2026-10-17](../evidence/teal-trout-views-2026-10-17/README.md)): back,
front-diagonal and back-diagonal for all four poses, every diagonal facing east for the game to
mirror, each on its family's canvas and bottom-centre anchor. The files:

- the charging dog: `art/events/charging_dog_resting*.svg` (sitting, ears soft, tail on the ground)
  and `charging_dog_alert*.svg` (up on four stiff legs, hackles up, teeth showing with the jaw
  shut), on the charging dog's own canvases, 40x22 side, 30x28 front and back, 38x28 diagonals;
- the alley robber: `art/events/robber_alert_*.svg`, both hands out of his coat, held low, 26x44;
- the guards: `art/checkpoints/guard_alert_*.svg`, square-on with one hand raised palm out, 22x44
  front and back and 26x44 side and diagonals.

**Changed since the first sheet**, from the after-the-fact review of #726: the dog's files took the
charging dog's family name (they were `dog_resting*` and `dog_alert*`, which read as the pet
dog's); the guard's side alert was widened to 26 and its body moved so he no longer steps a pixel
when it swaps with his standing picture.

**Choices open to overturn**, made where the brief was silent: the dog sits rather than stands at
rest, so all three stages read apart at 1x; the dog's alert shows teeth with the jaw shut, keeping
the open red jaw for the charge alone; no cue is added in any pose (the **cues** rules); one alert
picture serves both the roadblock's guard and the door guard (*proposed, not asked for*, in the
entry); the guard's back alert raises the hand on the picture's left, so it changes sides at the
swap to his lunge, whose arm is on the right.

**Open, asked on PR #745 and in the review item:**

- **The robber's side alert.** The first sheet's differs from his waiting picture by one small hand
  and half a pixel of lean at game scale. A second one, wider stance, near arm off the body, the far
  hand showing, is drawn beside it
  (`docs/evidence/teal-trout-views-2026-10-17/robber_alert_side-v2.svg`), and the robber's three
  new views follow it. `art/events/robber_alert_side.svg` is still the first. Choosing the first
  means redrawing those three views; the drawer would pick the second.
- **Whether an alert walks.** While warning, the dog, the robber and the roadblock's guard close to
  their stand-off at their pursuit speed (130px/s for the dog and the robber), so one still alert
  picture glides. (a) A stride frame per view for each of those alert poses, fifteen more pictures and five more
  comic transfers for the dog; (b) the pursuer stands still for its notice, a change to how it
  plays under the events rules that re-checks each row's pursuit fairness
  (`Tuning.validate_pursuit()`).
  The drawer's pick and the filer's: (a). Only the door guard holds his ground during his notice.
- **The guard's raised palm** is drawn to mean "stop there", and both guards who use it are escaped
  by running, while the gatehouse nearby teaches that standing still gets her let through: does it
  read as "he's onto me" or as "wait here"? The alternative is an arm pointing at her.
