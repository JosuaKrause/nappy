# jolly-owl — The zzz stays just above her walking south · built 2026-10-04

*([freckled-goose](../playtests/2026-10-04-freckled-goose.md), inbox #545: "When going south the zzz
jumps up a full stroller gap above the player", "It should stay over the player without extra gap",
"Like in the other directions", "(in the other other directions it's above the stroller but since
going south the stroller is below the player it should be positioned above the player instead)".)*

**What was built** (PR #547). Walking due south with the pram pushed, `Stroller.baby_cue_lift()`
measures the baby cue from her rather than from the pram: `FIGURE_HEIGHT + (BABY_CUE_LIFT -
PRAM_ART_HEIGHT) + pram_draw_offset().y`, which puts the cue about 52px above her feet, 6px clear of
her head, the same clearance the other facings keep over the pram's art (`BABY_CUE_LIFT` 36px over
`PRAM_ART_HEIGHT` 30px). It was about 73px, `BABY_CUE_LIFT + FIGURE_HEIGHT` above a pram 9px below
her. The other seven facings and the step aside under a danger mark are unchanged. Stills of all
eight facings before and after, and a phone-portrait due-south pair, are in
`docs/evidence/jolly-owl-zzz-south-2026-10-04/`.

**Proposed, not asked for, and open to overturn:** the carried case keeps its 82px lift walking
south (one line would give it the same 52px); `PRAM_ART_HEIGHT` as a named constant so the
clearance is derived.
