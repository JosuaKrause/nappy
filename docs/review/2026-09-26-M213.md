# M213 — Play days 6 to 9 for the chalk marks · 2026-09-27

**Play days 6 to 9 and watch the marks, their robbers and the task line.** On day 6 the mark sits at its
alley's mouth and its robber stands two-thirds through the alley, about 155px in
([feathery-marmot](../decisions/2026-10-03-feathery-marmot.md)), so reading the mark from its own
end may wake him in a one-block alley ("Two-thirds wins"), and neither the mark nor a robber should ever appear where you can see it happen. A read mark stays
standing, crossed through, for the rest of the attempt — never in more than one alley at once —
and is gone the next day or on a retry, so a failed day leaves no mark behind. The bottom-left task
line goes the moment its task is done. Day 8's text is short,
and day 9 reads "Cross at this district door. See if they let you through." A mark in a courtyard passage has its robber at the courtyard's inner end, to be lured out. Does any mark stand where no path leads, does any mark or
robber still come out of nowhere, does a read mark read right, and does the task line tell you a
task is done?
