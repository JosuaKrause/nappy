# M213 — Play days 6 to 9 for the chalk marks · 2026-09-27

**Play days 6 to 9 and watch the marks, their robbers and the task line.** On day 6 the robber
should stand at the far end of the mark's alley, so the mark can be reached from its own end, and
neither the mark nor a robber should ever appear where you can see it happen. A read mark stays
standing, crossed through, for the rest of the attempt — never in more than one alley at once —
and is gone the next day or on a retry, so a failed day leaves no mark behind. The bottom-left task
line goes the moment its task is done. Day 7's red arrow ends on the van, day 8's text is short,
and day 9 reads "Cross at this district door. See if they let you through." Does any mark or
robber still come out of nowhere, does a read mark reading right, and does the task line tell you a
task is done?
