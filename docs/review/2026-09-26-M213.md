# M213 — Play days 6 to 9 for the chalk marks · 2026-09-27

**Play days 6 to 9 and watch the marks, their robbers and the task line.** On day 6 the robber
should stand at the far end of the mark's alley, so the mark can be reached from its own end, and
neither the mark nor a robber should ever appear where you can see it happen. Once a mark is read
it is gone from every alley, and a failed day leaves no mark behind. The bottom-left task line
goes the moment its task is done. Day 7's red arrow ends on the van, day 8's text is short, and
day 9 reads "Cross at this district door. See if they let you through." Does any mark or robber
still come out of nowhere, and does the task line tell you a task is done?
