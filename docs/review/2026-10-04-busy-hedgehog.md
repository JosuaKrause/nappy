# busy-hedgehog — A return to the page does not start the day · 2026-10-04

After the next release, on the phone and in a desktop browser: leave the page during the day brief
(switch tab or app), come back with a tap or click on the page, and see whether the day brief is
still there; do the same during play, where the pause screen should be waiting. A tap after about
half a second should then act as usual. Record is [busy-hedgehog](../decisions/2026-10-04-busy-hedgehog.md).
