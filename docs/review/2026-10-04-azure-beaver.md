# azure-beaver — Walk each task scene · 2026-10-08

Open the task recipes from day 6 through day 14 with `tools/run.sh --recipe FILE`, then the
station-door corner. The [scene guide](../SCENE_RECIPES.md) names each file and its expected
task. Walk beyond the scripted route and turn back: does each layout feel like one inhabited
stretch of the day's streets, with nothing drawn outside it? Watch people and the cars present
on days 6–11 reach the ends; do they turn, wait or leave outside the view without popping in
or disappearing? Check the route-bag event actually reaches the picture.

Inspect the authored wear and props where the recipe lists them. Day 6 has no litter or
posters, and day 9 and the station corner have no posters; those absences are explicit scene
choices, not missing rendering. Days 6–13 begin with an unread mark and offer the task only
after she touches it. Follow the arrow and check that the target can be reached within the
visible streets. The ordinary danger remains active; a completed task can send a pursuer.

The start/later stills and available capture evidence are linked from
[PR #592](https://github.com/JosuaKrause/nappy/pull/592). The design and verification limits
are recorded in [azure-beaver, handcrafted scenes](../decisions/2026-10-04-azure-beaver.md).
