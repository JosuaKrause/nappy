# M215 — Walk behind a power station's stack · 2026-09-27

**Walk behind a power station's stack and see whether she disappears behind it.** On any day
with a power station, walk along the street north of it and pass behind one of its two
chimneys: the chimney should cover her and the pram, and anyone walking there. Also watch for
a warning caret or her exclamation mark over somebody standing behind a chimney, which is hidden
with them. That only happens inside a chimney's 32px width on the one street north of the
station; the alternative is to draw the marks (the caret, her exclamation mark, the baby's cue) in
a layer above everything, which changes how every cue is drawn for that one case, and the
recommendation is to keep it as built. Does the chimney standing in front read right, and is the
hidden mark acceptable? The
stills are in PR #405 and in `docs/evidence/m215-stacks-in-front-2026-09-27/` (seed 61400).
