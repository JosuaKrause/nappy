# tall-walrus — Walk into a precinct's posts · 2026-10-10

Walk to the end of a pedestrian precinct where a street meets it, and walk into the posts across
the carriageway: a post should stop her head-on and turn her aside when she meets it off-centre,
and the gap between the two posts and the pavements either side should let her through. The posts
now stand two to a row, 40px apart, where they stood five in a tight line, because five left no
gap her body fits (the player's "gaps still passable"). Do two posts still read as a street closed
on purpose rather than the road running out, or should the row change: five posts that bar the
carriageway with only the pavements open, or something else? The build is recorded in
