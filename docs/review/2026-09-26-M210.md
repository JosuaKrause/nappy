# M210 — The screens between days, as the coming day's · 2026-09-27

**Finish a day, fail one, and lose the run, and read each screen.** After a day she slept, the
title says how long it took and everything under it is about the coming day: its number, the
nerves as stars, its morning line. After a day the clock ran out, the title says so and the day
number is the same day again. On day 7 "The same face is on most of them." stands on its own line.
On the game over screen, "You made it to day N." names the day the run ended on. Does each screen
read as the coming day's, and is the missing "You got her home." on a won day a loss? The stills
are in PR #410.
