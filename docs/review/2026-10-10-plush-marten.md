# plush-marten — Play day 12's park both ways · 2026-10-10

Play day 12 to the swing. Once you touch it, police officers walk in from off screen and tape the
park's edge a stretch at a time, closing round to one last gap near where you stood (the nearest one
that still leads home), while others walk anyone in the park out through it. Leave through it once,
and on another run stay inside until it closes, which ends the day. Does it read as the park being
taken in front of you? Do you see them coming in time, with no badge? It closes about 12 seconds after
the touch on most parks, and later on a large park, up to about 28 seconds, so that you have the walk
to the gap and two seconds more after you first see an officer. Is the tape's look right (blue and
white, thigh height), or should it be yellow and black? Should the tape still stand on days 13 and
14? And since the swing's robber usually runs you out within a couple of seconds, is being shut in
only after that long the danger you meant? The build is recorded in
