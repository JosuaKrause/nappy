# round-gecko — Try switching the steering and Run sides on a phone · 2026-10-07

In joystick mode, tap to steer on either side and lift the finger. Run should remain on the
opposite focal point at the joystick's size. Tap outside Run on its half to steer there, and
check that the roles swap. Hold Run with one thumb while tapping and dragging with the other;
cross the middle stop band, release the run thumb, and try double-tap running. A held pointer
should retain its role, and releasing steering should never reset the selected side.

Try Run, pause, and the round title/pause/summary buttons near their visible rims on the phone,
in landscape and rotated portrait presentation. Their 5% extra reach should feel consistent;
the joystick's stop area should feel unchanged. Are the larger Run disc and reversible side
selection easy to use, and are the button edges forgiving enough? The implementation and
captured layouts are recorded in [round-gecko](../decisions/2026-10-07-round-gecko.md).
