# silky-rabbit — Nearby ground preparation during real play · 2026-10-02

Play an ordinary saved or fresh run in the browser or on the phone, traversing
several blocks and reversing direction after nearby ground begins preparing.
Check for missing or late ground, discontinuous water or route-curb coloring,
and problems at a day transition or orientation change. These correctness checks
are not covered by the mobile stutter report in
[snowy-ibis](../playtests/2026-10-02-snowy-ibis.md).

Record the device/browser, build, route and observations in a new playtest.
The [decision and matched comparison](../decisions/2026-10-02-silky-rabbit.md)
and [evidence](../evidence/silky-rabbit-ground-frames-2026-10-02/README.md)
describe the current stepped implementation and its source/cell equality checks.
This item holds the remaining correctness questions; it does not ask the player
to repeat the answered mobile perceptual-stutter question.
