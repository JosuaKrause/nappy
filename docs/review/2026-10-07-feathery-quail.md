# feathery-quail — Watch a police car leave · 2026-10-11


On a build from v0.27.0 or later (after PR #660, which already stopped the investigating car vanishing
beside her), over a few days with police patrols, watch police cars go: one that drives away, one that stops and
lets its man out and later drives on, and one you follow on foot as it leaves its street. Does any
car vanish while you can still see it, rather than driving out of view? If one does, note the day,
what it was doing and what happened just before. Walking back the way a car left, it should not be
standing at the end of its route again. The build is recorded in
[feathery-quail](../decisions/2026-10-07-feathery-quail.md).
