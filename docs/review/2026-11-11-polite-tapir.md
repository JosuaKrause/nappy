# polite-tapir — The dog walker, the patrol and the convoy come her way · 2026-11-16

Play days 2 to 5 on the desktop build (`tools/run.sh --seed 90210 --day 2`, then the same for days 3, 4
and 5), walking her routes as you would. The dog walker, the police patrol and, from day 13 or with
`--force military_convoy`, the military convoy are no longer standing where a street drew them; the
director sends each one just ahead of her on the street she walks, as a thing handed out from her
route's bag (inbox #1460). The dog walker comes down her sidewalk toward her, its dog
already barking on and off as it would be for one she walked up to; the patrol car drives toward her
in the lane of the road that runs her way, the near lane or the far one by which sidewalk she is on,
coming into view from the edge of the screen; the convoy is warned of first by its screen-edge badge, then its army
truck drives past in the lane beside her, from behind her or from ahead of her.

Watch whether each reads as someone coming her way rather than appearing beside her, whether the
dog walker coming at her leaves you the time to step aside or cross that a dog walker already out in
a street, which she walked up to, would leave you, and whether
the convoy's badge, then its truck from behind, feels warned or sprung. The pictures of one arrival of
each are in [the evidence folder](../evidence/polite-tapir-sent-ahead-arrivals-2026-11-16/README.md).
