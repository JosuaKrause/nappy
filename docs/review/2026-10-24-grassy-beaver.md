# grassy-beaver — Judge the thought bubbles' pictures · 2026-11-25

**What to do.** Open the review sheet, `docs/evidence/grassy-beaver-bubble-pictures-2026-11-25/review-sheet.png`
(embedded in the pull request that drew it). It shows the thought bubble, the paper-white cloud
that will float over the mother with a hint in it and no words, holding each of its eight icons,
over the mother and the pram at the game's zoom on the sidewalk, the asphalt and the grass, each
also enlarged four times, and a strip of all of them at native size beside the exclamation mark.
Nothing in the game draws them yet: the bubbles are shown by a later piece of the same entry, so
this is the moment to turn a picture down before it is built in.

**Where to look.** Each row is one hint: the way to the park, back to the tutorial route, use the
zebra crossing, cross the street, turn back, walk wide of the noise, the chalk mark in its alley,
and walk through the checkpoint hut. The first four point somewhere with one blue arrow beside the
icon, which the game turns toward the way to go (the sheet turns it to an example direction); the
other four carry their own blue arrow, or none. Blue is the one colour every arrow in a bubble
shares, meaning where to walk. The bubble is about twice her width, and larger than the
exclamation mark, so its icon reads at the zoom; the strip at the foot shows the two side by side.

**The question a run answers.** Does each icon say its hint at a glance with no words, "immediate
feedback especially since it doesn't need reading" (curly-moose #819), and does the bubble read as
a thought of hers and never as a warning beside the exclamation mark? In particular: is the
loudspeaker the right sign for a noise to walk wide of, does the back-to-the-route icon (the
route's sidewalk edged in the straw yellow of its tinted curbstones, with the pram standing off
it) read as her having left the route, and is the bubble the right size over her?
