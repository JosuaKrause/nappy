# grassy-beaver — Walk the loss shown · 2026-11-26

Lose a day three ways and watch what the game does before the summary comes up: walk into the
street in front of a car; stand beside a loud event until a hard fail takes you (a charging dog,
say); and let the baby cry on a day with one event beside you that charges her most. On each the
world stops on the frame of the loss, the camera eases to show you and the thing that caused it
together, holds for about a second and a half, and the summary comes up; a tap or a key skips it.
Nothing is drawn on the cause, so the framing is the only mark. The question a run answers: does
the framing alone say what did it, without text, a ring or a caret; is the pause the right length
(it runs too when the crowd, the traffic as a whole or her own running caused it, and then holds on
her); and does the view going back to her, under the summary, read as a jump. If the framing does
not read, the cause needs a mark of its own, which is a new cue and goes to the board as that
decision.
