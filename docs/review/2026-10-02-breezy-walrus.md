# breezy-walrus — Try the three ground modes on the phone · 2026-10-02

On the released page on the phone, play the same stretch three times, with `?debug=1&groundmode=1`,
then `?debug=1&groundmode=2`, then `?debug=1&groundmode=3` (each run stays off the save). Walk
somewhere new so fresh ground keeps coming into view. Does one of the three stutter less, or do
they feel the same? Mode 1 is the default. Record is
[breezy-walrus](../decisions/2026-10-02-breezy-walrus.md); the native measurements in it do not say
what the phone feels.
