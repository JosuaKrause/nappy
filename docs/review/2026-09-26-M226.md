# M226 — Which things get a warning · 2026-10-05


PR #597's table "Which rows telegraph, decided by their numbers" lists every row that came at her,
its speed, whether it comes toward her, its worst penalty and whether it now telegraphs. The player
let the build decide ("you can decide on the warnings for now. put the question into a review point
for later when the table is ready"): read the table and say which rows should change, and whether a
quarter of the meter in one pass is the right line for a heavy penalty. After the next release, also
meet the loose dog, which now comes without a badge. Record is [M226](../decisions/2026-09-26-M226.md).
