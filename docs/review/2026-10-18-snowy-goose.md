# snowy-goose — Look at the explosion's flash, shake and badge in the escape city · 2026-11-25

**What to do.** Play the escape on the desktop build (`tools/run.sh --seed 4242 --start-escape city`)
and walk along the streets until an explosion goes off, a few times. The stills and a clip of one
are in `docs/evidence/snowy-goose-explosion-badge-flash-shake-2026-11-25/` (its README says what each
frame shows).

**Where to look.** The mushroom badge at the screen edge, with its distance, a second before the
bang; the flash as it goes off (the city lit, the HUD not); the shake right after; the fainter
distant flashes between explosions; the crater standing where she walks.

**The question a run answers.** Are these right, or does one need changing: the flash brightness (0.6
for an explosion, 0.2 for a distant flash), the shake (3 px over 0.45 s), the 520 px distance at
which an explosion is set off and its 1 s badge? Should the apartment escape's own flashes get the
same limit of three a second (about 6.3% of walks there have a second with four)? Should some craters
stand from the start of the section (the escape's seals already put some there)? And is a badge for
an explosion, which is neither lethal nor moving, fine for the badge vocabulary ("the explosion badge
design", inbox #1026 in brisk-ferret, said it is liked)?
