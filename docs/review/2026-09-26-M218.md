# M218 — Watch a building burn, and walk to the burnt one · 2026-09-27

**On a day with a fire (day 3 is the first), look at the burning building, then on day 8 walk to
the burnt one.** The flames should climb the building's own facade with smoke above, the sidewalk
in front clear, and the fire truck be the thing to avoid. On day 8 the building behind the task
should itself be drawn burnt, with nothing standing on the sidewalk in front of it. Does the
building read as burning, and then as burnt?
