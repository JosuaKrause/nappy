# M109 — Watch the comic dogs walk · 2026-09-27

**On day 2 or later, watch a dog walker's dog and a loose dog walk, and on day 3 the dog that comes
after her.** The normal dog should step, rest, step with the other legs, rest, changing picture as
its walker does, with the side view's lighter hind leg joined to the body without a black line; the
pursuing dog should gallop on its two pictures. Do they read as walking at game size, and what are
the further comments on the pictures?
