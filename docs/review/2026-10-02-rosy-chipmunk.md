# rosy-chipmunk — Does saving work on the iPhone that lost its saves, and does the symbol say so when it does not · 2026-10-02


**Does the save symbol show what the browser actually kept, on the iPhone that lost its saves and
on a desktop browser?** Once the release carrying rosy-chipmunk is out, ask the person who
reported it to play a day on the iPhone in Safari, switch to another app for a few minutes, come
back, finish a day and start the next, then close Safari and open the page again. Bottom right,
the floppy disk should stay up while the browser is being asked and then fade; if it shows struck
through, the browser did not keep the save, and the question is at which moment. A struck symbol
on the very first save says the browser refuses storage, which is Safari's *Block All Cookies*
setting or a Screen Time restriction; a struck symbol only after the app switch says the connection
was lost and the retry did not recover it. Ask too whether they open the game from a Home Screen
icon or a Safari tab, since the two keep separate saves. On a desktop browser, the symbol should
show for each save, fully for at least a second and then fading over a second and a half, never
struck. It shows for deleting the save too: let a day lose the last nerve and see the symbol come
up on the ending, then hold the restart on the pause screen and see it stay up through the reload
onto the title; a second restart straight after, with no save left, shows nothing. Reopen a game
closed between days and none shows over the title, while a game closed mid-day shows it once.
