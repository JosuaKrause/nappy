# calm-pelican — The camera at the city's edge · 2026-10-10

The camera no longer stops at the city's edge: it stays centred on her, and the land past the edge
is painted for any view. That is what the player asked for in
[olive-hedgehog](../playtests/2026-10-05-olive-hedgehog.md): "also the camera during normal gameplay
shouldn't stop -- it should keep the player centered", recorded in
[M204](../decisions/2026-09-25-M204.md) as "The city camera has no limits"; PR #580 (the trailer,
its camera starting at the player) built it. Walk to the east edge, and to the south edge where the
spine meets the bridge, on a phone and on a computer: does the painted land past the edge read as
part of the world, and does anything look wrong there (the fence, the water, the cars on the deck)?
The two stills, at the east edge and the bridge, are in
[calm-pelican-camera-edge-2026-10-10](../evidence/calm-pelican-camera-edge-2026-10-10/README.md);
the developer readout and the day-1 controls hint are drawn over both. The record is
[calm-pelican-10](../decisions/2026-10-10-calm-pelican-10.md).
