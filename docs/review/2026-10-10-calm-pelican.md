# calm-pelican — The camera at the city's edge · 2026-10-10

The camera no longer stops at the city's edge: it stays centred on her, and the land past the edge
is painted for any view. PR #580 (the trailer, its camera starting at the player) made that change
for the whole game without describing or showing it. Walk to the east edge and onto the bridge, on
a phone and on a computer: does the painted land past the edge read as part of the world, and is a
camera that keeps her centred there better than one that stops at the edge? The two stills, at the
east edge and the bridge, are in
[calm-pelican-camera-edge-2026-10-10](../evidence/calm-pelican-camera-edge-2026-10-10/README.md);
the developer readout and the day-1 controls hint are drawn over both. The record is
