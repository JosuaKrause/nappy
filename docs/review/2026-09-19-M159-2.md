# M159 — Record a stutter on the phone and send the file · 2026-10-04

Once the next release is out, open the released page on the phone with
`?debug=1&framerecord=1&groundmode=2&seed=67` and play the same stretch as before: seed 67, one block to the
right, up through the car accident and between the stalls, and back down. Play at least once more
with `groundmode=1` on the same route. When a stutter is felt, look at the readout's `slow` line,
which should name that frame's largest costs, then tap `save frames` at the top of the page and send
the downloaded file over. The questions: does the download work on the phone's browser, and does the `slow` line catch the
stutters you feel? The files then answer, for whoever reads them, whether mode 2's slow frames are
ones where the scenery queue was short and another system was long. Record is
[M159, every frame's time by system](../decisions/2026-09-19-M159-5.md).
