and from behind: she should stop against it, never walk through, and never be pinned. Then stand
in the road ahead of one driving at her: the exclamation mark should go up over her with time to
step onto the sidewalk, and staying put should end the day. Does the car's danger read as the
front of a moving car and nothing else? (The car that comes over to investigate her on a hot run
is sandy-ferret's to change: it will stop on the street and a policeman will get out.) The build is
recorded in [sunny-finch](../decisions/2026-10-07-sunny-finch.md).
