# downy-llama — Does the open roadblock read as pass on the far side in play · 2026-11-16

Play day 8 on the desktop build (`tools/run.sh --seed 4242 --day 8`): a roadblock stands beside her
doorstep, a few steps from where the day starts, which is the case the pictures show. Then play a
day from 7 on with any seed and walk past a roadblock on a street her route runs along. The
roadblock is the open kind now (inbox #1465 in spry-plover; inbox #1491): its concrete band stands
on the kerb, covering its own sidewalk and the road, with a guard posted on each side of it, and the
far sidewalk is left whole, 64px, the one way past. Once four resistance tasks are done (heat 4),
the guard on her side leaves his post and comes at her if she lingers inside his radius.

Watch whether the band, its two guards and the open far sidewalk read at a glance as "cross over
and pass on the far side", whether the band looks like it stands on the kerb rather than in the
road, and whether passing on the far sidewalk under the guard's eye feels like a route choice
rather than a squeeze. The before and after pictures are in
[the evidence folder](../evidence/downy-llama-roadblock-band-2026-11-16/README.md).
