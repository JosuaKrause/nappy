across the road there, close together, and the road between them is shut to her; the pavements
either side of the row stay open. Is the road between the street and the precinct closed the way you
meant? Can you still get in comfortably by the pavements either side? Does the crowd walking through
the posts, which have no bodies for them, bother you? Does the day's route line go round by the
pavement rather than through the posts? The build is recorded in
