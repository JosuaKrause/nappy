# polite-tapir — A sent car turns a corner and drives on · 2026-11-16

Play a day from day 4 on the desktop build with the police patrol forced
(`tools/run.sh --seed 4242 --day 8 --force police_patrol 6`), and walk along a street. When the
patrol car the director sends toward you has passed you, turn and walk after it. Do the same on day
13 with the army truck of the convoy (`--day 13 --force military_convoy 6`): its screen-edge badge
goes up, the truck comes toward you down the road and passes, and you walk after it. The truck
stays loud for as long as you can see it, so walking after it costs: measured on open ground with
the meter starting empty, a truck met walking on against it costs 30 to 32 points, one you turn to
walk after once it has passed 94 to 102, and one you turn away from at its badge and walk ahead of,
the truck coming up behind you, 226 to 240 over 21 seconds, more than the meter's 100 points and the
9.5 past them that end the day crying. Stepping off its street stops the cost. Each car drives on
for as long as you can see it, from junction box to junction box, and where the street past the
next box runs into a pedestrian precinct, a closed street, a park or the city's edge, it turns the
corner in that box onto the crossing street, the way that leads away from you where that street
goes on, instead of driving over the paving or stopping there. A patrol whose turn would put you in
front of it, with you standing on the street it turns into, waits at the corner until you step off
it.

Watch whether the turn reads as a car driving on round the corner, or as something sliding or
snapping round, and whether a patrol waiting at a corner for you reads as a car giving way, and
whether a convoy truck you follow being loud the whole time reads as your choice to follow it. The
pictures of a patrol turning and of a convoy truck driving on and turning, most of them from a
camera that follows the car rather than hers, are in
[the evidence folder](../evidence/polite-tapir-way-on-turns-2026-11-16/README.md). Does a sent car
turning a corner read as a car driving on?
