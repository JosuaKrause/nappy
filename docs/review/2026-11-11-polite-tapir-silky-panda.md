# polite-tapir — A sent car turns a corner and drives on · 2026-11-16

Play a day from day 4 on the desktop build with the police patrol forced
(`tools/run.sh --seed 4242 --day 8 --force police_patrol 6`), and walk along a street. When the
patrol car the director sends toward you has passed you, turn and walk after it. Do the same on day
13 with the army truck of the convoy (`--day 13 --force military_convoy 6`): its screen-edge badge
goes up, the truck comes toward you down the road and passes, and you walk after it. Each car now
drives on for as long as you can see it, from junction box to junction box, and where the street
past the next box runs into a pedestrian precinct, a closed street, a park or the city's edge, it
turns the corner in that box onto the crossing street, the way that leads away from you where that
street goes on, instead of driving over the paving or stopping there. No event car turned a corner in your view before this.
A patrol whose turn would put you in front of it, with you standing on the street it turns into,
waits at the corner until you step off it.

Watch whether the turn reads as a car driving on round the corner, or as something sliding or
snapping round, and whether a patrol waiting at a corner for you reads as a car giving way. The
pictures of a patrol turning and of a convoy truck driving on, most of them from a camera that
follows the car rather than hers, are in
[the evidence folder](../evidence/polite-tapir-way-on-turns-2026-11-16/README.md). Does a sent car
turning a corner read as a car driving on?
