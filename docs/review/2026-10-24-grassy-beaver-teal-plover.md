# grassy-beaver — Walk the opening shot · 2026-11-26

Start a new run and press the title's button. The view cuts to the close park, rests a second, follows
the way home along the street the first lessons are on at one pace for about five seconds, and ends at
her door, where the line *Tap to walk, double tap to run* appears and she walks; a tap or a key skips
it at any point. Play it a few times (the player's own words: a few test runs to see how it feels) and
once with the tap at once. The question a run answers: is the shot kept. Does the park read as where she
is going, does the way back teach the route, is a second on the park and five along the way the right
length, and is the street at her door free of cars when the view arrives (a car that drove in the crowd's
box the whole way is still there; it was none in the one burst). A retry of day 1, and a resumed save,
start on her with no shot. The shot is one class and its wiring in `main`, so dropping it is cheap.
