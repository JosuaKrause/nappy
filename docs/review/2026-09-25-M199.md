# M199 — Walk past a closing roadblock · 2026-11-25

Play from day 7 and look for the two kinds of roadblock on streets beside your route. The open one
stands on its own side of the street and leaves the far sidewalk free to walk past; the closing one
stands on the middle of the street and its barrier runs wall to wall, both sidewalks included. The
question a run answers: does the closing one read as a closed street you detour round (and never
one you meet on your own route), and are its two guards, who stand in the road either side of the
barrier at the street's middle, where you expect them, or should they stand on the sidewalks as
the region wall's do.
