# M199 — Walk past a closing roadblock · 2026-11-25

Play from day 7 and look for the two kinds of roadblock. The open one stands on its own side of the
street, a striped concrete barrier that leaves the far sidewalk free to walk past; the closing one
stands on the middle of a street beside or off your route, a lower barrier with a coil of razor
wire along its top and red and cream blocks on its face, running wall to wall, both sidewalks
included. Coming from out of sight, its badge at the screen's edge is one block under a row of wire
loops, where the open roadblock's badge is flat-topped. The question a run answers: does the
closing one read as a closed street you detour round, never one you meet on your own route, does
its badge tell you which kind is coming before you see it, and are its two guards, who stand in the
road either side of the barrier at the street's middle, where you expect them, or should four stand
on the sidewalks as the region wall's do.
