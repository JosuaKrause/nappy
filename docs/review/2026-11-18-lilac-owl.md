# lilac-owl — The posted guard steps round his band and follows her round the corner · 2026-11-18

**What to do.** Play the escape on the desktop build (`tools/run.sh --seed 77 --start-escape`) to a
roadblock with a guard posted at its barrier. Walk straight past his post without luring him, on a long
street and on one that ends soon after the band, turning the corner there as he charges. Then lure him
off his post once and walk past.

**Where to look.** The guard as he charges. He now steps round the end of his own concrete band at
once, where before he slid along it for about a second, so a straight walker is caught sooner, about
260px past the band rather than about 400px. Round an ordinary corner he catches her as before; the
difference there shows only where a building as narrow as two tiles stands between her street and the
next way on, where before he was held behind it.

**The question a run answers.** Does his charge read as a guard running after her, round his band and
round the corner, and does being caught sooner on a straight walk still leave luring him away as the
way past ("it should be in a way that luring away the guard is enough", inbox #771 in minty-lemur)?
