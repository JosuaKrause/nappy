and the [short runtime clip](../evidence/pelican-diagonals-2026-10-07/runtime.mp4). In the

read as having two eyes, and do both diagonals' legs pedal naturally through both phases?

and partly hidden far wing are choices to judge. The implementation and evidence limits are
recorded in [spotted-osprey, pelican diagonal anatomy](../decisions/2026-10-07-spotted-osprey.md).
