Play an ordinary city day in the browser and on the phone. The starting view should be filled
around home. Walk and run along the offered routes, reverse direction several times, go far
enough away to unload the area, then return. Look for blank ground, building or prop pop-in,
seams in shadows or water, and a hitch when new scenery comes into view. Brief backtracking
near the loading boundary should keep the same scenery resident.

Continue to the next day after finishing away from home and revisit a distant changed block.
Its ground, windows, posters, street-tree pits and litter should reflect the current day.
Where the run reaches them, check the home seal, blackout and transfer into the finale city.
Turn the phone or resize the browser while paused and after resuming; the visible view should
stay filled. The question is whether nearby-only preparation and wider-boundary unloading
preserve the picture and feel smooth on these devices. Native automated checks do not settle
browser/GPU memory or perceived smoothness.
