# `--route` from the building section

The orchestrating session's carry-over from events-composed (minty-quail-26), not the player's ask.
A run booted into the escape's building section with `--route` waits at the city's brief, since
nobody presses its continue (`src/main.gd`, the comment over the building's boot); the route rig
(`src/dev/route_rig.gd`) walks the city only. **Build:** the route rig carries a building-section run
on into the city section, so `--route` can follow a run from the building to an exit, through the run
flow's own continue rather than a branch in `main`.
