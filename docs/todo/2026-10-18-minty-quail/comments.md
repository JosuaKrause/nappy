# Comments state the rule, not its history

Comment lines are 44 to 60 percent of the events files still to do (`event_catalogue.gd` 1,867 of
3,112, `event_def.gd` 1,193 of 1,854), with citations of inbox numbers, quotes, playtests, dates or
PRs, history phrasing ("PR #781's review 3", "any more", "the review finding this exists to fix") and
30-50 line doc blocks arguing the history. That is against `CLAUDE.md`'s present-tense rule.

**Build:** in each file a PR touches, state the rule in a sentence or two with one citation, and move
the history to the decision records.
