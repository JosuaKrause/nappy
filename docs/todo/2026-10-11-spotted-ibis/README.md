# spotted-ibis — Day 8's burnt-building door can be out of her reach · filed 2026-10-11


Found while building [teal-moose](../../decisions/2026-10-07-teal-moose.md), not reported by the
player. Day 8's task is at the door of the building day 3's fire burned, when one is remembered,
otherwise a fallback burnt building; the arrow, the contact and the touch are the door point. On a
sweep of 93 fronts on seeds 1, 2 and 3, the door point is used on 79. On the other 14 no tile within
reach of the door is one she can stand on and reach from home (the door shut off from home, or
bodies over all of it), so the contact falls back to the facade behind the shell:

- On 12 the shell's own frontage cannot be reached from home either, so the task cannot be done at
  all that day. The day goes on — "a task she skips is answered by somebody else"
  (`docs/NARRATIVE.md`) — and day 8 is not one of the three days whose target
  [M181](../../decisions/2026-09-24-M181-the-narrow-targets-are-reachable-by-construction.md)
  guarantees reachable.
- On 2 (seed 3, fronts (76,112) and (151,112)) the task can be done, but its arrow is 128px and 16px
  off the door: the azure-koala symptom teal-moose answered, still there in these cases.

The work is [reach-the-burnt-building.md](reach-the-burnt-building.md).
