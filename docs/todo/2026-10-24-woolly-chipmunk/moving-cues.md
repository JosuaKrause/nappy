# Something moving toward the park at each junction

**What:** the orchestrator's proposal from the research (a thing that starts moving catches the eye;
landmarks at junctions cut navigation errors): petals or leaves drifting toward the park, birds
lifting off in its direction, at the junctions of day 1's route.

**Not decided:** the player answered the signs and the bubbles but not this; it was left out of the
summary the player agreed to, so it is neither built nor rejected.

**Would come back** as a question, beside the junction signs, if signs used sparingly leave players
lost at a junction.
