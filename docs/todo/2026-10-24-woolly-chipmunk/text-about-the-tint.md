**What:** a commenter on the player's Reddit post suggested, in rough summary, a short first-day
line of text that teaches the player to notice the yellow-tinted safe sidewalks without a quest
arrow; the orchestrator offered to file it.

**Set aside:** "I agree that we need a better day one experience but I don't think the "warm
pavement" line is it", and "I deliberately don't focus on the pavement since the pavement itself is
an attempt to patch up issues from getting lost" (curly-moose #819).

**Would come back** only if the tint is kept as a teaching tool, which the player's reading of it
argues against.
