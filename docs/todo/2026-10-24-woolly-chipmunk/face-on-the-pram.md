# A face instead of, or beside, the two bars

**What:** the player's toolbox idea, "we can get rid of the two bars and replace them with a more
obvious indicator (eg diegetic face)" (curly-moose #819); the orchestrator then proposed keeping the
bars and adding a bigger reacting face over the pram, and the research favoured that pairing (players
read a continuous value better from a HUD; a HUD icon paired with an in-world display did best).

**Set aside:** "I don't want to add the face yet especially if also keeping the bars. I don't want
the UI to be cluttered and also take away more visual space" (curly-moose #819). "Yet": it is not
ruled out.

**Would come back** if new players still misread how wound up or how sleepy she is after
grassy-beaver ships (its milestone counts), or if the bars themselves go.
