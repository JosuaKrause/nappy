# The scheduler's suite tests difficulty on her route

`tests/test_events_scheduler.gd` holds `_test_a_day_has_enough_in_it_to_meet`, whose second half checks that day 14 places more than a fifth over day 1 (`late * 5 > early * 6`) on city 4242. That is a bar on the count of events, and the record silver-ferret measured it holding on 4 of 10 cities. The check is replaced with two claims about the events on her route, the streets of every route of the day's route tree: day 14's events there cost more to pass than day 1's (priced by the cost table in `docs/COSTS.md`, one pass at walking pace), and a larger share of them end the day or chase her than on day 1. **At what heat the share is measured:** the scheduler's suite plans its days at heat 0, where the share on her route is near zero in every act (0.7% in act IV, 0.0% to 0.4% before it), so a share claim is only meaningful with her heat at the lethal rung, where act IV's is 10.3% against act I's 0.0%. The filer's proposal is to measure the share at the lethal rung, and to drop the share claim and keep the price claim alone if the suite cannot set her heat through the game's own seam.

The first half of the check (days 1, 3, 7 and 14 each place about one event a block) stays; it is a different claim. **The count floor stays too**, as the record proposes: the last day places no fewer than the first, replacing the fifth-more bar (the filer's proposal, the record's). The new test reaches the route and the cost table through the game's own seams (**writing-tests**), and its bars are measured over several cities before they are set, never derived (**balance**). A bar the cities do not meet is reported to the orchestrating session, which asks the player on the board; it is never loosened to pass. The code PR deletes this item and files its record.
