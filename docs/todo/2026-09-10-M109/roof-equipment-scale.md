finds the standalone pipes, vents and ventilators too large. The roof access room
instead needs to be bigger: compare its door to a regular building door. The water
tank looks fine and keeps its current size. Correct these relative drawing scales
inside the existing roof PR, preserving the accepted art, connected ducts, attached
feet, cardinal perspective and the roof-foot depth anchors.

The skylights' scale is also acceptable. Keep it, and fix the long skylight's cut-off
left edge. Inspect its source, extraction and runtime placement to identify where
the edge is lost; preserve the approved drawing while restoring its complete outline.

**Proposed, not asked for:** keep connected air ducts at their current scale, reduce
the standalone pipe/vent/fan assemblies together with any separately drawn rotors,
and enlarge the access room using a regular door as the scale reference. Judge the
proportions in an early native engine still showing those references beside the
equipment and unchanged water tank. Choose coherent factors from those references
and report them as open to correction. Show both skylight sizes and the repaired
long left edge. Keep
other appliance families and gameplay geometry unchanged unless this specific
proportion correction requires otherwise; show every affected family in context.

The [network correction record](../../decisions/2026-09-10-M109-4.md) carries the
accepted source artwork, support and lifecycle contracts. Do not regenerate art
