# Fable looks over the designs Opus wrote in its place

**What it looks at.** polite-tapir's six merged designs have Fable's verdicts and their changes are filed (the
record calm-lemur). Every other piece of work whose design an Opus agent wrote in Fable's place while
Fable's quota was out, and which merged without Fable's review (inbox #1571 in teal-quail):

- minty-quail's (the refactor by layer) rows 3a and 3b of `event-interface.md`, the events'
  interface, whose designs Opus wrote and whose pull requests merge one by one;
- any further design Opus writes in Fable's place before the quota returns, each named here by the
  queue move of the pull request that builds it.

**What is done when the quota is back.** A Fable agent is briefed per design, oldest first, with the
design review or queue item the work answered, the design as Opus wrote it, and the record of what
was built. It judges whether the design is the one it would have written, and writes any change it
wants as a design (inbox #1501 and #1504 in silky-lemur). Each change is filed as an entry of its own
and built in its band's turn, like any other work; nothing that merged is held or reverted for it.

**When the entry closes.** When every design listed here has Fable's verdict and each change it asked
for is filed.
