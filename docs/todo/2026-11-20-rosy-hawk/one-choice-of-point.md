# Where a plan's body stands is chosen once

The plan answers where its body stands (or `BodyStance` gains a static over a plan), and the walk's ground, the route
rig's planner and the plan builder ask it, so the choice of point is written once. Nothing a player sees changes. It
starts after curly-stork's pull request, which owns two of the three files, and is checked against curly-stork's
design (finding 9.3, F3: the point a plan's pieces are built at is where the plan was placed).
