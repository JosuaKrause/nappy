**Cache event-shape classification where its inputs are immutable.**

The player asks to queue worthwhile next optimization directions after the scenery work.
The [latest native comparison](../../evidence/m159-scenery-animation-2026-09-26/native/README.md)
and its per-function summaries identify repeated `has_a_spread()` classification as a remaining
CPU cost. The source request is [Sunny lynx](../../playtests/2026-09-26-sunny-lynx.md).

**Proposed, not yet a chosen implementation:** derive the classification once from authored
event data and reuse it. First audit every input and every write to it; do not assume an event
definition is immutable merely because most callers treat it that way. Preserve classification
for every look, body geometry and phase, and invalidate or recompute where data really changes.

Keep this separate from sharing whole future-threat predictions. Require equivalence across the
catalogue and relevant state transitions, plus repeated controlled before/after measurements of
classification work and complete frame distributions with profiler-disabled companions. Preserve
warnings, selection, halos, collisions and meter contributions. A cheaper helper is a measured
work reduction; it does not by itself establish smoother frames or phone performance.
