# Recipes as named layers, and several takes of what repeats

From chirp's layered sounds and its habits (`chirp/SKILL.md`, "Habits"; the footsteps in
`chirp/showcase/forest_kit.cx.js`; the loudest-layers report in `chirp/src/node/feedback.ts`).

**Build:** a recipe is a set of named layers with their own levels, so "the ticks are too loud" is
one level to change and to report; an impact layers a tonal body under a noise transient; anything
heard over and over (steps, wheels) gets several seeded takes. Seeds stay per recipe label as now,
never by position in a file. Whether the game picks among takes at runtime (Godot's
`AudioStreamRandomizer`, if the release engine keeps it) is the builder's to check and state.
