# A resonant filter, and pink and brown noise

From chirp's synthesis (`chirp/src/core/synth.ts`: its biquad and noise colours).

**Build:** an RBJ-cookbook biquad (low-, high- and band-pass with Q, cutoff sweeps) and pink and
brown noise in `tools/synthesize-sfx.py`, beside the one-pole lowpass and white noise it has. It bears
on the player's sound-lab verdicts that the stroller wheels sounded like ocean waves and the steps
were too heavy (the Copper lark records under `docs/decisions/`).
