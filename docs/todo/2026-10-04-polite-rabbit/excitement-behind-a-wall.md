**Excitement does not go through any wall, for every source** (see the README). A source's
contribution reaches her only when the line between them does not cross a building past the middle
of its wall (one tile deep): her body overlapping a wall's edge, or a line grazing a corner, shields
nothing. Unlike the catch, which needs a fully clear line, this is the deeper test. Measure what it
does to route costs with `tools/cost-table.sh` under the **balance** rules, keep the halos, carets
and the meter consistent with what she now receives, and keep its per-frame cost in check since it
runs for every candidate source.
