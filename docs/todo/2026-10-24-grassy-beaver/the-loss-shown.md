# A loss shows what caused it

The player: "showing what causes a loss is also a good idea -- show not tell" (curly-moose #819).

**Build:** when a day is lost, the game shows the thing that caused it (the car that hit her, the
noise source that made the baby cry) before the summary or the retry, without text.

*Proposed, not asked for:* the camera holds on the cause for a moment, the cause outlined with the
existing rim, from the research's finding that players who see their own mistake rate a game
higher than players who blame the game.
