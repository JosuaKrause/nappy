# Day 1 starts with no cars spawning, and a bubble for the zebra crossings

The player, "for when we work on the beginning": "in the title screen we have the normal density we
have today. then on the first day we show the park and then go back to home. when showing the park,
turn off car spawning so when the camera comes back home no cars are around anymore. this should
prevent the most common day 1 mistake which (I think) is figuring out the controls and getting hit by
a car. the first few blocks shouldn't have cars spawning in and only a thought bubble advising to use
the zebra crossings. that way a player can get used to the controls before cars start spawning"
(minty-dolphin #867). Asked when the bubble shows: "when the player steps on the road", and, read
back as away from a crossing, "yyes away from the crossing" (minty-dolphin #867).

**Build:** the title screen keeps today's traffic density. On day 1, while the opening shot
(`opening-shot.md`) shows the park, no new cars spawn, so the cars there drive off and none are near
her door when the camera comes back. For her first few blocks no cars spawn in. The zebra-crossing
thought bubble (`thought-bubbles.md`) shows when she steps onto the road away from a zebra crossing.
After the first few blocks cars spawn as normal.

**Read as:** this answers day 1's car losses, which `day-two.md` reads as players running across the
street (curly-moose #832), by letting a new player learn the controls before any car can reach her.

**Open:** what ends the car-free stretch (a distance, a number of blocks, her first crossing, or a
time), and whether it holds on a retry of day 1. *Proposed, not asked for:* a retry of day 1 keeps
it, since the player's reason, learning the controls, can still apply on a second attempt.
