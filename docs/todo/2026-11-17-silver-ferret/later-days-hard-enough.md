# The later days are judged hard enough on their events' difficulty and their count

**The ask:** "event density is not the only way to tweak difficulty. events themselves are also more
difficult to deal with" (silky-lemur #1464), answering whether to raise the later acts' density now
or wait for the end remeasure.

**Read as:** at the refactor's end remeasure, the later days (acts III and IV) are judged against the
early ones on two counts together: how many events a day places, and how hard those events are to
deal with. Only if the later days still read as too easy with both counted are the later acts' cells
of `PlacementDensity` raised, by declaration, as that table is meant to be edited.

**What the remeasure already has:** the event count per day, from the baseline readings on `v0.29.0`
(minty-quail's `README.md`, the remeasure) and from the record polite-tapir-coral-owl, which also
leaves open whether `test_events_costs`'s bar (day 14 a fifth over day 1) or the table is wrong, under
the **balance** skill's rule that a test pushing back asks which of the two is.

*Proposed, not asked for:* how a day's difficulty is counted. The plainest reading is the cost
table's price of one pass of each row (`docs/COSTS.md`, what meeting a row costs her), weighted by
each act's mix of rows, beside the share of a day's events whose rows end the day or hunt her, read
per act. A different measure the remeasure
proposes instead is put to the player on the board before anything is raised.
