**A standalone experiment draws the water outside the game first**: a scene of its own (or a
`tools/` script) with the blue base, the randomly placed centroids, their Voronoi outlines in white,
and each centroid's intensity oscillating. The first attempt comes back early as a short burst or
clip in the pull request, since the look is judged by eye. It is drawing work, so an Opus agent does
it. Alongside the look, it reports the effect's cost per frame at a phone-sized canvas, and the
alternative of prerendering it into tiles, so the player can choose how it enters the game.
