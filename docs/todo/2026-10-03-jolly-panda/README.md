[minty-hedgehog](../../playtests/2026-10-03-minty-hedgehog.md), statement 14 (note #451). **The waterfront has a proper edge: a guard rail or mooring
bollards, and perhaps lanterns.** `docs/CITY.md` (from the M120 record, "The map edge") says the
south is "a bulkhead and then open water … ground rather than objects, outside the map" and
"Deliberately no headland, no bay and no new terrain"; a rail, bollards or lanterns are objects on
that edge, so the PR quotes it and rewrites the sentence. Mooring bollards are not the street
`bollard` prop (`src/city/prop.gd`).

**Proposed, not asked for:** the choice between a rail and bollards shown on a review sheet; the
fittings drawn, outside the map and not solid.
