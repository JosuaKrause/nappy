# The escape ends when she enters the tunnel or crosses the bridge

The player: "the escape should end when actually entering the tunnel or crossing the bridge not when
reach that intersection" (inbox #1733 in spry-ferret).

**Build:** the escape's win fires on her entering the tunnel mouth or crossing onto the bridge, the
way out of the city, and no longer on her reaching the intersection before it. The intersection stays
part of her route; only where the run ends moves.

**Read as:** "that intersection" is the junction the escape's goal sits at before the tunnel or the
bridge, and the end is the way out itself. Which point of the tunnel (its mouth or inside it) and of the
bridge (its first tile or its far end) counts is open for the builder to settle with the escape's
day spec and show the player.
