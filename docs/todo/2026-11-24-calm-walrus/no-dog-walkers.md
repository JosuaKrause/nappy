# No dog walkers in the escape

The player: "there should be no dog walkers in escape it's not a calm night where people walk their
dogs" (inbox #1733 in spry-ferret).

**Build:** the escape's city draws no dog walker (the pedestrian the director sends ahead of her, walking
a dog on a lead), whatever the escape's bag holds today. Which marbles of the escape's bag are dog
walkers, and what replaces them, is for the builder to find in the escape's day spec; a replacement
must not make the escape emptier, since the player saw the dog walker as "the only actual obstacle
showing up".

**Proposed, not asked for:** the replacement is another obstacle from the catalogue that suits a night
of the occupation, picked by the builder and shown to the player; the plainer alternative is to
remove the marbles and leave the bag smaller, which leaves lilac-marten's fuller escape to fill it.
