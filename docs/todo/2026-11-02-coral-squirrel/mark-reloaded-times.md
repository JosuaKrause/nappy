# A resumed mid-walk day marks its shown times as inaccurate

**Build:** a save records whether it was written while a day was being walked; a run resumed from
such a save carries a flag saying its shown times are inaccurate because of a reload, and every
screen that shows a time the lost stretch makes short says so (the day's completion time for that
day, and the total game time on the ending screens). A save written at a day brief carries no flag.
Which screens and how the marking reads are open until the work starts (the entry's open question).
