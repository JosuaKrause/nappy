# tiny-alpaca — A seal across a pedestrian street closes all six lanes · filed 2026-10-11

Found by the review of [M219](../../decisions/2026-09-26-M219.md)'s pull request (#727), not
reported by the player. On a pedestrian street a soft seal, and a hard seal of two bodies, are placed
as if its middle two lanes were a carriageway, so they leave those two lanes open: on seed 67 that
is days 2 to 8 and 10 to 13, and walkers walk through the gap. Whether she can walk through it too
was not checked. A seal is what makes a sealed street closed (the
[M64 sealing record](../../decisions/2026-09-03-M64-off-the-path-is-closed-sealing.md)); one with
a hole in it is a closure that does not close, and every route guarantee the
**city** rules give is counted with it closed.

**What to find out first:** whether she can pass the gap, on one of those days, headless. Then
either the seal on a pedestrian street spans all six lanes, or, if the gap is meant (a seal the
crowd may pass and she may not), the record that says so is quoted and the entry closes with it.
A test places a seal on a pedestrian street and checks no lane is left open to whatever the seal is
meant to stop.

**The same review saw walkers drawn inside bodies** for a few frames on seed 67 days 2 and 7,
identically on `main` and on the branch; nothing says yet which bodies or why. It is filed here
because it was found the same way, not because it has the same cause: the work is finding which
bodies on those days hold a walker inside them, and either fixing it or splitting it into an entry
of its own once the cause is known.

*Proposed, not asked for:* the band `next`, and both findings as one entry.
