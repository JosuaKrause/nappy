# Playtest 118 — The calendar's order, the spare task and the walk home

2026-09-20. Answered in conversation, to the three questions M181, the resistance has a reason,
and a task is one day, left with the player in [PLAYTEST-117](PLAYTEST-117.md). No run was
played; each question was put with its options, what each costs, and a recommendation.

## The questions, as they were answered

**Whether day 9 is too full, and where the park goes.** The proposed calendar had day 9 carry
the doors between districts arriving, the task *cross a named door*, and the once-only *a park
is taken in front of her*; day 12 had no once-only happening. Offered: the park moves to day
12; both stay on day 9; the park on day 9 and the doors on day 10, which overturns "doors start
day 9"; the park moves to day 8.

> "Park moves to day 12"

1. **Day 9 is the doors and nothing else new**: they arrive, and the task is to cross a named
   one. **The park is taken on day 12.** Days 9 to 13 each carry one new thing, and day 12's
   brief line is about the park.

**Which of the nine tasks is the spare.** Nine tasks for eight task days: the note for the man
shouting, the package at a van's drop, the burnt shell, cross a named door, warn a neighbor
before the raid, silence a loudspeaker mast, the poster wall before the crew finishes, the
middle of the densest crowd, and walking into a roadblock's band. Offered: the roadblock band,
recommended as the least distinct beside a named door; the densest crowd; the van's drop; no
spare, with nine task days.

> "The densest crowd"

2. **The densest crowd is the task with no day.** *The orchestrator proposed the roadblock band
   as the spare · the player chose the densest crowd.* The roadblock band keeps a day; day 13,
   which the crowd held, takes it.

**Whether the walk home after a task owes anything.** Offered: an ordinary return; she is looked
for, with more return patrols on a task day; extra pressure only after loud tasks.

> "Ordinary return"

3. **The walk home after a task is an ordinary return.** A task counts only on a day she wins,
   so getting home is the stake, and the return leg's own patrols are what it already has.
