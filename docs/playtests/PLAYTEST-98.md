# Playtest 98 — The basement stair is gray with dark lines

**Date:** 2026-09-19

Said in conversation, about the one-tile stair down into the basement
(`assets/interior/stair_down.svg`: treads narrowing away from the viewer between two stepped
sides, with an arrow), which [PLAYTEST-85](PLAYTEST-85.md) asked for *"at the very least"*. No run
attached.

## What the player said

> "btw the triangle stairs picture that you used in the basement -- can we just replace it with a
> full gray texture with dark gray lines every x pixels. example:
>
> s -- darker gray
> g -- lighter gray"

followed by a 32 by 32 grid: one row of `s` across the full width, then three rows of `g`, eight
times over, the first `s` row at the top.

## What is asked for, as statements

1. **The basement stair's tile is a flat lighter gray with a darker gray line every four pixels**,
   each line one pixel tall and the full width of the tile, the first on the tile's top row: eight
   lines in a 32px tile, so tiles stacked in a run continue the rhythm with no seam.
2. **Nothing else is on it.** The example has no narrowing treads, no stepped sides and no arrow.

## What was not spoken to

The exact grays, and the stairwells' diagonal flights, which are a different picture.
