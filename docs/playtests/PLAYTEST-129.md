# Playtest 129 — A hut she walks into ends the guard's chase

2026-09-24. Said in conversation, answering an open question from M100 (the boom never inspects
her).

## What was put to the player

Walking under a lowered boom sends that door's guard, `door_guard`, after her (M100). If she then
walks into another checkpoint's hut, the hut holds her in place for its inspection while the guard
keeps coming, and a catch ends the day while she cannot move. Keep it (a), since she walked into a
checkpoint while wanted, or let a hold end the chase (b), which makes a hut a way to shake off a
guard?

## What the player said

> "we can try b. if she voluntarily goes to a hut the whole pursuit has been accomplished"

## The statements

1. **A hut's hold ends the door guard's chase.** When a checkpoint hut holds her for its
   inspection, a door guard chasing her stops chasing, and the catch cannot happen during the
   hold or after it.
2. **Why:** the guard chases her to have her inspected, so walking into a hut of her own accord
   does what the chase was for.
3. **"We can try"**: it is a trial, and the player judges it by playing it.
