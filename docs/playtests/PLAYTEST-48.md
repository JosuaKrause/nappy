# Playtest 48 — The north-facing signal head · 2026-09-09

One note from the player, after v0.8.1 went out.

## 1. The head that faces north shows its lamps to the south

> "the north facing traffic light shows the lights towards the south. we should only see the back
> of it. the information is fully encoded in the south facing one. let's make a proper north
> facing graphic"

Every signalled junction has four heads, each standing beside the carriageway it stops and facing
the traffic it stops. The two on the spine's own arms are both drawn with the same face-on
picture, so the head standing north of the junction, which faces north at the southbound traffic,
shows three lamps to a camera that is looking at it from the south. The player's design: that head
gets a drawing of its **back**, and carries no lamp, because the head on the other side of the
junction, facing south, already says everything the light has to say.
