# Playtest 120 — The walk home changes nothing about where the fire may go

2026-09-21. Said in conversation, in answer to a review finding on the pull request for M179, the
fire is on her way, guaranteed. No run was played.

## What was put to the player

The review of M179's first build found that nothing stops the unseen fire being sited again once
she has turned for home: she settles the baby in a park, turns back, and three seconds of walking
later the fire is moved ahead of her new heading, onto the walk home. With the engine parked at it
for the rest of the day ([PLAYTEST-119](PLAYTEST-119.md), statements 24 and 25) that is a closed
path on the return leg. Two ways out were offered: stop siting and re-siting the fire once the
return leg starts, or leave the rule alone and let the check that home stays reachable outside
both fields be the whole answer.

> "they way home shouldn't change anything here. if they managed to avoid it thus far they should
> still have to try avoid it further. only once the event has actually taken place does it become
> fixed"

## What is asked for, as statements

1. **The return leg changes nothing about the fire.** It is sited, and moved while it has not
   happened, by the same rule on the way home as on the way out. *Stopping at the start of the
   return leg, offered by the orchestrator · refused by the player.*
2. **A fire she has avoided so far is still to be avoided.** Reaching a park without meeting it
   does not retire it; it is ahead of her again on the way back, and the reaction it asks for is
   the same: take a different route.
3. **It is fixed only once it has actually taken place.** Read by the orchestrator as the line
   M179's build already draws: the first time the fire enters the world, which is where its scar
   is recorded and its block moves along its arc, and open to correction.
4. What the siting owes her stands as PLAYTEST-119's statement 25 has it, on either leg: a calm
   area and the home stay reachable without entering the fire's field or the engine's.
