# Playtest merry-stork — A torn poster's policeman ends the day; an investigating car stops and a policeman gets out

2026-10-10.

Said in a session, answering the orchestrator's questions while PR #660 (sunny-finch, the police
car is solid and lethal only in front while it drives) was in review.

## The pursuers for a torn poster and for the tasks

The player asked:

> is the pursuer for the poster ripping and some tasks still a police car or is it police men now?

Told that a torn poster's pursuit marble still sends a police car (sandy-ferret, not yet built) and
that a finished task already sends pursuers on foot (the robber, day 7's van guard, day 9's door
guard), and that sandy-ferret would need drawings:

> there is already a policeman drawing and the guardsman also exists

## The policeman's catch

The question: "a torn poster's pursuit marble will send a policeman on foot (a guardsman from day
9), instantly, from off screen, chasing for up to 30s; running shakes him off. What happens when he
catches her? The police car he replaces never ends the day. The robber, whose drawing the policeman
mirrors, does." Offered "Ends the day (Recommended)" — "Like the robber: his touch is a loss" — and
"Costs calm, chase ends". The player chose:

> Ends the day (Recommended)

## The investigating police car

The question: "on a hot run a police car stops patrolling and drives over to investigate her. As
built, that car stops against her and never strikes; only a car patrolling its route is deadly in
front. Should the investigating car be deadly in front too?" Offered "Stops, never strikes" and
"Deadly in front too". The player answered neither:

> an investigating police car should not leave the street. it should stop if close to her and a policeman should emerge and pursue her
