2026-09-25. Said in conversation, answering the open item under M181 (the resistance has a
reason, and a task is one day): where the neighbor's sealed door is seen.

## What was put to the player

it. Where should it be seen: (a) on her building's front, which she walks out of every morning
from day 11 on, or (b) on the neighbor's own door inside the building, which only the escape
would ever show? (a) was the orchestrator's pick.
