## What is asked for, as statements

1. **Where a task takes any instance, the story's one is the one she reaches.** The man
   shouting she hands the note to is the group's lookout; which of the look-alikes that is, is
   decided by how the player plays, and the others are not part of the story.
2. **The player never said the walk home after the sabotage is the easiest in the run.** "She
   walks home on the easiest ground in the run. That stands", in `TODO.md` under M102, the
   finale: out of the apartment, out of the city, is the orchestrator's sentence; the player's
   words there are "yes, the sabotage is the cause of the brutal crackdown". A review
   attributed it to the player and was corrected.
3. **The escape is not easy.** "the escape shouldn't be easy!" Read by the orchestrator as
   covering the whole of the last night — the walk home from the station under dead traffic
   lights ([PLAYTEST-119](PLAYTEST-119.md), statement 20) and the escape after it — so ease is
   no part of the good ending's reward, and open to correction. What the blackout does to the
   meter is unchanged: the masts stop, so the ambient floor is zero.
4. **A day 3 she wins with no fire met still burns.** "I agree with the fire fix." If every
   siting was refused by the end of the day, the fire is lit then, off her path, on a site the
   dawn rules accept, so the shell stands on day 4 and the scar is unconditional. Meeting it
   stays the strong guarantee.
5. **A retried day 3 walked another way meets the fire on another street, and that is the
   plan.** "days don't have memory on retries. that's fine." Nothing is asked of the player
   about it in `docs/REVIEW.md`.
6. **The story makes sense for the mother and for the father.** No row rests on which parent
   she is.
7. **More tasks may be made mandatory if the story needs it.** Offered by the player, not
   taken: under statement 8 nothing before day 14 rests on her alone, so the goal stays five of
   eight and the day 14 sabotage stays the one must.
8. **The orchestrator's adjustments are agreed.** *Proposed by the orchestrator · agreed by the
   player on 2026-09-21*:
   - **The neighbor down the hall works at the power station.** The group cannot approach a
     watched worker; a parent in the same building, out every day with a stroller, can. That is
     why it wants her.
   - **Nothing in the stroller is dangerous to lie next to.** The package is tools and a lamp:
     damning to be caught with, harmless beside the baby (`docs/NARRATIVE.md`, tone rule 2).
     She carries it home, which is where the neighbor is.
   - **The burnt shell is the dead drop** for what comes back from the neighbor: a drawing of
     the station, which door and which shift.
   - **The neighbor hid the station key at the swing before the raid**, feeling watched, so the
     swing holds whether or not she warned them.
   - **The front door is a hand-over.** She passes the key to the neighbor's colleague on the
     night shift and walks away; the minutes he needs are why the lights go out once she is at
     a distance.
   - **The group has other couriers.** What she skips somebody else does, at a higher cost.
     Day 14 has to be her: under curfew, with the army on the streets, only a parent with a
     crying baby reaches that door.
   - **She is never told the plan.** Each errand is small and deniable, and she learns what
     they were for when the windows go dark.
   - **The bombing answers the uprising**, and the blackout is the uprising's signal: it
     reaches everybody at once with nothing spoken or sent.
   - **The blackout is what silences the masts**, and day 11 is where she finds out they have
     no power of their own.
   - **The power station is a landmark she has passed since day 1**, stacks and lit windows, so
     the last night's target is a place she knows.
