# Playtest crisp-moose — Where a chalk mark may stand, and what a read mark does

2026-09-27. Said in conversation while PR #414 (the chalk mark, its robber, and the task line) was
being fixed after its review, answering three questions about where a mark may stand and what a
read mark does.

## Statements

1. **A read mark.** Asked whether a mark she has read should vanish at once or stay where she
   read it, the player answered:

   > "Stays crossed, until the day ends"

   A read mark keeps its touched picture, crossed through, where she read it, and is gone the next
   day or on a retry; no other alley shows it. → M221, velvet-plover.

2. **A mark with no path to it.** The fix agent could not reproduce a mark in a sealed alley
   (olive-koala, statement 9): every mark it placed, at dawn or relocated, was reachable from home
   with closures, seals and bodies counted. It found that most dawn marks on days 9 to 13 are
   reached only through a district door, and asked whether a mark should stand only where she can
   reach it without crossing one. The player answered:

   > "the alley must be next to a path. if there is no path to it it shouldn't appear"

   A district door always lets her through after its hold, so a door is a path and a mark behind
   one stays allowed; a mark with no path to it never appears. → downy-otter.

3. **A mark in a courtyard passage.** The fix agent had stopped placing marks in the dead-end
   passage into a courtyard, since the robber has no far end there to stand at out of her reach,
   and asked whether that was right. The player answered:

   > "robber at inner end of the courtyard is fine. I encountered it in game and it worked well
   > for me. you just have to lure the robber out first."

   Marks stand in courtyard passages too, their robber at the courtyard's inner end. → M213.
