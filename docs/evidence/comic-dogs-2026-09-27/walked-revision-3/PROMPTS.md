# Fixed-joint edit prompts

Built-in `image_gen` edits use the frozen first-pass A crop as the edit target for identity,
torso, projection and joint placement. The approved style references are inspected for style;
the existing SVG pair is inspected for the fixed-body animation contract. Neither rejected B
generation is used as a drawing reference. The exact prompts below record each call.

Preserve its head, face, ears, collar, tail, back, belly, torso proportions, torso position and all colors exactly. Keep the same camera. All four leg attachments stay at the identical anatomical positions relative to the torso. Do not slide legs along the belly. The near hind hip is inside the rear haunch at approximately (82,123) on this 320x219 reference; the far hind hip is behind that haunch at (104,128). The near shoulder is inside the chest at (201,118), the far shoulder behind it at (218,116). These four joint positions are FIXED. Change only the articulated limbs below those joints, with continuous hip-to-knee-to-hock-to-paw and shoulder-to-elbow-to-wrist-to-paw anatomy. Near hind leg swings forward with its paw centered near (120,199); far hind leg swings back with its paw centered near (55,188). Near foreleg swings back with its paw near (173,200); far foreleg swings forward with paw near (260,190). The near hind thigh must angle forward from its original hip, not begin under the middle of the belly. Maintain four separately owned continuous legs with naturally dark far legs and tan near legs. Lift the far paws slightly; keep near paws at the same ground height. No extra legs, detached paws, shifted joints, body movement, floor or cast shadow. Output one dog only, no text or guide marks, real transparent background, same tight composition and proportions as the reference. This is animation art: unchanged body registration is crucial.
