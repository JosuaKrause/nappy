# M181, the resistance has a reason, and a task is one day — slice two

Captures on `feature/m181-slice-two`, 1280×720, each with `--no-save --invincible` so the day clock
and the meter stand still while the capture waits. The developer readout is on in each.

**The neighbor's art** — `neighbor-family-sheet.png`, the figure's five views and their
feet-passing frames, and `wanted-notice-neighbor-slot.png`, the wanted notice's `neighbor_slot`
wearing the same cap.

**The neighbor leaving for work** — `neighbor-leaves-for-work-day2.png`, from
`tools/shot.sh … 2 --seed 4242 --day 2`. She stands at her own door; the figure in steel-blue
coveralls and a dark cap, walking off along her street to the right of her, is the neighbor (the
readout's `nearest` line names it, 140px away). Nothing points at it.

**The raid waiting at her building** — `raid-at-her-building-day10.png`, from
`--seed 4242 --day 10 --spawn park --route home`, 17.5 seconds in. The raid came while she was out
of sight of her door; she is walking home across her own street, and the two raid vans stand on
the far sidewalk either side of her door with the patrol car pacing the carriageway. Her own
sidewalk, up to the doorstep at the top of the frame, is open. The route passed day 10's mark on
the way, so the teaching line carries its words.

**A mast before and after she silences it** — `mast-live-day11.png` and `mast-silenced-day11.png`,
from `--seed 90210 --day 11 --zoom 0.6 --route mark,task,home`, 36 and 39 seconds in. Seed 90210
rather than 4242 because the route rig sticks on day 11's mark on 4242 (M184). Before: she is
at the mast's foot, its lamp green and its arcs up, the red arrow beside her. After: she has
walked on; the mast at the top of the frame stands with no lamp and no arcs, and the red arrow is
gone.

**Day 12's park closing in front of her** — `park-at-the-swing-day12.png` and
`park-closing-day12.png`, from `--seed 4242 --day 12 --zoom 0.6 --route mark,task,home`, 50 and
53 seconds in. At the swing, with the red arrow on it; three seconds after she reached it, the
park's outer ring is mud and the middle is still grass. It is all mud nine seconds later.

**Day 13's column** — `column-on-the-main-road-day13.png`, from
`--seed 4242 --day 13 --spawn signal`, 114 seconds in. She stands at a spine junction without
walking, so the column comes at `Tuning.COLUMN_BY`, southbound: two of its three army trucks are
in view in one lane of the main road on the left of the frame.
