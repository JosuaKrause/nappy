# jolly-owl: the zzz walking south, before and after

Stills of the sleeping baby's zzz in each of the eight facings (`before-<facing>.png`,
`after-<facing>.png`; `n`, `ne`, `e`, `se`, `s`, `sw`, `w`, `nw`) and a phone-portrait still of due
south (`before-portrait-s.png`, `after-portrait-s.png`, the rotated presentation, so the screen's
left is the game's south). Captured with `tools/shot.sh --no-save --seed 4242 --meters 100 0
--zoom 2 --walk 0.4@<bearing>@` (landscape, 640x360) and `RESOLUTION=720x1280 ... --touch`
(portrait). Only due south differs between before and after; the other facings are the same
frames. In the landscape due-south still the camera's look-ahead puts her at the top edge, so the
cue is clipped; the portrait still shows it.
