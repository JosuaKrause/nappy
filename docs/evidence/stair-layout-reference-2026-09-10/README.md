# Sideways switchback stair references

These two generated illustrations are supplied by the player in
[PLAYTEST-53](../../playtests/PLAYTEST-53.md#sideways-switchback-stairs). They are preserved as
the original JPEG bytes. Their role is stair construction and orientation: flights run sideways
across the view and reverse lateral direction at landings. They do not prescribe the game's
camera, illustrated finish, characters, lettering or city-hall setting.

- [Interior switchback](interior-switchback.jpeg), supplied as
  `Gemini_Generated_Image_5fyv1c5fyv1c5fyv.jpeg`: alternating lateral flights and landings.
- [Exterior fire escape](exterior-fire-escape.jpeg), supplied as
  `Gemini_Generated_Image_63n59h63n59h63n5.jpeg`: flights, platforms and rails parallel to the facade.

M102, the finale, has exactly two stairwells in the building: one on the left and one on the
right. Each stairwell contains its own switchback flights; the pair does not mean one stair
in front and one behind. M106, roofs, fronts and street trees, uses the same side-on construction
principle for its fire-escape overlays. These are reference images, not runtime textures.
