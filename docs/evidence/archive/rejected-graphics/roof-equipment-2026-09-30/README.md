# Rejected roof equipment PNGs

These eight installed PNG transfers were rejected in the gray-otter playtest: they did not read
as a coherent, grounded rooftop family; the ducts floated without roof supports and the water
tank was open instead of carrying its closed conical roof. They are retained here as exact
historical artifacts and are not generation references.
