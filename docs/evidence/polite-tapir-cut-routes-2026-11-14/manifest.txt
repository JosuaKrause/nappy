Evidence for PR #1462 (polite-tapir, units at dawn, placement step 2 of 4): the cut routes.

Claim: on this branch a drawn dog walker's and police patrol's route is cut to the street's run
(the branch's `Location.run()`, with `DawnDraw.ROUTES_STAY_ON_THE_RUN`), so its stretch is shorter
than on main. The purple line in each picture is the day's route (debug layer 5, `--layers 5`).

Limits: a route's length is read from the picture only roughly; the burst frames show a walker
moving along it, not its exact length. The two builds draw different days (their plans differ, see
the counts below), so "same day" means the same seed and day number, not the same events. The
police patrol follow frames (day4-police-patrol-follow-*) do not clearly show a patrol on the route;
the overview pictures are the route evidence for day 4. A burst is a rig walk with `--invincible`,
so no real run's timing is shown.

Source revision: branch feature-polite-tapir-units-at-dawn at 97490b92e (PR #1462 head); main at
da1daf08d (origin/main when captured, detached checkout).
