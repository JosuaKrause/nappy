# The robber's two side alerts, side by side · 2026-10-17

One row, the alley robber seen from the side, at game scale (top left) and six times larger. Left
to right: **waiting** (`art/events/robber_waiting_side.svg`), **alert A**, the one approved on PR
#726 (`art/events/robber_alert_side_b.svg`), **alert B**, the bolder one
(`art/events/robber_alert_side.svg`, drawn first as
`docs/evidence/teal-trout-views-2026-10-17/robber_alert_side-v2.svg`), and the **lunge**
(`art/events/robber_lunging_side.svg`). It answers the open question in
[teal-trout-3](../../decisions/2026-10-11-teal-trout-3.md): which side alert is the robber's.

Rendered from `robber-side-choice-v1.json` with the second sheet's
`docs/evidence/teal-trout-views-2026-10-17/render-sheet.gd` (its README gives the command), Godot
4.7's own SVG parser, at commit a87a0a05. The recipe names the paths the two alerts had at that commit, so it is
rerun there. The player read A and B as the two frames of his walk while he warns
([teal-trout-robber-walk-2026-10-17](../teal-trout-robber-walk-2026-10-17/README.md)).
